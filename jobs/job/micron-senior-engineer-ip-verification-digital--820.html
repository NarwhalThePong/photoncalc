Senior Engineer IP Verification - Digital Design Verification

Responsibilities will include, but are not limited to:

  • Understand design specifications and define verification scope, strategy, and plans for block/chip RTL verification
  • Develop, execute, and maintain verification testbenches and test vectors using digital simulation tools and methodologies (SV-UVM)
  • Run directed and constrained-random tests and regressions; analyze results and triage failures.
  • Close verification metrics (functional coverage / code coverage as applicable) and identify verification holes to demonstrate readiness toward tapeout
  • Provide verification support to design projects by simulating, analyzing, and debugging block level RTL. Develop Test cases/Stimulus to increase the functional coverage for all DRAM architectures and features.
  • Develop and maintain test benches and test vectors using simulation tools and run regressions for coverage analysis and improvements. Co-work with international colleagues on developing new verification flows to take on the challenges in DRAM and emerging memory design.
  • Debug RTL / testbench / environment issues and partner with design engineers to identify root cause, propose fixes, and prevent recurrence.
  • Provide verification status and summaries to stakeholders; communicate risk clearly and drive action closure.
  • Good understanding of digital circuits and experience in RTL Verification. Understand the usage of tools like Virtuoso, Xcellium, Simvision, Verdi/Verisium.
  • Hands on experience in building SV, UVM based Verification testbenches at Block Level.
  • Good scripting skills using perl, python
  • Previous work experience in DRAM memory related fields and Must possess good communication, debugging skills and ability to work well in a team.
  • Should be proactive in exploring and leveraging AI-related tools in day-to-day tasks.
  • Proactively adopt approved AI tools to support verification workflows, scripting, and methodology improvements, maintaining full responsibility for technical decisions.

Bachelor’s with 4-6 years of experience or Post Graduate Degree in Electronics Engineering or related engineering field required with 2-4 years of experience.
