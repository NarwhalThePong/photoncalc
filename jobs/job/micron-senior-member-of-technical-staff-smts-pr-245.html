Job / Role Summary

The SMTS acts as a technical authority in NAND Advanced Thin Films, setting strategic direction, architecting solutions, and leading multi-functional initiatives with department-wide impact. The role drives innovation adoption, influences technical standards, leads advanced development/fixing/integration, and develops engineering capability through mentorship and knowledge building.

Main Responsibilities

  • Define technical strategy and lead multi-functional projects in structural and electrical film development.

  • Architect solutions and promote innovative technologies across teams; influence standards and drive process improvements.

  • Lead advanced process development, fixing, and integration; validate within and cross-module interactions and device/process linkages via wafer-level electricals.

  • Drive complex problem solving across process/structure/hardware/device interactions (RCA, FMEA, SPC, corrective actions that recover window without yield trade-off).

  • Lead vendor engagements to push hardware/process capability; secure resources and set achievements.

Other Responsibilities

  • Mentor engineers through structured development; build SME pipelines; publish internal papers/BKMs and present to senior leadership; uphold Micron’s technical leadership expectations

  • Integrates AI-assisted tools and insights into daily work to improve efficiency, quality, or effectiveness, exercising sound judgment and complying with organizational standards and legal requirements.

  • Contributes to a culture of continuous improvement by identifying, testing, and sharing AI-enabled enhancements within one’s scope of work.

Candidate Profile

  • PhD ≥ 12 yrs , or Master’s ≥ 1 5 yrs, of relevant experience in
    Electrical Engineering, Materials Science, Chemical Engineering, Physics, or related field,

  • Advanced thin film processing proficiency (PECVD / thermal CVD / ALD) and hands-on equipment optimization for dielectric + carbon films.

  • Advanced characterization and correlation modeling linking process parameters to reliability/scaling behavior.

  • Strong understanding of process-mechanical-device interactions and NAND scaling.
