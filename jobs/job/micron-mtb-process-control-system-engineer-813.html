Job Summary

As a Process Control Systems Engineer, you will integrate and monitor key performance indicators across semiconductor manufacturing Defense Line systems, including SPC, FDC, R2R, and other process control applications. You will identify KPI gaps, coordinate CAPA follow-up, track actions through closure, and provide management-level performance visibility.

You will also develop dashboards, retrieve and analyze data using SQL and Snowflake, support data digitalization, and participate in AI-enabled manufacturing and analytics projects. This role requires strong analytical thinking, cross-functional coordination, learning agility, and an interest in semiconductor process control and smart manufacturing.

Key Responsibilities

  • Integrate and monitor KPIs across different Defense Line and PCS systems.
  • Identify performance gaps and follow up on corrective and preventive actions.
  • Track action owners, due dates, risks, effectiveness verification, and closure status.
  • Develop dashboards for KPI, system health, issue, and CAPA monitoring.
  • Retrieve, clean, transform, and analyze data using SQL and Snowflake.
  • Support AI, machine learning, and advanced analytics applications.
  • Coordinate with process, manufacturing, quality, automation, IT, and global PCS teams.
  • Participate in PCS integration, digitalization, and management-assigned projects.
  • Maintain technical documents, governance workflows, and audit evidence.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree or above in engineering, computer science, data science, statistics, or a related technical field.
  • Basic knowledge of SQL, databases, data analysis, or programming is preferred.
  • Strong analytical, communication, and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to communicate in English in a technical working environment.
  • Experience with Snowflake, Power BI, Python, AI, SPC, FDC, or semiconductor manufacturing is preferred.
