At Intel's Logic Technology Development (LTD) we have continued to extend Moore's law to be world leaders in computing technology. To achieve the necessary scaling of our semiconductor manufacturing process, we have led the industry in developing enhancements such as strained silicon for carrier transport enhancement, high-k metal gates, and FINFETs. LTD has been at the center of every new development that has kept Moore's Law moving forward. Defect Metrology plays a key role in the development of every new silicon process development. Our analysis builds the plan that allows LTD to take technology from the research phase to the high-volume manufacturing phase while maintaining high-quality standards. In the MIE organization, we engineer world-class yields on F25 processes by driving defects down to Best-in-Class targets.

 

As a Defect Metrology engineer, you will be responsible for identifying all defects that impact yield and performance in an advanced silicon process technology. You will characterize the process using cutting-edge metrology tools. With this characterization, you will be responsible for investigating and identifying the root cause owners and developing defect reduction roadmaps to achieve program milestone commitments to our customers. You will then work directly with the process modules and integration teams to drive that roadmap by developing and qualifying process fixes through Model-Based Problem Solving (MBPS). You will also respond to exclusionary baseline shifts in defect trends to isolate the root cause and implement preventative actions consistent with 8D methodology. This role provides an opportunity to influence Intel's future process technologies that will keep Moore's Law moving forward.

 

Responsibilities include but are not limited to:

  • Owning a segment of the process to ensure adequate metrology is placed to detect and respond to all yield-limiting defects prior to reaching end-of-line sort
  • Manage data collection strategies, data integrity and quality for the metrology scans in the segment
  • Investigate and respond to exclusionary baseline shifts to ensure segment defect health is restored to capable, demonstrated baseline
  • Develop new recipes and response action plans as part of New Product Introduction (NPI)
  • Provide navigation and leadership to meet Intel's yield objectives, utilizing state-of-the-art metrology tools and test vehicles (QTM, Short Loop, etc.)
  • Organizing and presenting defect summaries to F25, LTD, MDCE engineering teams.
  • Partnering with Intel Integration, Yield, and failure analysis labs to identify root cause for all defect issues.
  • Hold the team and collaborators accountable for quality through Integrated Module Team (IMT) and Focus Team (FT) forums.
  • Work collaboratively as a part of the overall LTD Defect metrology group.
  • Role-model and establish a team culture of trust, collaboration, safety, accountability, and sustaining excellence.
  • Build strong relationships with other LTD teams based on mutual trust and respect.
