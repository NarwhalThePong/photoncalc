Job Description

JOB SUMMARY

Senior Engineer Manufacturing Engineering  is responsible for process development, optimization, qualification, and continuous improvement of substrate manufacturing processes to ensure stable, reliable, and high-quality output. Chemical, mechanical, and optical process parameters are monitored, analysed and optimized to achieve consistent substrate quality and performance. Process documentation, safety standards, and technician training are developed, while process projects, substrate characterization, and manufacturing capability development are supported.

ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:

  • Optical Precision Tool Experience –experience in handling optical-based precision manufacturing tools and equipment (laser systems, alignment systems, measurement tools) 

  • Glass Processing & Material Science – experience in glass/ceramic properties, handling, and quality parameters specific to optical/substrate glass 

  • Troubleshooting & Root Cause Analysis – systematic problem-solving for equipment failures and process deviations 

  • Process Engineering – ability to optimize (manufacturing) processes, identify bottlenecks, and improve yield/throughput and quality 

Qualifications

REQUIRED:

  • Master’s degree in Mechanical engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics Engineering, Materials Engineering, Automation Engineering, Biomedical Engineering ,Aerospace Engineering or a related engineering field.
  • Fresh Master’s graduates in relevant engineering disciplines are encouraged to apply.

PREFERRED:

  • AI/ML Literacy – data-driven, with foundational knowledge of machine learning applications in handling large size of data for predictive maintenance, defect detection, and process optimization  

  • Project Management: Ability to track and manage multiple projects, commissioning and improvement action plan
