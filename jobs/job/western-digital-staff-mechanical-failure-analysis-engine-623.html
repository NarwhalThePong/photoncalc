Job Description

ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:

  • Responsible for HDD Teardown FA all customers from China.
  • Owning of the mechanical failure analysis process and equipment.
  • Documentation of all observations and conclusions through images, teardown analysis and possible cause of failure.
  • Discussing the FA findings and communicating the next course of analysis.
  • General housekeeping, including maintenance logs and calibration schedule for all lab equipment
  • Ability to interact across all levels of the organization.
  • Provide effective information on tool or process improvements related to FA.

Qualifications

EDUCATION:

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering (preferred), Electrical Engineering, Physics, or equivalent

EXPERIENCE:

  •  2-5 years of experience in hard disk drive or related industry

REQUIRED SKILLS:

  • Strong written and verbal English communication skills

  • Hands-on individual who enjoys lab work and performs analysis using high-power microscopes and precision equipment

PREFERRED SKILLS/EXPERIENCE:

  • Basic knowledge of hard disk drive and head/disk interface
  • Familiarity with analytical techniques: Raman, FTIR, SEM, and TOF-SIMS
  •  Familiarity with electrical failure analysis methodology and interpretation of findings
  • Data analytical skills
