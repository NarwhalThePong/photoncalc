What You’ll Do

  • Analyze yield, defect, and failure analysis data to identify trends and anomalies.
  • Investigate yield excursions and product failures to identify root causes and corrective actions.
  • Collaborate with engineering teams to improve yield, quality, and manufacturing performance.
  • Collaborate with engineers to improve accuracy, tool matching, and data integrity.
  • Support defect characterization, failure analysis, and correlation studies.
  • Develop dashboards, automation solutions, and data visualization tools.
  • Drive an engineering project to completion and present results at the end of the internship.
  • Apply AI-enabled tools and data-driven methodologies to improve productivity, enhance technical analyses, and support innovation in yield and failure analysis workflows.
