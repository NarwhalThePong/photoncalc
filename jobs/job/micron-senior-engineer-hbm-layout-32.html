Job Description:


Join Micron Technology, Inc.’s HBM Team in Guadalajara, Mexico, and be part of a top-tier group of visionaries and scientists advancing High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) technologies. These technologies drive artificial intelligence and high-performance computing solutions. As a Senior Engineer HBM Layout, you will collaborate with international teams on multiple projects to ensure accurate execution and timely delivery of vital layouts.


Responsibilities:


  • Build and develop analog, mixed-signal, and custom digital blocks, along with full-chip level integration support.
  • Perform layout verification tasks, including LVS/DRC/Antenna checks, quality control, and documentation.
  • Ensure timely delivery of block-level layouts with outstanding quality.
  • Lead planning, area/time estimation, scheduling, delegation, and execution to meet project schedules and achievements.
  • Communicate effectively with global engineering teams to determine project success.
  • Apply and build AI-enabled methodologies for automation and problem-solving.

Minimum Qualifications:


  • 5+ years of experience in analog/custom layout development in advanced CMOS processes, spanning various technology nodes (Planar, FinFET).
  • BE or MTech in Electronic/VLSI Engineering, or a related field.
  • Proficiency in Cadence VLE/VXL and Mentor Graphic Calibre DRC/LVS is essential.
  • Hands-on experience in crafting layouts for critical blocks such as Temperature Sensors, PLL, ADC, DAC, LDO, Bandgap, Reference Generators, Charge Pumps, Current Mirrors, Comparators, and Differential Amplifiers.
  • Strong knowledge of Analog Layout fundamentals (e.g., Matching, Electro-migration, Latch-up, Coupling, Crosstalk, IR-drop, active and passive parasitic devices).
  • Understanding of layout effects on circuit performance, including speed, capacitance, power, and area.
  • Capability to comprehend build constraints and implement high-quality layouts.

Preferred Qualifications:


  • Experience supporting multiple tape-outs is highly advantageous.
  • Proven track record in managing multiple layout projects while ensuring quality checks at all stages.
  • Outstanding problem-solving skills in the physical verification of custom layouts.
