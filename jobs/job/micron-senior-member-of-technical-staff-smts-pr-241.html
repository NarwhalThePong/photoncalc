Role Summary

As a Senior Member of Technical Staff (SMTS), you are a module‑level CMP technical authority, responsible for defining CMP technical direction, leading FOAK developments, and driving cross‑functional execution across NAND nodes. As a SMTS (Senior Member of Technical Staff), you will be a part of revolutionary Next Generation NAND research & development CMP team driving Advanced NAND and CMP technology forward. The position promises ample opportunities for innovation, collaboration and testing a new dimension and scaling with Next Generation NAND. A SMTS CMP Engineer is a senior individual contributor responsible for defining, developing, and transferring advanced CMP processes for Micron NAND technologies. You will work directly with process integration and other process areas such as Wet Etch, CVD, Diffusion, Metals, Lithography & Dry Etch. You will work on state of the art and best in class CMP chambers & own the process roadmap to meet program and yield goals.

Main Responsibilities

  • Own and lead key NAND CMP process modules, translating technology challenges into executable roadmaps across current and future nodes
  • Drive cross‑functional execution across Technology Development, Process Integration, Manufacturing, and Equipment teams
  • Lead FOAK CMP process development, including recipe architecture, control strategies, and integration alignment
  • Ensure process robustness and manufacturability through run‑to‑run controls, SPC, qualification execution, and risk mitigation
  • Resolve complex yield, defectivity, and integration issues using model‑based and data‑driven problem solving
  • Define and execute process‑level hardware strategies in collaboration with equipment suppliers
  • Act as a CMP technical leader, mentoring engineers, influencing adjacent process roadmaps, and communicating technology status and plans to senior stakeholders

Candidate Profile

Minimum Qualifications / Experience

  • PhD ≥12 yrs, or Master’s ≥15 yrs, of relevant experience in
    Electrical Engineering, Materials Science, Chemical Engineering, Physics, or related field,
  • Experience in CMP Process Development
  • Deep expertise in CMP chemistry, physics, and integration
  • Publications/patents and strong record of technical contributions.

Must-Have Technical Skills

  • In‑depth expertise in CMP fundamentals, including pad–slurry–wafer interactions, slurry chemistry at a mechanistic level, and pattern density/layout effects
  • Strong understanding of multiple CMP process types (e.g., metal, dielectric, barrier/hardmask, poly‑Si/Si CMP)
  • Extensive hands‑on experience with advanced 300 mm CMP OEM platforms, including driving hardware capability, throughput, and cost improvements
  • Proven expertise in statistical process control (SPC), data analysis, and qualification methodologies
  • Ability to independently drive complex technical execution, resolve yield and defectivity issues, and influence cross‑functional teams

Must-Have Soft Skills

  • Strong ability to influence without authority across process integration, equipment, and manufacturing teams
  • Clear and structured technical communication skills, including written documentation and stakeholder updates
  • Strong analytical mindset with disciplined, data‑driven decision making
