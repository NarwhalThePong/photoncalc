Project Title

AI-Enabled Manufacturing Analytics and Productivity Automation

Project Description

The intern will undertake a structured project that applies Artificial Intelligence, Generative AI, data analytics, and automation technologies to selected opportunities within New Product Manufacturing.

Under the guidance of experienced engineers and business stakeholders, the intern will evaluate defined processes where AI-Enabled solutions may reduce manual effort, improve data visibility, automate reporting, and generate actionable insights from manufacturing and operational data.

The project may involve automated work-in-process reporting, lot-prioritization analysis, intelligent dashboards, or productivity automation. The intern will gain practical experience using approved tools such as Microsoft Copilot, Power BI, Power Automate, Power Apps, SharePoint, Python, and Structured Query Language.

Objective of the Project

  • Develop an understanding of New Product Manufacturing processes, data sources, and operational challenges.
  • Identify a defined opportunity where AI, Generative AI, data analytics, or automation may improve productivity and data visibility.
  • Design and develop an AI-Enabled proof of concept for the selected use case.
  • Evaluate the solution’s potential impact and document recommendations for future development.

Opportunities for Full Time Employment

High-performing interns may be considered for future internship or full-time employment opportunities, subject to business requirements, position availability, and the applicable selection process.

Project Scope

  • Evaluate selected New Product Manufacturing processes and identify opportunities for AI-Enabled analytics or workflow automation.
  • Define the requirements, data inputs, business rules, and intended outcomes for the assigned use case.
  • Develop a proof of concept involving intelligent data processing, dashboard development, reporting automation, or AI-Assisted decision workflows.
  • Create visualizations for selected operational metrics, work-in-process visibility, risk monitoring, alerts, or trend analysis.
  • Measure the prototype’s potential productivity, efficiency, and decision-making benefits using agreed project indicators.

Learning Opportunities

  • Gain exposure to New Product Manufacturing processes, operational data, and digital-transformation initiatives.

  • Collaborate with engineering, manufacturing, automation, data, and business stakeholders.

Deliverables

  • An AI opportunity assessment covering the current process, proposed use case, expected benefits, and recommended implementation roadmap.

  • An interactive dashboard or productivity tool that demonstrates relevant metrics, insights, notifications, or reporting automation.
  • Technical documentation, a user guide, a business-impact assessment, and a final presentation.

Impact of the Project
