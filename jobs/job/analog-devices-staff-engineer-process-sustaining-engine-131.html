Responsibilities:

  • Serve as the technical subject matter expert (SME) for Dry Etch, Wet Etch, and Clean process technologies in a semiconductor manufacturing environment.
  • Lead yield improvement initiatives through root cause analysis, defect reduction, and process optimization.
  • Develop, execute, and analyze Design of Experiments (DOE) and Statistical Process Control (SPC) methodologies to improve process capability and product quality.
  • Drive continuous improvement projects focused on throughput, cycle time, manufacturability, cost reduction, and process robustness.
  • Lead qualification of new processes, equipment, toolsets, and technology transfers into manufacturing.
  • Troubleshoot and resolve complex process and equipment issues through data-driven problem-solving and cross-functional collaboration.
  • Partner with Product, Integration, Equipment, and Manufacturing teams to ensure process compatibility, manufacturability, and production readiness.
  • Establish and maintain process control strategies, specifications, and documentation to support stable manufacturing operations.
  • Provide technical leadership, mentoring, and guidance to engineers, technicians, and manufacturing personnel.
  • Lead cross-functional projects and represent Sustaining Engineering in support of organizational goals related to safety, quality, delivery, and operational excellence.

 

Requirements:

  • Bachelors or Master’s degree in ChemE, Mat Sci, EE, Physics, Chemistry or a related discipline.
  • 7+ years of direct semiconductor industry experience, Subject Matter Experts (SME) with Dry and Wet Etch, Clean process technologies.
  • Ability to develop resolutions to transfer technology and qualify complex toolsets, resolve problems with diverse scopes requiring in-depth analysis.
  • Advanced understanding of statistical process control (SPC), MFG throughput, efficiency and engineering statistics.

 

Preferred:

  • 7+ years of direct semiconductor manufacturing industry with experience on Applied Material Etchers, Wet Etch, FSI and/or JST equipment are a plus.
  • Significant CMOS/Bipolar knowledge of device physics as-well-as device integration.
  • 5+ years of interdisciplinary project management experience within semiconductor industry. 
  • 5+ years of hands-on academic or industry experience with process and materials development.
  • Able to occasionally travel domestically and internationally as needed.
