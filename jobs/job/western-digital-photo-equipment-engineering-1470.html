Job Description

The position is needed for equipment engineering group in Photo area responsible for manufacturing equipment. Equipment failure analysis and characterization, with the emphasize on the following areas:

ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Assist Equipment Engineering – Optimizing, improving and providing solutions/feedback for equipment.
  • Learn and maintain obsolescence tools
  • PM trainer/assistant) that could work a hybrid shift. 
    • Train and assist, as needed, with PMs.  Since we have a lot of PAS tools that might be a good place to start. 
    • This would really help upskill shifts that need more help and alleviate some of the shift specific PMs. 
    • This individual would help keep data together, highlight potential problems and provide feedback on all shifts need more resources.   
  • Work with maintenance team to trouble-shoot, provide long term solutions and training.
  • Lead and drive the corrective actions associated with equipment issue.
  • Assist in transitioning FR/GO Wiki
  • Monitor equipment performance, identify issues and fix tool.
  • Certify and train maintenance, chemical technicians and equipment tool owners.
  • Establish written procedures and specs for maintenance tech.
  • Work with process engineering team to provide resolution/reduction of rework and scrap issues.
  • Work with EE on investigations for equipment related excursion and provide a solution.
  • Assiste EE in 8D summary to maint/prod/process and various department stake holders.
  • Provide safety recommendations on equipment in addition ensuring tools meet QA site requirements.

REQUIRED

  • Demonstrated ability to manage projects with set milestone and timeline, and ability to execute under minimal supervision.
  • A responsible and team player. Detail-oriented and willingness to learn and thrive in a cross-functional manufacturing environment.
