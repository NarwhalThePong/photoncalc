Introduction

We are seeking a highly motivated Senior Hardware Engineer to drive the development and integration of advanced power-delivery solutions for next-generation AI and data center applications. In this role, you will work across ASIC, package, PCB, and system levels, ensuring robust electrical, thermal, and mechanical integration of innovative power management products.


As part of a multidisciplinary engineering team, you will collaborate closely with ASIC designers, package and OSAT partners, PCB designers, system engineers, and customers to translate system requirements into successful hardware implementations. From architecture definition through bring-up, validation, and product realization, you will play a key role in delivering industry-leading power delivery solutions for AI accelerators, GPUs, and high-performance computing platforms.


Your Job

  • Define hardware and system-level requirements for advanced power delivery ASIC solutions.
  • Drive hardware development and integration across ASIC, package, PCB, and system levels.
  • Collaborate with package designers, OSAT partners, PCB designers, and IC design teams to optimize power delivery performance, manufacturability, and reliability.
  • Evaluate and address electrical, thermal, and mechanical design constraints throughout product development.
  • Partner with customers to understand application requirements and translate them into technical specifications.
  • Lead hardware bring-up, characterization, debugging, root-cause analysis, and system validation activities.
  • Define validation strategies and support technical reviews, project execution, and stakeholder communication.

 

 

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, or a related technical discipline.
  • 5+ years of experience in hardware, system, or power electronics design within the semiconductor, power management, or high-performance computing industry.
  • Proven experience developing and bringing up complex PCB- and semiconductor-based systems.
  • Strong understanding of power electronics, power delivery architectures, and system integration across ASIC, package, PCB, and system levels.
  • Good knowledge of PCB design principles, including power distribution networks (PDN), signal integrity, and power integrity.
  • Experience with hardware characterization, laboratory debugging, validation, and failure analysis.
  • Understanding of thermal and mechanical considerations in high-performance electronic systems.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and cross-functional collaboration skills.
  • Fluent English language skills, both written and spoken.
  • Willingness to work in the office in Pavia, mobile work possible.

 

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience working with advanced semiconductor packaging technologies and OSAT partners.
  • Familiarity with high-current, high-power-density applications.
  • Experience with power delivery solutions for AI accelerators, GPUs, CPUs, or data-center platforms.
  • Experience interacting directly with customers and translating system requirements into engineering solutions.
