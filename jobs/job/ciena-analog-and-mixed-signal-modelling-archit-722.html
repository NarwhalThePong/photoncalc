We are seeking a Analog & Mixed-Signal Modelling Architect – New Grad to join our DSP team. In this role, you will drive the design, modeling, and system-level validation of analog and mixed-signal subsystems for next-generation high-speed coherent optical modems.


This position emphasizes behavioral modeling using C++ and SystemC, tightly integrated with Python and MATLAB-based system simulations, firmware bring-up flows, and lab validation. You will operate at the intersection of analog-/digital-hardware, DSP, firmware, and electro-optical subsystems - contributing architectural decisions from early concept through silicon bring-up and product deployment.


How you will make an impact:

  • Define and review system-level analog and mixed-signal functions for coherent optical modems.
  • Develop behavioral models of analog and mixed-signal blocks using C++ and SystemC, emphasizing firmware register-map interfaces, control, observability, and execution speed.
  • Apply C++/ SystemC-AMS to model continuous-time and mixed-signal behavior of PLLs, clocking systems, filters, DACs, ADCs, SerDes, and related subsystems.
  • Build and maintain C++ and Python/MATLAB-based verification flows, including C++ MEX wrappers for system simulation environments.
  • Collaborate with analog, digital, DSP, firmware, and lab teams to align models, specifications, and validation plans.
  • Support ASIC lab bring‑up and characterization, correlating silicon and lab data with behavioral models and system simulations.
  • Plan design activities and create design guidelines, implementation documentation, and setup guides in Confluence.

The must haves:

  • Education: Post-graduate degree in Physics or Engineering with a focus on ASIC hardware, communication systems, and/or control systems.
  • Experience: Previous internship, co-op, or applied project experience relevant to the role.
  • Understanding of analog and mixed-signal processing in high-speed communication systems.
  • Experience writing C++ and/or SystemC behavioral models for hardware-adjacent systems.
  • Demonstrated ability to operate at the system-architecture level while remaining grounded in real analog behavior and physical impairments.
  • Knowledge of analog impairments, including noise, distortion, non-linearity, bandwidth limitations, jitter, and phase noise, and their impact on modem-level performance.
  • Strong intuition for analog-digital partitioning, power-performance trade-offs, and calibration strategies.
  • Comfortable working in a Linux-based development environment, using Git and modern collaborative workflows.
  • Clear technical communication skills and the ability to work effectively across disciplines.
  • Experience with the following tools: C++, SystemC-AMS, Python, MATLAB, Simulink, Cadence Virtuoso, Cadence AMS Designer, Xcelium AMS
