Project Title

AI-Enabled Predictive Analytics for SSD Test Equipment Health Monitoring
 

Project Description

Objective of the Project

Develop a data-driven equipment monitoring framework that leverages engineering data and AI-enabled analytics to identify early indicators of equipment degradation, optimize maintenance strategies, and improve SSD test equipment reliability in a high-volume semiconductor manufacturing environment.
 

The intern will gain hands-on exposure to semiconductor manufacturing, equipment engineering, data analytics, automation, and AI-enabled engineering workflows while working on real-world manufacturing challenges.
 

Project Scope

Learning Opportunities

  • Understand SSD test manufacturing processes and semiconductor production environments.

  • Learn the architecture and operation of automated SSD test equipment platforms.

  • Gain experience in equipment reliability engineering and predictive maintenance methodologies.

  • Develop skills in data extraction, cleansing, analysis, and visualization using engineering datasets.

  • Apply AI tools and analytical techniques to derive actionable equipment health insights.

  • Collaborate with equipment, process, manufacturing, and automation engineering teams on cross-functional improvement initiatives.
     

Deliverables

  • Develop a centralized equipment health monitoring dashboard utilizing manufacturing and equipment datasets.

  • Perform statistical analysis to identify key indicators associated with equipment performance degradation.

  • Create an AI-assisted anomaly detection methodology for selected equipment subsystems.

  • Recommend predictive maintenance opportunities based on engineering data findings.

  • Document the analysis methodology, results, and business impact of the implemented solution.

  • Present project outcomes and recommendations to site engineering stakeholders.
     

Skillsets Required

  • Fundamental understanding of engineering principles and data analysis concepts.

  • Programming knowledge in Python, SQL, or equivalent analytical tools.

  • Familiarity with data visualization platforms such as Tableau, Power BI, or similar tools.

  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

  • Effective verbal and written communication skills.

  • Exposure to AI tools, machine learning concepts, or AI-enabled workflows is advantageous.

  • Ability to work independently while collaborating effectively within cross-functional teams.
     

Course of Interest

The ideal candidate should be pursuing Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Electronic Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Data Science, Industrial Engineering, Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, or a related field of study.
 

Duration of Period

The ideal candidate should be able to commit to a full-time internship period of 5 months from Jan to May 2027.
