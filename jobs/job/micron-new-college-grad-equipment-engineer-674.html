Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

As an Equipment Engineer, you will ensure the reliability, performance, and continuous improvement of advanced semiconductor manufacturing equipment by solving complex technical challenges, owning end-to-end equipment performances, and partnering across manufacturing, process engineering, maintenance, and supplier teams. As part of Micron’s AI-enabled ecosystem, Fab Equipment Engineers use AI-supported data, equipment health insights, and advanced analytics to improve tool performance, reliability, troubleshooting, and continuous improvement across semiconductor manufacturing.

 

RESPONSIBILITIES 

  • Own equipment performance and reliability by identifying issues, driving corrective actions, and lifecycle improvement strategies
  • Diagnose technical issues by applying structured problem-solving and enabling rapid tool recovery to prevent emerging issues 
  • Drive equipment upgrade/modification projects, implement hardware solutions and partner with vendors to resolve issues 
  • Partner and collaborate across functions (Process, Manufacturing, Facilities, Quality, Maintenance teams) to resolve issues 
  • Communicate findings and recommendations to stakeholders
  • Create and maintain SOPs, troubleshooting guides, and drive knowledge sharing 
  • Ensure compliance with safety, quality and manufacturing standards 

Minimum Qualifications

Shown through education, internships, research, military, manufacturing, or professional experience.

  • Demonstrated experience with technical troubleshooting, problem solving, root cause analysis, equipment or system diagnostics, and analyzing or interpreting data.
  • Demonstrated knowledge of SPC principles and continuous improvement methods.
  • Experience working with mechanical, electrical, automation, or manufacturing systems.
  • Demonstrated ability to create technical documentation, communicate effectively with customers and stakeholders, and collaborate across functions.
  • Demonstrated ownership, initiative, adaptability, attention to detail, and analytical thinking. Preferred disciplines include Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Materials Science, Manufacturing, Mechatronics, or Industrial Engineering.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience or exposure to semiconductor manufacturing or high-volume manufacturing environments.
  • Experience or exposure to equipment maintenance, equipment reliability, equipment qualification, or validation.
  • Experience or exposure to robotics, automation, vacuum systems, or industrial control systems.
  • Experience or exposure to SPC, FDC, MES, or other manufacturing data and control systems.
  • Experience or exposure to process improvement and data-driven optimization of manufacturing processes or equipment.
