Job Description

ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:

  • Assist in setting up and troubleshooting test equipment, including Testcell, Tester, Bagger, VDCS, and Conveyor systems, under the guidance of senior technicians or engineers.
  • Support troubleshooting and resolution of Testcell yield issues to maintain product quality and throughput.
  • Assist in investigations for quality incidents related to test equipment and support root cause analysis and corrective actions.
  • Assist in performing product conversions on test equipment to support various production requirements.
  • Monitor key process input variables (KPIVs) using tools such as the KPOV database, OEE tracking, Maintenance Logs, and the Digital Preventive Maintenance system.
  • Support equipment upgrade and continuous improvement projects under the direction of senior technicians or engineers.
  • Assist engineers and senior technicians with daily tasks and contribute to special projects as required.
  • Ensure compliance with ISO standards and documentation practices.
  • Adhere strictly to all safety rules and cleanroom protocols.
  • Perform equipment checklists in accordance with standard operating procedures.

Qualifications

Qualifications

REQUIRED:

  • Diploma in Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Mechatronics, or related field.
  • 1–2 years' working experience in a manufacturing environment, preferably in equipment maintenance, troubleshooting, and repair.
  • Fresh graduates with a Diploma are encouraged to apply.
  • Basic knowledge of mechanical systems (motors, bearings, gears, conveyors, pumps, compressors) and electrical systems (AC/DC, sensors, relays).
  • Basic familiarity with hydraulic and pneumatic systems, including valves, cylinders, and pressure controls.
  • Ability to read and interpret basic technical documentation (electrical diagrams, equipment manuals) with guidance.
  • Understanding of fundamental failure mechanisms and ability to assist in isolating, detecting, and correcting equipment issues.
  • Proficiency in using MS Windows Software (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook) for reporting and documentation.
  • Knowledge of safety protocols and regulations in a manufacturing environment (Lockout/Tagout, PPE usage).

PREFERRED:

  • Basic exposure to PLC concepts and automation/controls.
  • Exposure to preventive maintenance activities.
  • Basic experience with Test & Measurement equipment.

SKILLS:

  • Technical Aptitude – Eagerness and ability to learn hands-on maintenance, repairs, and adjustments on industrial machinery.
  • Troubleshooting – Basic logical fault isolation and problem-solving skills for mechanical and electrical issues, with guidance from senior staff.
  • Tool & Instrument Proficiency – Basic familiarity with the use of multimeters, calipers, torque wrenches, and other common diagnostic tools.
  • Communication – Ability to clearly relay technical findings to colleagues, senior technicians, and supervisors.
  • Safety Awareness – Knowledge and practice of safe work procedures in industrial settings.
  • Teamwork – Effective collaborator across production, engineering, and quality teams.
  • Adaptability – Capable of handling unexpected equipment issues and evolving responsibilities.
  • Continuous Learning – Strong willingness to learn new machines, processes, and emerging automation technologies.
