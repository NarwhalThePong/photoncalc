Responsibilities and Tasks

Perform Electrical Validation and Failure Analysis Support

  • Execute electrical validation and characterization activities on semiconductor devices.
  • Perform fault verification and data gathering to support failure analysis investigations.
  • Operate engineering test platforms and laboratory instrumentation according to established procedures.
  • Support fault isolation activities through systematic testing and analysis techniques.
  • Capture, review, and document test results with high attention to accuracy and detail.
  • Assist engineers in identifying failure signatures, abnormal behaviors, and performance deviations.
  • Ensure all analysis activities comply with quality, safety, and operational guidelines.

Laboratory Operations and Equipment Readiness

  • Prepare test hardware, fixtures, adapters, and supporting materials for analysis activities.
  • Verify laboratory equipment readiness prior to testing activities.
  • Perform routine laboratory housekeeping and maintain an organized working environment.
  • Participate in preventive maintenance, calibration coordination, and operational checks as required.
  • Ensure proper handling and tracking of engineering samples and materials.
  • Follow ESD, safety, and contamination control requirements during all activities.

Data Collection and Technical Documentation

  • Maintain accurate records of validation results, observations, and analysis findings.
  • Generate test summaries and support engineering reports.
  • Track progress of assigned activities and communicate status updates effectively.
  • Support continuous improvement initiatives through data-driven observations and recommendations.
  • Assist in maintaining laboratory documentation, work instructions, and training records.

Digital and AI Competency

  • Utilize AI-Enabled, AI-Assisted, and AI-Augmented tools to improve productivity, technical analysis, troubleshooting, and decision-making.
  • Demonstrate familiarity with Artificial Intelligence, Large Language Models (LLMs), AI Assistants, Agentic AI, and emerging Agentic Solutions to support engineering and operational activities.
  • Leverage digital platforms such as Cloud Assist, Code Assist, Roo Code, and other AI-supported solutions to accelerate learning, knowledge discovery, documentation, and problem solving.
  • Apply Generative AI and AI-Supported technologies responsibly for data analysis, automation, technical reporting, and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Continuously develop competency in Using AI, Applying AI, Leveraging AI, Using Artificial Intelligence, Applying Artificial Intelligence, and Leveraging Artificial Intelligence within an engineering and laboratory environment.

Engineering and Cross-Functional Collaboration

  • Work closely with Failure Analysis Engineers, Product Engineers, Test Engineers, and Manufacturing teams.
  • Support engineering investigations involving product quality, reliability, and manufacturing performance.
  • Participate in technical discussions, problem-solving activities, and knowledge-sharing sessions.
  • Assist in qualification, benchmarking, and evaluation of new test methodologies and laboratory capabilities.
  • Contribute to continuous improvement projects that enhance laboratory efficiency, capability, and quality.

Continuous Learning and Skill Development

  • Build competency in semiconductor technology, electrical characterization, and failure analysis methodologies.
  • Complete structured on-the-job training and certification programs.
  • Develop troubleshooting, analytical thinking, and technical communication skills through practical experience.
  • Support laboratory learning initiatives and technical knowledge transfers.

Education/Skills/Experience Requirement
