Responsibilities

  • Validation Execution
  • Assist in executing Validation Runs on IQP/ETV materials under defined timelines, ensuring coverage completeness across MBIST and Native Mode flows.
  • Support time-bound data crunching (log parsing, statistical summaries) and contribute to summary report preparation.
  • Platform & Test Program Support
  • Operate and debug programs on SM3/Cobra (MBIST) and Advantest V93K (Native).
  • Assist in hardware readiness: handler, socket, loadboard, and probe card setup.
  • Technical Analysis & Simulation
  • Validate high-speed I/O (>11 Gbps) and assist with eye diagram and signal integrity checks.
  • Support Verilog simulation tasks (testbench execution, waveform review) to reproduce sightings and validate fixes.
  • Data & Automation
  • Develop basic scripts in Python for log parsing and data visualization.
  • Maintain documentation in Confluence/Jira and version control in Git/Perforce.
  • Debug & Collaboration
  • Participate in root cause analysis sessions and assist in corrective action closure.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams (Design, DV, QA, GQ) and attend technical meetings.
  • Contributes to a culture of continuous improvement by identifying, testing, and sharing AI-enabled enhancements within one’s scope of work.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s in Electrical or Computer Engineering (or related) with Experience or MS in EE/CE
  • 2+ years experience in semiconductor validation or product engineering (DRAM/HBM preferred).
  • Exposure to HBM Validation concepts and flows.
  • Basic experience in test platform code development (C/C++, Python) and familiarity with APG (Algorithmic Pattern Generator).
  • Understanding of MBIST and Native Mode testing platforms.
  • Verilog simulation exposure (running testbenches, interpreting waveforms).
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills; ability to work in a fast-paced environment.

​Preferred Skills

  • Familiarity with Linux environment and version control systems (Git/SVN).
  • Comfortable with lab equipment (oscilloscopes, logic analyzers).
  • Knowledge of DRAM/HBM architecture and datasheet interpretation.
  • Interest in automation and continuous improvement initiatives.
