Join Our Ambitious Team

Join the team as an intern at Micron Technology, Inc., you will be offered a unique opportunity to work with a versatile team of process and equipment engineers. Your role will be crucial in launching, developing, and enhancing processes that improve product quality and reliability. We concentrate on process yield increases, cost savings, efficiency improvements, and risk control. This position lets you identify, diagnose, and fix process-related challenges using failure analysis, FMEA, 8D, or SPC approaches. You will also lead and perform evaluations and adjustments of processes, equipment, and materials in order to implement changes throughout various steps. Leading and participating in yield and cost improvement projects, handling new process baseline qualifications, and managing, auditing, and coordinating with material suppliers to meet quality, cost, and risk objectives are core duties.

Responsibilities

Responsibilities and Tasks:

  • Establish and improve process conditions and technology to ensure perfect operations.
  • Upgrade process capability and reduce production costs through innovative solutions.
  • Establish and modify process management projects to meet bold goals.
  • Set up process parameters for a variety of semiconductor equipment, ensuring extraordinary performance.
  • Evaluate, promote, and plan for new equipment and materials to stay ahead in the competitive market.
  • Analyze and improve abnormal conditions to maintain consistent, high-quality outputs.
  • Improve yield by working on tools, processes, or wafer fabrication recipes, ensuring excellent efficiency.
  • Solve wafer fabrication problems and perform root cause analysis to prevent future issues.
  • Collaborate with other process areas to understand and control process deviations technically.
  • Conduct long-term investigations of defect issues, requiring extensive collaboration with shift engineering and other functional areas.
  • Evaluate products to ensure they meet the required build and performance specifications, challenging at the highest standards.
  • Conduct research to assess process adjustment requirements and recommend vital improvements or equipment changes to efficiently manufacture new product or part types.
