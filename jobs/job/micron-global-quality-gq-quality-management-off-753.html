Role Summary and Key Responsibilities

The GQ QMO Enterprise Process Analyst plays a critical role in driving operational excellence, process governance, and continuous improvement across Micron's enterprise quality management system. This position combines system-level process stewardship, change governance leadership, and New Product Introduction (NPI) process optimization to ensure business processes remain effective, compliant, scalable, and aligned with Micron's strategic objectives.

This role is designed for an experienced professional who excels at the intersection of business process management, systems governance, operational analytics, and cross-functional collaboration. The successful candidate will work with stakeholders across Engineering, Manufacturing, IT, Quality, Supply Chain, and Business Units to improve enterprise processes, govern critical systems, and drive measurable performance improvements.

NPI System Level Process Steward (SLPS)

As the System Level Process Steward for the Introduce New Product (NPI) process, you will serve as the primary execution expert and advocate for the process, partnering closely with the System Level Process Owner (SLPO) and business process analysts. This role is responsible for ensuring the NPI process remains effective, compliant, and continuously improving while supporting business objectives across global manufacturing operations.

Key responsibilities include:

  • Execute business process and improvement activities. 
  • Develop and maintain process artifacts. 
  • Lead or facilitate business process review and ensure business process meets the planned performance. 
  • Accountable to communicate timely on major changes of the process or where there are presence of non-conformities or new CSRs, etc and identify the need to conduct additional System Level Business Process Reviews. 
  • Make certain that the process continues to meet requirements for its stakeholders. 
  • Provide process metrics and improve performance results. 
  • Report performance to the stakeholders. 
  • Provide governance for business processes including adopting of this BPM standard, aligning with best methods and tools, and driving process excellence. 
  • Support process owner to define, document, improve, and sustain the process. 
  • Support process owner to assess process risk and implement treatment and contingency plans when required. 
  • Manage risk using the QMSRM App and monitor and update process failures and/or opportunities and mitigation plans as applicable. 
  • Coordinate business process improvement activities to maintain competitive edge. 
  • Support SLPO in Customer Requirements related efforts. 

Non-Manufacturing Change Governance & Systems Ownership

This role serves as the business owner and governance lead for Micron's Non-Manufacturing Change Management process and supporting systems. You will ensure enterprise changes are managed consistently, efficiently, and in accordance with established governance requirements.

Key responsibilities include:

  • Oversee execution and compliance of the Non-Manufacturing Change process across the enterprise.
  • Monitor adherence to process requirements and drive accountability among change owners and stakeholders.
  • Serve as business owner for the Non-Manufacturing Change Management system, ensuring system capabilities support process objectives.
  • Partner with IT teams to prioritize bug resolution, system maintenance, and enhancement requests.
  • Develop and maintain enhancement roadmaps based on business priorities and stakeholder needs.
  • Analyze system usage trends, user feedback, and operational performance to identify improvement opportunities.
  • Chair governance and steering committee meetings, facilitating decision-making, issue resolution, and prioritization activities.
  • Develop reporting and performance metrics that provide visibility into process effectiveness, compliance, and business impact.
  • Ensure process documentation, training materials, and user guidance remain current and effective.

NPI Reprocessing Reduction Champion

As the NPI Reprocessing Champion, you will lead cross-functional efforts to identify, analyze, and eliminate reprocessing activities that negatively impact manufacturing capacity, cycle time, and operational efficiency during NPI execution.

Key responsibilities include:

  • Establish and maintain visibility of reprocessing trends across NPI programs.
  • Analyze process data to identify root causes and systemic drivers of reprocessing.
  • Lead cross-functional improvement initiatives focused on prevention rather than containment.
  • Partner with Network Planning, Technical Engineering and Operations teams to improve process readiness and execution quality.
  • Develop dashboards, KPIs, and reporting mechanisms to track reprocessing performance and resulting business impact.
  • Facilitate structured problem-solving and lessons-learned activities to prevent recurrence.
  • Drive standardization of best practices across sites and organizations to reduce variation and improve NPI efficiency.
  • Influence stakeholders to implement changes that improve capacity utilization, cycle time performance, and overall NPI effectiveness.
