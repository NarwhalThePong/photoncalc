Role Overview  

As a Senior Design Verification Engineer, you will help build and  validate  complex connectivity silicon that enables the next generation of AI infrastructure. You will develop scalable verification environments, integrate UVM and C/C++ workflows, and partner closely with RTL designers to  identify  and resolve issues across simulation, co-simulation, and emulation.  

This is an opportunity to contribute across the  verification  lifecycle at a hyper-growth connectivity company. Your work will help deliver reliable, high-bandwidth products that  connect  CPUs, GPUs, memory, storage, and networking devices in rack-scale AI systems.  

Key Responsibilities  

  • Verification Architecture and Planning  
  • Build and maintain UVM-based verification environments using  SystemVerilog  and C/C++ integration through DPI/PLI.  
  • Translate design specifications into verification plans, test strategies, and reusable test sequences.  
  • Develop VIP abstraction layers that simplify and scale verification across projects and platforms.  
  • Stimulus, Assertions, and Coverage  
  • Create directed and  constrained-random  tests, transaction-level sequences, and user-controlled random constraints.  
  • Write assertions and cover properties, analyze coverage data, and drive closure against verification goals.  
  • Automate verification infrastructure and regression workflows using Python or Perl.  
  • Debug and Cross-Functional Collaboration  
  • Collaborate with RTL designers and other engineers to isolate, debug, and resolve failures across simulation, co-simulation, and FPGA-based emulation.  
  • Apply third-party verification IP to  validate  protocols including PCIe, Ethernet, InfiniBand, DDR,  NVMe , and USB.  
  • Contribute to technical reviews, communicate verification status clearly, and manage priorities across multiple deliverables.  

Basic Qualifications  

  • Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering  or  a related  field.  
  • 2 - 5 years of experience developing or verifying complex SoC or silicon products.  
  • Hands-on experience with  SystemVerilog , UVM, and C/C++ in production verification environments.  
  • Experience integrating C/C++ with  SystemVerilog  environments using DPI or PLI.  
  • Experience developing verification infrastructure and tests in directed and constrained-random environments.  
  • Strong debugging, prioritization, collaboration, and communication skills.  
  • Authorized to work in Canada.  

Preferred Qualifications  

  • Experience verifying PCIe across the physical, link, and transaction layers.  
  • Experience with DDR4, DDR5, or HBM memory technologies.  
  • Experience with FPGA-based verification or emulation.  
  • Experience verifying Ethernet protocols across the PMA,  PCS  and MAC layers.  
  • Experience debugging SoC software, including kernels, device drivers, or U-Boot.  
  • Familiarity with  verification  IP from third-party vendors and protocol-level modeling.  
  • Self-directed, adaptable approach with a strong focus on quality, execution, and customer impact.
