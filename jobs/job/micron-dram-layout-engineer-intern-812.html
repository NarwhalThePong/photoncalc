DRAM Layout engineer (Intern)

Micron · Jalisco, Mexico · posted 2026-10-12

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

SUMMARY:

At Micron, we focus on developing groundbreaking silicon-to-systems solutions. We handle technology development, advanced memory builds, product development, system development, and validation to create world-class memory solutions.

In this position, you will collaborate with Micron’s global design, layout, and verification teams to develop high-quality physical layouts for cutting-edge DRAM products. You will gain hands-on experience in semiconductor layout building, physical verification, and industry-standard Electronic Compose Automation (EDA) tools.

RESPONSABILITIES:

  • Design and Verify All Levels of Analog and Digital Layout
  • Use computer aided design software (i.e. Cadence, Calibre, etc.)
  • Work with Design Engineers to floor plan and design layout
  • Understand and follow verification protocols
  • Apply custom and/or automated layout techniques to design pitch, array, and peripheral layout for digital and analog circuits.
  • Collaborate with build engineering to understand layout methodology.
  • Possess basic understanding of circuit design and electronics.

Minimum Qualifications:

  • Student pursuing a bachelor's degree (last 2 semesters preferred) in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Mechatronics, Robotics, Nanotechnology, Computer Engineering, or a related field.
  • Completed coursework or academic projects related to microelectronics, semiconductor devices, VLSI, or integrated circuit design.
  • Basic understanding of CMOS technology and layout design principles.
  • Effective interpersonal and problem-solving abilities.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Strong interest in semiconductor physical design and memory technologies.
  • Self-motivated, enthusiastic team player with a willingness to learn.
  • Familiarity with Linux environments and scripting basics.
  • Exposure to EDA tools such as Cadence Virtuoso, Synopsys IC Compiler II, Fusion Compiler, or similar tools is a plus.
  • Experience with programming or scripting languages such as Python, Tcl, Perl, C, or C++ is a plus.
  • Understanding of physical verification concepts (DRC, LVS, ERC) is a plus.
  • Using AI Tools for Design procedures.
