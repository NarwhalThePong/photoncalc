Department Overview

The Pathfinding Design team explores and develops innovative circuit architectures and design concepts that enable future generations of Micron memory products. By combining advanced digital, analog, and mixed-signal design techniques with simulation, modeling, and performance analysis, the team drives early-stage technology development and helps shape the future of memory solutions.


Position Overview

As a Design Engineer on Micron's Pathfinding Design Team, you will support the design, modeling, and analysis of digital, analog, and mixed-signal circuits used in next-generation memory technologies. You will work with experienced engineers to evaluate design concepts, develop simulation models, analyze performance trade-offs, and contribute to innovative circuit solutions that improve memory performance, power efficiency, and scalability.


Responsibilities

  • Develop simulation models to evaluate the performance of digital, analog, and mixed-signal circuit designs.
  • Perform simulations, analyze results, and create performance scorecards to compare design alternatives and architectural trade-offs.
  • Design and evaluate new circuit concepts that improve performance, power efficiency, and functionality.
  • Support mixed-signal circuit design, modeling, characterization, and validation activities.
  • Collaborate with multi-functional engineering teams to document results, communicate findings, and support technology development initiatives.

Minimum Qualifications


  • 0-2 years of relevant experience through internships, research, academic projects, or coursework in circuit design, semiconductor technology, or related fields.
  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related technical field.
  • Knowledge of mixed-signal, analog, or digital circuit design principles gained through coursework, research, or project experience.
  • Experience with circuit simulation, performance analysis, and semiconductor fundamentals.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and teamwork skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Understanding of memory architectures, memory subsystems, or DRAM technologies.
  • Experience with circuit design and simulation tools through coursework, research, internships, or personal projects.
  • Knowledge of statistics, data analysis, and engineering characterization methodologies.
  • Experience with scripting, automation, or AI-enabled engineering tools to improve technical workflows.
  • Demonstrated ability to work independently, manage multiple priorities, and solve complex technical problems.
