Job Description

As Sputter Process Technician, you will be responsible for executing process characterization activities, ensuring sputter process meets key performance parameters, and supporting production through process monitoring and adjustments.

 

Regular Responsibilities

  • Perform process characterization for sputter equipment and materials.
  • Conduct buy-off by verifying key process input variables (KPIV), including pressure, temperature, and voltage.
  • Perform measurements and data collection for characterization samples.
  • Monitor key process output variables (KPOV) during production to ensure process stability and product quality.
  • Perform process adjustments as needed to maintain specifications and resolve deviations.
  • Assist in troubleshooting process and equipment issues to minimize downtime.
  • Maintain accurate records of process parameters, test results, and adjustments.
  • Always adhere to safety guidelines and cleanroom protocols.
  • Monitor Machine alarms, take appropriate action based on guidelines & instructions provided.
  • Carry out periodic samples housekeeping & other tasks assigned by Engineer.
  • Comply and support EHS policy. Participating in company activities such as IMS, 6S, RBA, BCMS etc.

 

Essential Duties and Responsibilities:

Provide continuous support to production in sputter process methodologies, enhance sputter process performance, and optimize workflow efficiency.

Qualifications

Qualification – Education Requirement:

  • Diploma in Engineering (Mechanical), Engineering (Electrical/Electronic), Engineering (Mechatronic/Electromechanical) or equivalent.
  • Fresh graduate is encouraged to apply.
  • Working experience in sputter process and knowledge of Magnetic materials used in HDD would be an added advantage.
  • Proficient in MS Windows Software (Word/Excel/Power Point/Outlook). Macro in excel would be an added advantage.
  • Effective team player with proven relationship building & communication skills, including cross-functional Production, Equipment, Yield, and Quality.

 

Skill / Experience Requirement:

  • Junior Level Process Technician.
  • Required to work in 12 hours shift pattern with day or night cycles.
  • Willing to work in cleanroom environment.
  • Problem Solving – Proficiency in troubleshooting process controls, process adjustments, data readback from machine, control limits.
  • Communication - Ability to communicate technical information effectively to colleagues and engineers.
  • Safety – Strong understanding of safety protocols and regulations in the manufacturing environment.
  • Teamwork – Ability to work collaboratively with engineering, production, and quality teams.
  • Adaptability - Proficiency in handling emergencies and unexpected equipment failures.
