Director, Advanced Packaging Technology Development

Micron · Taichung - Fab 16, Taiwan · posted 2026-10-19

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

As a Director of Process Development in Advanced Packaging Technology Development (APTD) within Micron, you will provide strategic and technical leadership for the development and deployment of advanced packaging assembly processes enabling next-generation memory products. You will collaborate with internal and external stakeholders to define and execute technology development strategies aligned with organizational and business objectives . Your team will be responsible for developing scalable, manufacturable, and cost-effective assembly processes that deliver industry-leading performance, quality, yield, and reliability.

You will drive innovation across advanced packaging technologies including 2.5D, 3DIC, hybrid bonding, wafer-level and panel-level packaging, advanced interconnect solutions, and emerging assembly platforms. You will lead process development from concept through technology qualification, productization, and high-volume manufacturing transfer while ensuring alignment with Technology Development business processes.

In this role, you will develop organizational capability through leadership coaching, talent development, succession planning, and strategic workforce planning. You will establish performance metrics, drive continuous improvement initiatives, and provide organizational leadership in developing department strategies, technology roadmaps, and cross-functional execution plans.

Responsibilities include, but are not limited to, the following:

Technology Strategy and Roadmap Development

  • Develop and communicate advanced packaging process technology strategies aligned with Micron's long-term product and technology roadmap.

  • Define process development roadmaps for next-generation packaging platforms.

  • Evaluate emerging assembly technologies, materials, and equipment solutions to identify future competitive advantages.

  • Understand market, product, and technology trends and translate them into executable development strategies.

  • Influence organizational investment decisions through data-driven technical recommendations.

Process Technology Leadership

  • Establish strategic and long-term technical objectives for assembly process development organizations.

  • Drive innovation in die attach , hybrid bonding, thermocompression bonding, molding, underfill, substrate assembly, singulation, and related packaging processes.

  • Lead development of scalable manufacturing solutions for advanced packaging technologies.

  • Champion process architecture decisions balancing performance, manufacturability, cost, reliability, and cycle time.

  • Drive breakthrough solutions for challenging yield, reliability, and process integration problems.

Product Development and Manufacturing Readiness

  • Lead process development activities from concept through qualification and manufacturing transfer.

  • Ensure development projects meet technology milestones, quality expectations, and business objectives .

  • Drive manufacturability improvements through process optimization and design enablement.

  • Ensure process capability, yield targets, cost objectives , and reliability requirements are achieved.

  • Establish development methodologies, best practices, and qualification strategies.

Cross-Functional Leadership

  • Represent APTD Process Development in company-wide technical and business initiatives.

  • Partner with Process Integration, Design, Product Engineering, Reliability, Manufacturing, Procurement, Quality, and Supply Chain organizations.

  • Develop strategic partnerships with equipment and material suppliers.

  • Lead cross-functional teams in resolving complex technical and operational challenges.

  • Influence internal and external stakeholders without direct authority.

Organizational Leadership

  • Build and develop a high-performing technical organization.

  • Create a culture focused on innovation, collaboration, accountability, and continuous improvement.

  • Establish succession plans and organizational capability roadmaps.

  • Mentor managers, technical leaders, and emerging talent .

  • Drive employee engagement, inclusion, and leadership development initiatives.

Business Impact and Execution

  • Ensure critical deliverables, milestones, and business objectives are achieved.

  • Guide effective utilization of personnel, capital, and equipment resources.

Continuous Improvement and Growth

  • Apply Artificial Intelligence to p romote adoption of new technologies , data science methodologies, and advanced analytics.

Qualifications and Skills

Experience

  • Minimum 1 2 years of semiconductor industry experience with at least 5 years in advanced packaging technology development.

  • Minimum 5 years of leadership experience managing managers and/or large technical organizations.

  • Demonstrated success developing and qualifying advanced packaging technologies from concept to manufacturing.

  • Experience working with global teams and external technology partners.

Technical Expertise

  • Strong knowledge in one or more of the following areas: 2.5D and 3D packaging technologies; hybrid bonding (D2W, W2W, C2W); HBM and heterogeneous integration; advanced assembly processes and materials; wafer-level packaging and fan-out technologies; reliability engineering and qualification methodologies; semiconductor manufacturing equipment and process control; yield improvement and failure analysis; and process characterization, DOE, and statistical methodologies.

Data and Analytical Skills

  • Excellent data extraction, analytics, and visualization skills.

  • Experience with JMP, Python, R, SQL, or equivalent analytical tools.

  • Strong understanding of process capability, statistical process control, and experimental design.

  • Ability to translate data into actionable technical and business decisions.

Leadership Competencies

  • Proven ability to lead large and diverse organizations.

  • Strong strategic thinking and technology vision.

  • Excellent stakeholder management and influencing skills.

  • Ability to navigate ambiguity and drive execution under tight timelines.

  • Outstanding verbal, written, and executive presentation skills.

  • Strong customer and product focus.

  • Demonstrated ability to build effective partnerships across global organizations.

Education

  • MS or Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Materials Science, Chemical Engineering, Physics, Chemistry, Computer Engineering, or a related technical field.
