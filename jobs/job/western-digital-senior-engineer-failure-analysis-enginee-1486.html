Job Description

ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:

  • Conduct and supervise Failure Analysis (FA) activities for Production and New Product Introduction (NPI) samples, including trend analysis, reporting, and defect characterization.
  • Manage and mentor a team of technicians for media FA analysis and defect characterization using first level FA equipment.
  • Coordinate second-level analysis with analytical lab teams.
  • Generate and communicate comprehensive FA findings to stakeholders, including quarterly defect pareto reports
  • Collaborate with Yield/Defect Engineering, Process Teams, QC Teams, and Analytical Teams to drive yield and quality improvements
  • Participate in cross-functional meetings and global FA initiatives to share knowledge and best practices, including FA Database
  • Drive defect unification initiatives and FA methodology improvements
  • Maintain & update defect library when necessary.
  • Carry out simulations & experiments to understand the mechanism of defect formation.

Qualifications

Requirement:

  • Minimum Bachelor Degree in Science, Engineering or related technical field
  • 2+ years of experience 

Preferred :

  • Strong problem-solving and analytical thinking capabilities
  • Excellent communication skills for cross-functional collaboration
  • Ability to work independently and manage multiple priorities
  • Detail-oriented with strong documentation and reporting skills
  • Ability to conduct experiments and simulations for defect mechanism understanding

Skills :

  • Proficiency with FA equipment including Optical Microscopy (OM), Atomic Force Microscopy (AFM), Zygo, and OSA
  • Experience with analytical tools such as SEM-EDX, FIB-SEM, Raman spectroscopy, ESCA, and TOF-SIMS
  • Strong understanding of defect characterization techniques and failure mode analysis
  • Knowledge of statistical analysis and data interpretation methods
  • Experience with Test Database systems and data visualization tools (Spotfire)
  • Familiarity with FA database systems and IT enhancement projects
