Minimum Qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s degree, master or PhD in Electronics, Mechatronics, Robotics Engineering, Nanotechnology or similar.
  • Graduated within the last 2 years from a bachelor’s degree, master’s degree, or PhD program.
  • Strong knowledge of electronics circuit concepts.
  • Experience using electronic circuits simulation and design tools
  • Effective interpersonal and problem-solving abilities.
  • English language communication skills (conversational level is a must).
  • A self-motivated, enthusiastic team player who enjoys working with others
  • Passionate about Technology, willing to learn and develop into the semiconductor field.
  • Applying AI for automation of technical and non - technical process is required.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Participated in internships or school projects related to pre-silicon circuit design.
  • Knowledge of Pre-silicon development areas such as logic circuit design and computer architecture.
  • Have concepts of RTL, verification and logic simulation basics
  • Organized and goal focused thinking.
  • Experience with programming, scripting and hardware description languages such as C++/Phyton, Verilog.
  • Attitude for attaining problem-solving and debug skills in logic verification.

Role and Responsibilities

  • Provide verification support to design circuits by simulating, analyzing and debugging pre-silicon full chip designs.
  • Develop and maintain test benches and test cases to increase the coverage of HBM and emerging memory architectures and features.
  • Participate in developing verification methodologies and test plans for advanced HBM and emerging memory products.
