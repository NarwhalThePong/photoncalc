Role Overview

The Lead Analog IC Design Engineer is responsible for designing and developing analog and mixed-signal circuits- from initial concept and specification through final verification- to ensure conformance with customer requirements.

 

Required Qualifications

• At least three years of experience designing and developing CMOS SerDes or high-speed I/O integrated circuits.

• Design experience with most of the following SerDes circuit blocks: Transmitters and receivers, serializers and deserializers, phase interpolators, low-jitter PLLs, high-speed clock-distribution circuits, bias circuits, and voltage regulators.

• Good understanding of jitter and signal-equalization techniques.

• Proficiency with CAD tools for circuit simulation, layout, and physical verification.

• Master’s degree or Ph.D. in electrical engineering.

• Strong problem-solving skills and analog design aptitude.

• Clear written and verbal communication skills.

• Ability to collaborate effectively in a team environment.

 

Preferred Qualifications

• Working knowledge of common SerDes standards and their electrical requirements.

• Experience with Cadence design tools and laboratory testing.

• Design experience at data rates above 10 Gbps and in process technologies below 28 nm.
