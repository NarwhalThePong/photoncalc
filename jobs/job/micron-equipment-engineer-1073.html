Equipment Engineer

Micron Idaho Semiconductor Manufacturing (Triton) LLC


Enables the development of best-in-class memory technology. Utilize fundamental equipment knowledge to characterize and drive continuous improvement projects on existing wet process tools and next generation equipment. Success is derived through partnership with the process development team and effective project management of vendor resources to achieve process technology and efficiency benchmarks. Utilize creative experimental techniques to tackle unique technical challenges associated with Process and Hardware issues. Drive and manage vendor execution to meet goals. Provide hardware technical support to the Wet Process area. Complete project milestones - close hardware gaps before transferring to manufacturing. Partner with process engineering to define hardware roadmaps. This includes wafer processing hardware, diagnostics, data collection requirements, and hardware quality control/variation reduction. Establish productive Vendor relations to maintain Supplier engagement to achieve Best in Class Equipment Metrics. Domestic and international travel required.


 Employer will accept a Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Materials Engineering, Engineering or a related field and 2 years of experience in the job offered or in a related occupation.


Position requires:

1. Semi-conductor equipment troubleshooting and managing a set of tools

2. Model-based problem-solving including G8D methodology

3. Supplier management & maintaining key performing Indices

4. Plan tool install and de-install requirements in collaboration with other stakeholders

5. Equipment improvement project management and coordination
