Senior System Validation Engineer Astera Labs is seeking a motivated Senior System Validation Engineer to join our growing post-silicon validation team in Israel and support next-generation connectivity products for AI and data-center applications. In this role, you will perform system-level post-silicon validation, characterization, and debug across silicon, hardware, firmware, and software. You will support silicon bring-up and PVT/corner testing, develop and execute functional and system validation tests, analyze results, and work with cross-functional teams to identify and resolve complex system issues. This is a highly hands-on lab position. The primary location is Haifa, with temporary support at the Ramat Gan lab as needed.   Key Responsibilities:
  • Develop and execute post-silicon functional and system validation from silicon bring-up through production readiness.
  • Run PVT, silicon corner, stress, characterization, and qualification testing across a wide range of operating conditions.
  • Develop validation tests to verify functionality, performance, stability, power, and operating margins.
  • Debug issues across silicon, hardware, firmware, drivers, and system software.
  • Validate high-speed interfaces such as PCIe, CXL, Ethernet/SerDes, and DDR, as well as common system interfaces.
  • Develop Python scripts and automation to improve validation coverage, regression, and test efficiency.
  • Analyze validation data, identify issues and trends, and drive problems to root cause and closure.
  • Work closely with hardware, architecture, firmware, software, design, and DV teams to investigate and resolve issues.
  • Support the transition of new silicon from initial bring-up to a qualified, production-ready product.
  • Operate system platforms and laboratory equipment to support validation and debug.
  • Document test results, issues, root cause, and recommendations and communicate findings clearly.
Basic Qualifications:
  • B.Sc. or M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
  • 6+ years of experience in post-silicon, system, hardware, or product validation.
  • Hands-on experience with SoC/silicon bring-up, functional validation, and system-level debug.
  • Experience developing and executing validation plans, test cases, and characterization experiments.
  • Strong Python scripting and test automation skills.
  • Strong hardware debugging, analytical, and problem-solving skills with the ability to work independently.
  • Experience debugging issues across hardware, firmware, and software.
  • Experience validating high-speed interfaces such as PCIe, Ethernet, SerDes, CXL, or DDR.
  • Hands-on experience working in a hardware or system validation laboratory.
  • Strong communication and cross-functional collaboration skills.
Preferred Qualifications:
  • Experience with PVT validation, silicon corner testing, characterization, qualification, and margin analysis.
  • Experience with server, storage, networking, or AI infrastructure products.
  • Familiarity with PCIe/CXL architecture and high-speed SerDes technologies.
  • Experience with oscilloscopes, BERTs, protocol analyzers, traffic generators, or similar lab equipment.
  • Working knowledge of Python, firmware, Linux, and system-level software debugging.
  • Experience developing automated validation and regression environments.
  • Familiarity with Git and software development practices.
  • Experience with pre-silicon validation, emulation, or hardware prototyping.
  • Experience with system performance, throughput, latency, power, and stability testing.
