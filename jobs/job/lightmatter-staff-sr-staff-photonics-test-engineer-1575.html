About this Role

You will be focused on architecting wafer, package and system level characterization and test strategies of Lightmatter’s novel Silicon Photonic product line. As part of this role you will have the ability to directly influence the design and development of Lightmatter silicon photonics technologies. You will be part of a growing interdisciplinary team with a lot of opportunities to learn and make a big impact.

 

Responsibilities

  • Work directly with the architecture and design teams to understand the technology and conceptualize characterization and test techniques targeting feedback for design model validations.
  • Architect test concepts, test platforms, design for test rules, and provide feedback early on during the technology concept phase.
  • Create detailed test plan documentation and ensure timely execution.
  • Define, develop, and setup of wafer, package and system level tests for Lightmatter silicon photonics products. 
  • Develop test software to enable test execution, data analysis, and parameter extraction.
  • Work with vendors to specify and procure test equipment.
  • Setup and operate a range of optical equipment such as tunable lasers, power meters, polarization synthesizer, OBR/OVA, and high-speed test equipment.
  • Engage with silicon and platform validation teams and bridge any gaps between test and validation teams.
  • Work with OSAT teams to define, validate, and refine test techniques for high volume manufacturing.
  • Work with the reliability team for pre and post-stress test needs.
  • Keep up to date with the latest developments in Silicon Photonics technology and testability.

 

Requirements:

  • Master’s in Photonics, Physics, Electrical Engineering or a related field
  • A minimum of 6+ years of proven experience in silicon photonics test development 
  • Proven industrial experience in supporting new product introduction and volume production  
  • Understanding of Silicon Photonics components and circuits
  • Experience working with design teams to define test cells for model feedback and validation 
  • Hands on experience in characterization and test of photonics components and circuits 
  • Direct experience in high-speed photonic device test and familiar with test equipment such as AWG, DCA, DSO, PNA, LCA
  • Experience in test system automation using Python, C#, or other languages
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills to identify and solve issues during the testing process
  • Ability to convey complex technical concepts to both technical and non-technical stakeholders 

 

Preferred Qualifications:

  • PhD in Photonics, Physics, Electrical Engineering or a related field
  • 2+ years of photonic component or circuit design experience 
  • Exposure to system link bring-up
