Job Description:

Role Overview

As a Board Validation Engineer, you will validate next-generation products against rigorous technical requirements, ensuring maximum reliability and performance. You will design, develop, and implement end-to-end validation procedures, perform deep signal analysis, and execute standardized compliance tests. Your primary focus areas will include Power, Thermal, Timing, High-Speed Digital Design, and Host/Target interfaces.


Key Responsibilities

  • Drive Strategy: Own the hardware verification strategy and drive end-to-end test execution across a diverse portfolio, including storage controllers, network cards, switch devices, and AI accelerators.
  • Mitigate Risk: Analyze performance data to identify product risks, communicating findings clearly and proactively to cross-functional engineering teams.
  • Document Results: Author comprehensive test reports with precise documentation of results, deviations, and failure analysis.
  • Advance Automation: Identify, architect, and implement automation frameworks to streamline test execution, data collection, and reporting processes.

Required Qualifications

  • Education: Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience.
  • Experience: 8+ years of hands-on experience in hardware testing (including test methodologies, planning, execution, and reporting) or board-level design.
  • Lab Equipment: Proficiency with laboratory tools, including oscilloscopes, digital multi-meters, thermal chambers, and soldering tools.
  • Schematics: Proven ability to read and interpret complex board schematics and utilize PCB Viewer software tools.

Technical Skills & Tools

  • Protocols: Deep familiarity with common bus protocols (e.g., DDR4, PCIe, UART, I2C, SPI).
  • Environments: Competency working across both Windows and Linux operating systems.
  • Software & AI: Experience utilizing Python for test scripting, GitHub for version control, and modern AI developer tools like Claude Code to accelerate workflow automation.
