Lead Quantum Engineer, Systems Performance

Rigetti · Berkeley, CA · posted 2026-08-19

Rigetti is seeking a Lead Quantum Engineer for the Systems Performance team. 

In this position you will play a critical role in shaping our technical roadmap. You will lead a cross-functional team focused on building the next generation of Rigetti’s quantum hardware, and take a high-ownership role in delivering the technologies required to unlock new levels of performance at scale. 

In your daily work, you will propose new ideas to push the performance and scalability of superconducting qubit systems, design and execute experiments to test those ideas, and lead a diverse set of engineers and scientists to identify and mitigate key performance gaps. 

The right candidate will have a demonstrated track record of scientific success across multiple institutions, technical leadership experience, a high-ownership mentality, and strong organization and communication skills.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, build, and lead cross-functional experimental programs to inform the technical roadmap and drive systems-level improvements.

  • Propose and explore new technical directions. 

  • Identify and address complex gaps in existing technology.

  • Own the delivery of key quantum hardware technologies.

About You

  • PhD in Physics, Applied Physics, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.

  • Prior technical role in an industry or start-up environment.

  • Demonstrated technical leadership experience.

  • Direct experience with cryogenic systems and RF measurement techniques

  • Extensive hands-on experience with time-domain measurement, and superconducting quantum devices.

  • Track record of meaningful accomplishments in quantum science or related fields.

  • Working knowledge of Python.

  • Enjoys teamwork and inter-team collaboration.

  • Excellent communication skills.
