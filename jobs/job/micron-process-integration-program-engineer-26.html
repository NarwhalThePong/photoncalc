Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

We are excited to develop next-generation memory technologies that power the world's most advanced computing solutions. Our team works at the intersection of engineering, manufacturing, and business planning to bring innovative DRAM and NAND products from concept to high-volume production. We value collaboration, continuous improvement, and a commitment to operational excellence. As an ID1 Process Integration Program Engineer, you will play a key role in managing part type introductions, wafer resource planning, and yield performance throughout the product lifecycle. You will partner with cross-functional teams to ensure successful new product introductions, seamless production transitions, and optimized manufacturing performance. This is an excellent opportunity for an engineer who enjoys driving coordination, solving complex challenges, and influencing product success. Responsibilities:
  • Manage system setup, traveler creation, and pilot scheduling for new product introductions
  • Coordinate startup planning activities, including risk assessments and resource alignment
  • Support yield curve projections and performance tracking to enable business planning and forecasting
  • Oversee sampling plans, attribute management, SWR processing, and hold management activities
  • Partner with planning, operations, and engineering teams to manage wafer, reticle, and priority lot requirements
Minimum Qualifications:
  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Materials Science, Physics, or a related engineering field
  • Experience in semiconductor manufacturing, product introduction, PTO, NPI, or related engineering disciplines
  • Knowledge of DRAM and/or NAND technologies
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and cross-functional collaboration skills
Preferred Qualifications:
  • Experience in PTO (Part Type Ownership) or New Product Introduction within a DRAM or NAND manufacturing environment
  • Experience coordinating wafer planning, yield performance monitoring, and product lifecycle transitions
  • Familiarity with manufacturing systems, attribute management, and reticle tracking processes
  • Experience collaborating across engineering, planning, and operations organizations in a high-volume manufacturing environment
  • Experience using AI-assisted analytics to enhance data interpretation, anomaly detection, and yield improvement initiatives
