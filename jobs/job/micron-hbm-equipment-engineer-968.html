HBM EQUIPMENT ENGINEER

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

Responsibilities and Tasks: 

  • Evaluate and Improve Assigned Assembly Manufacturing Processes, Materials, and/or Equipment. 
  • Maintain Equipment knowledge as it relates to the process. 
  • Improve utilization/capacity/cost by machine upgrade, procedure optimization, or material selection. 
  • Optimize and identify equipment variables. 
  • Understand DOE/FMEA/OCAP/8D/SPC/Repair/PM (preventive) techniques 
  • Perform containment/prevention actions of process and equipment deviations 
  • Prepare and update equipment operation manuals, maintenance logs, and incident reports. 
  • Support the introduction of new equipment and technologies, including validation and evaluation. 
  • Design equipment components (Improve equipment capability by exploring new ideas/concepts and implement on equipment) 
  • Coordinate with vendors for equipment upgrades, repairs, and technical support. 
  • Conduct regular equipment performance analysis and propose improvements by using and applying AI. 
  • Locate and research technical information on equipment, materials, and/or processes in journals, literature, web, etc.

Requirements 

  • Bachelor’s degree or above in Electrical, Mechanical, Materials, Electronics Engineering, or related fields. 
  • Familiarity with semiconductor equipment operation and maintenance procedures and work experience: 3 years above 
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. 
  • Good teamwork and communication abilities. 
  • Ability to handle the wafer level molding, dicing, thinning, mount and debond. 
  • Ability to handle the FDC/Tool alarm analysis and improve is prefer 
  • Understanding the tool signal is preferable, such as SVID, CEID, ECID...etc. 
  • Understand the tool installation, setup and buyoff procedure 
  • English: ability to communicate for discussion with supplier and global team (TOEIC 600 above)."
