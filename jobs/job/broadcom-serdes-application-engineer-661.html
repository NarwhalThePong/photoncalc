Job Description:

SerDes Applications Engineer

In June of 2025, Broadcom announced the Tomahawk® 6 switch series of devices delivering a world’s first 102.4 Terabits/sec of switching capacity in a single chip – doubling the bandwidth of any Ethernet switch available on the market at the time. With unprecedented scale, energy efficiency, and AI-optimized features, Tomahawk 6 was built to power the next generation of scale-up and scale-out AI networks, delivering unmatched flexibility with support for 100G/200G SerDes and co-packaged optics (CPO). Broadcom's Core Switching Group (CSG) is looking for a SerDes Applications Engineer with focused expertise in serial IO technology who can help support some of it's largest Tier 1 customers operating at the bleeding edge including 200Gbps per lane PAM4 Ethernet interfaces and 64 GT/s PCIe Gen6 interfaces. 

Main Job Tasks and Responsibilities
------------------------------------
As a SerDes Applications Engineer (AE), you will be the owner of all technical aspects relating to the SerDes interfaces of Broadcom CSG products. These products span a range of markets including Service Provider, Data Center and Enterprise. Key responsibilities include the handling of all aspects of technical support pertinent to the high speed serial IO interfaces of CSG products. These responsibilities include:

- Support of architecture and low-level hardware questions and issues
- Answer field and customer questions in a timely manner
- Replicate field issues in the lab using reference designs and test equipment
- Troubleshoot challenging hardware issues associated with high speed serial interfaces
- Perform customer demos and presentations upon request
- Develop and maintain documentation and supporting collateral 
- Provide expert level support to the Field Applications Engineering organization supporting customers globally
- Understand the SerDes architecture of the products thoroughly
- Understand detailed datapath flows through the MAC, PCS, and PMD of various Ethernet Serdes Port Macros 
- Interface to the engineering, software development, architecture, and marketing teams when developing collateral and training materials for CSG products
- Debug SerDes software driver bugs


Minimum Position Required Qualifications
----------------------------------------
- B.S./M.S. Electrical/Computer Engineering (or similar degrees)
- 8+ years of experience working with high speed SerDes and Analog IP
- Good understanding of high speed SerDes architecture
- Hands on experience with high speed debug and characterization
- Must have strong debug and problem-solving skills
- Experience with script-based test automation and strong scripting skills in general
- Strong understanding of channel loss, jitter, and typical impairments encountered in high speed serial IO designs

Preferred Qualifications
- Experience working with PCIe including Gen6
- Experience working with 100/200 Gbps per lane Ethernet interfaces
- Hands on Debug experience with high speed oscilloscopes, BERTs
- Experience in test and screening methodology for High-Speed interfaces
- Prior experience with support of high speed serial IO interfaces
- Prior experience supporting Ethernet and or PCIe interfaces
