About IonQ: 

IonQ, Inc . [NYSE: IONQ] is the world’s leading quantum platform and merchant supplier - delivering integrated quantum solutions across computing, networking, sensing, and security. IonQ’s newest generation of quantum computers, the IonQ Tempo, is the latest in a line of cutting-edge systems that have been helping customers and partners including Amazon Web Services, and AstraZeneca achieve 20x performance results and accelerate innovation in drug discovery, materials science, financial modeling, logistics, cybersecurity, and defense. In 2025, the company achieved 99.99% two-qubit gate fidelity, setting a world record in quantum computing performance .

Headquartered in College Park, Maryland, IonQ has operations in California, Colorado, Massachusetts, Tennessee, Washington, Italy, South Korea, Sweden, Switzerland, Canada, and the United Kingdom. Our quantum computing services are available through all major cloud providers, while we also meet the needs of networking and sensing customers across land, sea, air, and space. IonQ is making quantum platforms more accessible and impactful than ever before.  

What to Expect

We are seeking a Senior Cryogenic Engineer to support and develop advanced cryogenic platforms for our SNSPD (Superconducting Nanowire Single-Photon Detector) product line, with a strong emphasis on next-generation rack-mountable systems.

You will join a cross-functional team dedicated to advancing world-class quantum technologies that address some of the most complex challenges in computing, networking, and sensing. In this role, you will play a central part in the development lifecycle, overseeing cryogenic subsystems and ensuring robust, reliable 2K cryo-systems that can be deployed across internal and customer environments, while remaining maintainable and adaptable as the technology evolves.

What You'll be Doing

Working at the intersection of engineering and innovation, you will lead system-level specification, integration, and testing of cryogenic platforms tailored to SNSPD requirements. You will collaborate closely with internal teams, subcontractors, and external partners to design, configure, and improve deployable systems, while also providing operational support for existing platforms.

What you'll Bring

This position suits a highly experienced engineer with a strong background in cryogenic systems, hands-on expertise with cryostats and related technologies, and the ability to drive integration efforts across multidisciplinary teams and external vendors.

You'll be a great fit with:

  • Master’s degree (PhD preferred) in applied physics, system engineering, mechanical engineering, or a related field, with 5-7+ years of experience in the cryogenic industry
  • Proven experience designing, evaluating, and maintaining complex cryogenic systems, including dilution, sorption, and 1 K cryostats
  • Strong knowledge of cryogenic wiring, sensors, electronics, and CAD-based design and manufacturing processes
  • Experience collaborating with cryogenic vendors, subcontractors, and external design partners on system integration
  • Desirable expertise in fibre or free-space optics, superconducting nanowire single-photon detectors, and leading integration projects
