Job Description:

Job Description Summary – PCD Validation Engineer

An exciting position in the explosive growth area of Data Center Enterprise Storage responsible for providing low power, high performance analog and mixed signal products. In this role you will be responsible for developing the most advanced semiconductor devices to support high capacity storage needs across all the top tier service providers. You will work with highly talented teams to meet the company business goals while growing your skills.

Detailed Job Description

The candidate will be involved in IC product validation which could include one or more of the following areas:

  • Full chip Mixed Signal validation

  • Product validation planning and execution

  • Design and debug of test and measurement setup, which includes hardware, software, electronic circuits and systems

  • Validation review with detailed analysis of validation results leading to improvements in product design as well as test methodology

Requirements

  • Bachelor of Science degree in Electrical Engineering (or related field) +5 years of analog/mixed signal IC design, verification, and validation experience or

  • Master of Science degree in Electrical Engineering (or related field) +3 years of analog/mixed signal IC design, verification, and validation experience

  • Proficiency in English
