Senior Materials Engineer / Scientist

Micron · Boise, ID - Main Site · posted 2026-10-08

Apply on Micron's site →

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

As a Senior Materials Engineer / Scientist in Advanced Packaging Technology Development (APTD), you will help define and enable the materials foundation for Micron's next-generation memory and advanced packaging technologies, including HBM, 3D memory, and heterogeneous integration. You will find, develop, characterize, qualify, and integrate new materials that make new product architectures possible. You'll also improve performance, reliability, manufacturability, scalability, and cost.


You will work with materials science, process development, integration, product, manufacturing, quality, and supplier teams to turn scientific insight into technology impact. We're looking for people who question assumptions, look beyond their own field, and stay with hard, ambiguous problems until they're solved. Direct semiconductor experience is valuable but not required. We encourage candidates from other materials-intensive industries and research institutions to apply.


Responsibilities

  • Develop, characterize, qualify, and integrate advanced materials for next-generation memory and packaging technologies.
  • Form hypotheses, design and run rigorous experiments, analyze data, and use the results to guide materials and technology decisions.
  • Investigate materials behavior, including interfaces, adhesion, mechanical response, thermal performance, chemical compatibility, reliability, and process interactions.
  • Drive materials development in areas such as: Polymer composites and encapsulation materials, such as NCF, MUF, mold compounds, underfills, and die-attach materials. Temporary bonding and release materials, carrier systems, back-grind and dicing tapes, and other materials for thin-wafer handling. Dielectrics, films and coatings, adhesion and surface preparation materials, thermal materials, and substrate or RDL materials. Emerging material platforms for HBM, 3D memory, and heterogeneous integration.
  • Constructively challenge existing assumptions, specifications, and practices when data supports a better approach.
  • Partner with process, integration, product, manufacturing, quality, equipment, and supplier teams to move materials from concept through qualification and manufacturing readiness.
  • Work with suppliers, universities, research organizations, and other external partners to evaluate new materials technologies and speed up their development.
  • Use modeling, simulation, data analytics, and AI tools to speed up materials development and problem solving.
  • Contribute invention disclosures, patents, technical reports, and publications where appropriate.

Basic Qualifications

  • MS or PhD in Materials Science and Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Chemistry, Polymer Science, Physics, Mechanical Engineering with a strong materials focus, or a closely related field.
  • Professional or research experience developing, characterizing, qualifying, integrating, or scaling advanced materials technologies.
  • Strong materials science fundamentals, including structure–property–process–performance relationships.
  • Proven ability to solve complex, multidisciplinary technical problems through experimentation, analysis, and structured problem solving.
  • Clear technical communication and the ability to collaborate across disciplines, organizations, suppliers, and geographies.
  • Ability to work independently, build alignment, and deliver results as part of a global team.
  • Final level will be based on technical depth, demonstrated impact, scope, independence, and relevant experience.

Preferred Qualifications

  • PhD in a relevant technical discipline.
  • Experience taking a new material, product, process, or manufacturing capability from concept through implementation.
  • Documented innovation, such as patents, invention disclosures, or publications.
  • Experience working with materials suppliers, universities, research labs, startups, or industry consortia.
  • Background in semiconductors, microelectronics, advanced packaging, aerospace, photonics, energy, specialty chemicals, composites, or another technology-intensive materials field.
  • Hands-on experience with polymer encapsulation, temporary bonding/debonding, thin-wafer handling, or thermal and interface materials.
  • Experience using AI-enabled tools, modeling, or data analytics to improve technical learning and decision quality.
