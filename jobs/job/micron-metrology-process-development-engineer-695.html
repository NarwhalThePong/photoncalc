Position Summary

As a Metrology Process Development Engineer, you will play a key role in developing and deploying advanced metrology solutions that enable next-generation DRAM and HBM technologies.

In this role, you will drive metrology recipe development, data analysis, equipment evaluation, qualification, and startup activities. You will also collaborate with equipment suppliers to develop and implement innovative metrology solutions that support Micron's cutting-edge memory technologies.

Previous semiconductor industry experience is not required. Candidates with strong analytical and problem-solving skills gained through manufacturing or other technical environments are encouraged to apply. This position offers a unique opportunity to work with global engineering teams, contribute to world-class memory technology development, and build expertise in advanced semiconductor processes.


Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and qualify advanced metrology methodologies and measurement recipes for next-generation DRAM and HBM technologies.
  • Analyze measurement data and lead problem-solving activities to improve metrology capability, accuracy, and process control.
  • Evaluate, qualify, and deploy new metrology tools and measurement technologies.
  • Lead metrology equipment development projects, including requirement definition, supplier engagement, tool development, startup, and implementation.
  • Define process control strategies and establish monitoring plans for critical manufacturing processes.
  • Utilize experimental and production data to support process optimization and yield improvement initiatives.
  • Collaborate with Process Integration, Manufacturing, Yield Enhancement, Equipment Engineering, and other cross-functional teams.
  • Drive technical reviews and development activities with metrology equipment suppliers.
  • Support capital planning, equipment installation, startup, qualification, and validation activities.
  • Leverage AI-driven tools and technologies to improve productivity, enhance decision-making, and optimize engineering workflows.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree or higher in Electrical Engineering, Electronics, Materials Science, Physics, Chemistry, Chemical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or a related technical field.
  • Experience in manufacturing, engineering, or other technical roles.
  • Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to analyze data, identify root causes, and develop effective solutions.
  • Business-level Japanese communication skills (equivalent to JLPT N2 or above).
  • Ability to communicate effectively in English within a global work environment.
  • Experience using or a willingness to utilize AI tools (e.g., Anthoropic Claude、Microsoft 365 Copilot) to improve productivity and solve technical challenges.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in the semiconductor industry.
  • Experience in manufacturing industries such as automotive, chemical, precision equipment, or electronics.
  • Knowledge of metrology systems, analytical instruments, or manufacturing equipment.
  • Experience with or knowledge of XRD, SIMS, OCD, Scatterometry, CD-SEM, Film Metrology, or related measurement technologies.
  • Experience with statistical analysis and process control methodologies.
  • Interest in advanced memory technologies, including DRAM, HBM, and NAND.
  • Experience working with global teams or international projects.

What We Are Looking For

  • A strong analytical thinker who can effectively solve complex technical problems.
  • A self-motivated professional who takes ownership and drives results independently.
  • A collaborative team player who thrives in a cross-functional and multicultural environment.
  • An individual who embraces innovation and actively leverages emerging technologies, including AI.
  • Someone eager to grow and build a career in a global organization.
  • A passionate engineer who is excited to contribute to world-leading semiconductor technology development.

Why Join This Role

  • Contribute directly to the development of industry-leading DRAM and HBM technologies.
  • Be part of a global headquarters organization while based at Micron's Fab15 site.
  • Work closely with engineering teams around the world and gain extensive global exposure.
  • Participate in technical exchanges and international business travel opportunities with Micron sites worldwide.
  • Develop expertise in advanced metrology, data analytics, process development, and AI-enabled engineering solutions.
  • Play a critical role in enabling the next generation of memory technologies that power the future of computing.
