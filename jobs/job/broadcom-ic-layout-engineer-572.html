Job Description:

Role Overview Responsible for executing high-quality layouts across Standard Cells, I/O pads, and Mixed-Signal IP in advanced processes within project schedules. Key Responsibilities
  • Layout Design & Execution: Apply design guidelines and advanced process rules to deliver high-performance, robust layouts.
  • Planning & Floorplanning: Manage schedule and execute layouts from the transistor level up including cell- and block-level floorplanning.
  • Collaboration: Adopt new methodologies, communicate actively across sites, and work closely with senior layout engineers.
Skill Set & Requirements
  • Education & Experience:
    • Bachelor’s Degree in Electronic Engineering.
    • 2 to 3 years of hands-on IC layout experience
  • Technical Skills:
    • Solid grasp of Standard Cell, Memory, or Analog/Mixed-Signal layout basics.
    • Familiarity with basic ESD/Latch-up layout techniques and transistor-level placement.
    • Practical experience with physical verification: DRC, LVS, ERC, and Antenna checks.
    • Basic understanding or exposure to sub-micron or advanced process nodes ( e.g. 5nm, 3nm, 2nm, 1.4nm ) .
  • Tools & Concepts:
    • Proficient with Cadence Virtuoso layout tool and Calibre physical verification (DRC, LVS, ERC, Antenna, and EM/IR analysis.)
    • Scripting knowledge (Shell, Python, Tcl) or Cadence SKILL programming in a plus.
  • Personal Attributes: Quick learner, proactive, team player, with good communication and strong problem-solving skills.
