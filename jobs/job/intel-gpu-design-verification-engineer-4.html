The Role and Impact
As a GPU Design Verification Engineer, you will contribute to the development of next-generation graphics components, ensuring their functionality meets rigorous specifications. You will play a pivotal role in implementing verification strategies, debugging issues, and collaborating with cross-functional teams to deliver industry-leading Graphics IP solutions. Your work will drive innovation across integrated, discrete, and data center AI applications, shaping Intel's leadership in the GPU space.

Business Group
The Graphics IP team within Intel's Data Center Group (DCG) is dedicated to delivering cutting-edge GPU technologies that support high-performance computing and AI solutions. We focus on continuous innovation, designing scalable and reusable hardware solutions used by millions globally. By collaborating across Intel, we ensure our efforts align with the company's broader goals of advancing graphics and compute capabilities.

Key Responsibilities
- Define and execute scalable IP verification plans, test benches, and validation methodologies to meet graphics microarchitecture specifications.
- Debug and resolve failing tests by identifying issues, performing root cause analysis, and implementing corrective measures in pre-silicon environments.
- Execute system simulations and analyze power, timing, and functional coverage metrics.
- Collaborate with architecture, design, and physical teams to optimize functional, performance, and power goals.
- Independently develop verification environments and ensure functional coverage closure.
- Create technical documentation, test plans, and drive reviews to ensure alignment with architectural objectives.
- Maintain and enhance verification infrastructure and methodologies for graphics logic components.

Qualifications:

You must possess the below minimum qualifications to be initially considered for this position. Preferred qualifications are in addition to the minimum requirements and are considered a plus factor in identifying top candidates. Experience listed below would be obtained through a combination of your degree, research and or relevant previous job and or internship experiences.

Minimum Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or related field plus 4+ years of relevant experience, or Master's degree with 3+ years of relevant experience, or PhD with no prior professional experience in:
- System Verilog and UVM for creating verification environments.
- Developing feature verification test benches and achieving functional coverage closure.
- Analytical and debugging skills in pre-silicon environments, including issue replication and resolution.
- Scripting languages such as Python.

Preferred Qualifications:
- Knowledge of GPU architecture fundamentals and 3D graphics pipeline concepts.
- Experience with industry-standard frontend design and verification tools and methodologies.
- Exposure to memory subsystem protocols (e.g., CXL, AXI, PCIe).
- Familiarity with assertion-based verification techniques.
- Experience working with emulators or FPGA for pre-silicon validation.
