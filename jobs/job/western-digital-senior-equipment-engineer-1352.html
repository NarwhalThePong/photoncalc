Job Description

  • Responsible for equipment troubleshooting, installation, qualification, modification, automation, and continuous improvement to ensure stable, reliable, and efficient equipment performance.
  • Design and integrate mechanical, electrical, software, AI, and automation solutions to enhance process equipment capability and automation tooling performance.
  • Develop maintenance procedures, technical documentation, safety standards, and technician training, while supporting equipment projects, equipment characterization, and manufacturing capability development.

ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:

  • Support equipment troubleshooting, installation, qualification, modification, and continuous improvement.
  • Develop and implement equipment automation and upgrades, using AI and the latest automation technologies to improve equipment stability, performance, and productivity.
  • Prepare electrical drawings, wiring layouts, software debugging, and control systems for cleaning equipment.
  • Integrate different equipment modules and systems to ensure stable and reliable operation.
  • Design, modify, and assemble mechanical and electrical components to improve equipment performance for square glass, chemical etch, and cleaning processes.
  • Develop maintenance, troubleshooting, and testing procedures for electrical and electronic components.
  • Perform equipment characterization and capability studies to ensure equipment meets safety standards and requirements.
  • Support equipment design, modification, expansion, and automation projects.
  • Prepare and maintain SOPs, safety procedures, environmental requirements, and HIRAC documentation.
  • Provide technical and safety training and establish safety guidelines for technicians.
  • Work closely with process, manufacturing, automation teams, and suppliers to identify problems, develop solutions, and drive equipment improvements.

Qualifications

REQUIRED:

  • Currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics Engineering, Optical Engineering, Automation Engineering, or a related engineering field.
  • Fresh graduates with relevant engineering qualifications are encouraged to apply.

PREFERRED:

  • Strong technical knowledge and hands-on experience in equipment, automation, electrical/mechanical systems, or process equipment.
  • Good problem-solving, troubleshooting, and analytical skills.
  • Experience or strong interest in automation, AI, robotics, and new technologies is an advantage.
