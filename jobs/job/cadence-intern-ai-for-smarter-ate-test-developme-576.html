Intern: AI for Smarter ATE Test Development & Debug

Location: Cadence Design Systems, Sophia Antipolis, France

Duration: 6 months

Profile: Final-year engineering student



About the Internship


Are you curious about Artificial Intelligence and interested in applying it to real semiconductor testing challenges? Do you enjoy working at the intersection of electronics, software and data? If so, we'd love to hear from you.


At Cadence, we believe great candidates come from a variety of backgrounds and experiences. You do not need to be an AI expert or already have experience with semiconductor test equipment to apply. We are looking for motivated and curious individuals who are eager to learn and interested in exploring how AI can improve the way silicon devices are tested and debugged.


During this internship, you will work with the ATE Test team to investigate how AI and automation can improve test development, debugging and engineering workflows. You will work with real semiconductor test methods and results, exploring opportunities to make test development more efficient and help engineers identify issues faster.


You will be supported by experienced test engineers and will gain hands-on exposure to real post-silicon test and validation activities.



What You Will Do


  • Explore how AI can improve ATE test development and debug workflows.
  • Analyse existing test methods, test programs, logs and results to identify opportunities for automation.
  • Develop Python-based tools and AI prototypes to assist engineers during test development and debugging.
  • Investigate methods to automatically detect anomalies or unexpected behaviour in test results.
  • Explore AI-assisted approaches for root cause analysis and troubleshooting.
  • Improve the way test information, debug data and results are presented to engineers.
  • Collaborate with test engineers to validate proposed solutions on real engineering use cases.



Expected Deliverables


By the end of the internship, you will:

  • Develop and demonstrate at least one AI-assisted test or debug prototype.
  • Validate the proposed approach using real semiconductor test cases.
  • Demonstrate how the solution can simplify or accelerate an existing engineering workflow.
  • Document the methodology, implementation, results and recommendations.
  • Present your work to the engineering team.



Success Criteria


Success will be measured by the solution's ability to provide value in one or more of the following areas:

  • Reduced manual effort during test development or debug.
  • Faster identification of test or silicon issues.
  • Improved automation of repetitive engineering activities.
  • More efficient analysis of test logs and results.
  • Improved usability and accessibility of engineering information.
  • Positive feedback from test engineers using the prototype.



Preferred Qualifications


  • Engineering background in electronics, microelectronics, embedded systems, computer science or a related field.
  • Basic programming skills, with Python being a plus.
  • Interest in Artificial Intelligence and data-driven engineering.
  • Strong curiosity and willingness to learn.
  • Problem-solving mindset and attention to detail.
  • Good communication and teamwork skills.



Nice to Have


  • Exposure to semiconductor testing or validation.
  • Basic knowledge of digital or analogue electronics.
  • Experience with Python, data analysis or software development.
  • Interest in Automated Test Equipment (ATE) and silicon validation.



What Matters Most


Curiosity, enthusiasm and a desire to learn. Whether your background is primarily electronics, software, data science or AI, we encourage you to apply.


This is an opportunity to gain hands-on experience with semiconductor testing while exploring how AI can transform traditional test development and debug methodologies.
