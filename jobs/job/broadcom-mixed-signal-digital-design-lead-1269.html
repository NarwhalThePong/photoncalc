Job Description:

We are seeking a  Digital Mixed-Signal Lead with a strong digital design and architecture background to lead the development of digital control infrastructures for advanced clocking sub-systems ( PLLs, FLLs, and DLLs ).

Core Responsibilities

  • Architecture & RTL: Own the architectural definition, RTL implementation, and system-level integration of the digital logic driving clocking IPs.

  • Logic Design: Design complex digital control loops, state machines for adaptive calibration, DVFS (dynamic voltage/frequency scaling) controllers, and fractional-N delta-sigma modulators.

  • Analog Interface: Serve as the primary bridge to Analog Circuit Designers. Translate analog specifications into robust digital architectures and define clean boundaries at the analog-digital interface.

  • Verification Partnership: Partner with Verification Teams to drive Mixed-Signal Verification (MSV) and Real Number Modeling (RNM) strategies for high-coverage loop simulations.

  • Physical Design Collaboration: Work with Physical Design Teams to co-optimize floorplanning and protect sensitive analog nodes from digital switching noise.

  • Sign-off Methodology: Enforce a rigorous full-chip CDC (Clock Domain Crossing) and RDC (Reset Domain Crossing) sign-off methodology for the clocking network.

Candidate Requirements

  • Education: Bachelors degree

  • Experience: 8+ years of professional digital ASIC design experience.

  • RTL Mastery: Deep expertise in writing robust, synthesizable SystemVerilog RTL

  • Process Nodes: Direct experience with advanced technology nodes, specifically N5, N3P, and beyond.

  • Track Record: Proven history of steering cross-functional execution from initial concept through to successful tapeout s.
