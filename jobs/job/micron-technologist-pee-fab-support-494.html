Responsibility:

  • Proactively promote safety priorities and standards

  • Maintain vigilance and bring up workplace safety concerns

  • Proactively supervise the performance of equipment and address any issues in a timely manner

  • Direct shift equipment team in the execution of preventive and corrective maintenance

  • Demonstrate a solid grasp of all Fab Support duties and able to assist in developing the technical skills of your team. Focus on Pumps, Chiller and Abatement systems.

  • Perform administrative and supervisory duties for shift techs: attendance, staffing, and reviews

  • Serve as the main point of contact between the FAB personnel and the Fab Support group regarding Production issues

  • Accept responsibility for all equipment, process, and personnel issues during shift

  • Ensure coverage as needed to maintain shift needs

  • Coordinate shift contribution to TPM activities

  • Foster an environment for other Fab10 employees to resolve problems, give feedback and express concern

  • Assist with equipment installs, tests and commissioning

  • Integrates AI-assisted tools and insights into daily work to improve efficiency, quality, or effectiveness, exercising sound judgment and complying with organizational standards and legal requirements.​

  • Contributes to a culture of continuous improvement by identifying, testing, and sharing AI-enabled enhancements within one’s scope of work.
     

Requirements:

  • A relevant Diploma in any Engineering courses OR equivalent working experience

  • Strong mechanical competence with the ability to understand and repair mechanical systems

  • Vacuum system knowledge and troubleshooting ability

  • Ability to use schematic diagrams to solve systems to components level

  • Intermediate PC skills including a familiarity with MS Office Suite

  • Problem-solving abilities and the capacity to effectively prioritize tasks

  • Time management, organization, and prioritization skills

  • Follows procedures and work with minimal supervision

  • Able to perform 12-hour work shift

  • Good communication and interpersonal skills
