Key Responsibilities

  • Support the reliability stress tests (HAST, Temperature Cycle, HTS, THS, and Moisture Soak) & semiconductor electrical test operations in accordance with process specifications & SOPs
  • Support troubleshooting activities in resolving process or equipment issues with DOE & analysis.
  • Maintain equipment, ensuring readiness, uptime, and adherence to safety standards
  • Support lot movement and ensure smooth process flow across operations to meet TAT requirements
  • Maintain 6S, ESD, and workplace safety compliance in assigned areas
  • Participate in training, continuous improvement, and audit-related activities
  • Coordinate with vendors to ensure timely support for equipment maintenance, calibration, and service activities
  • Assist in material handling and inventory control to ensure availability of required consumables and test materials
  • Support shipping and logistics operations by preparing, packing, documenting, and tracking outgoing lots to ensure timely movement and on-time delivery
  • Leveraging AI, and AI Assistants (e.g., Copilot, LLMs, Agentic AI) to automate reporting, dashboards, and workflow approvals, improving operation & process efficiency
  • Integrate AI solutions with lab systems (Power BI, SharePoint, automation workflows)

Job Requirements

  • Diploma in Engineering (Electrical / Electronics / Mechanical or related)
  • 0–3 years of experience in semiconductor manufacturing, lab, or test environment is preffered
  • Basic knowledge of manufacturing processes, equipment handling, or quality inspection
  • Strong attention to detail with a quality and safety mindset
  • Good communication and teamwork skills
  • Ability to learn quickly, multi-task, and work in a fast-paced environment
  • Able to work on shift when needed
