Head of Photonics

Atom Computing · Boulder, CO · posted 2026-10-08

Apply on Atom Computing's site →

At Atom Computing, we build quantum computers using arrays of optically trapped neutral atoms that will empower customers to achieve unprecedented computational breakthroughs. Join a world-class team of scientists, engineers, and business professionals to advance the state-of-the-art in quantum computing.

We are seeking a Head of Photonics to establish and lead Atom Computing's integrated photonics program. Scaling neutral-atom quantum computers is a problem of controlling light: delivering fast, precise, independent optical control to very large numbers of qubits. Photonic integrated circuits (PICs) are central to our scaling roadmap, and we are investing in them for the long term.

You will own the company's photonics strategy: selecting material platforms and architectures, and leading development programs from concept to hardware running in our quantum computers. You will build an in-house team with design, test, and integration capability, and direct technical collaborations with foundries and photonics companies.

You will work closely with the physicists and engineers who translate qubit requirements into device specifications. Prior experience in atomic physics or quantum computing is not required.

Responsibilities

  • Define and own the integrated photonics roadmap. Assess competing material platforms and architectures against system-level requirements, and make clear, well-reasoned downselect decisions.
  • Lead PIC development programs from specification through design, fabrication, test, packaging, and integration into our quantum computers.
  • Build and lead an in-house photonics team, and establish its design, simulation, test, and integration capabilities.
  • Serve as technical lead for external partnerships with foundries, materials suppliers, and photonics companies: scope development efforts, write and negotiate specifications, review designs, and hold partners to performance and schedule.
  • Scout and evaluate emerging photonic technologies that could open new approaches to qubit control.
  • Generate intellectual property and communicate results internally and, as appropriate, in publications and presentations.
  • Capable of lifting and moving objects that weigh up to 25 pounds.

Experience & Education

  • PhD in Electrical Engineering, Physics, Optical Sciences, Applied Physics, or a related field.
  • 8+ years of post-PhD experience in integrated photonics in industry or a leading research group, including leading PIC programs from concept to fabricated, tested devices.
  • Demonstrated experience building and leading technical teams.

Required Qualifications

  • Recognized expertise in integrated photonics, with command of the full landscape: material platforms, foundry ecosystems, and the current state of the art in modulators, nonlinear devices, and large-scale photonic circuits.
  • Hands-on track record designing, taping out, and characterizing PICs through external foundries or internal fabs.
  • Deep understanding of high-performance electro-optic modulation and/or integrated nonlinear optics.
  • Proven ability to manage external development partners, including writing specifications and driving multi-party programs to schedule.
  • Sound technical judgment: comfortable making high-stakes platform decisions with incomplete information, and defending them with data.
  • Ability to communicate clearly with physicists, engineers, executives, and external partners.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with visible or near-infrared integrated photonics.
  • Experience across multiple material platforms, such as thin-film electro-optic materials, silicon nitride, and heterogeneous integration.
  • Experience with large-scale photonic circuits and with interfacing PICs to free-space optics.
  • Experience with high-speed RF drive, photonic packaging, and fiber and free-space coupling.
  • Established relationships across photonics foundries, academic groups, and startups.
  • Experience in a fast-paced hardware development or startup environment.
