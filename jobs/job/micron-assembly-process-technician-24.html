Assembly Process Technician

Micron · Boise, ID - Main Site · posted 2026-10-27

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

Responsibilities:

  • Perform die attach using automated die bonders, including dispensing conductive adhesives, die placement, alignment, and cure/fire profiles.
  • Wire bond assembled devices and verify bond quality against established criteria.
  • Set up, run, and monitor electromigration and reliability stress tests.
  • Collect, organize, and report test data.
  • Perform equipment troubleshooting, preventive maintenance, and calibration support on assembly and test tooling.
  • Schedule PMs and service calls with vendor.
  • Purchase parts and services through Ariba.
  • Leverage Copilot or similar Artificial Intelligence tools to summarize shift activities, generate maintenance reports, and document production issues.
  • Support implementation of smart-manufacturing initiatives by providing operator feedback on AI tools and automated workflows.
  • Follow and help improve written work instructions, process documentation, and safety procedures.
  • Partner with engineering on new sample builds, process changes, and failure analysis support.

Required Qualifications:

  • Associate degree in electronics, engineering technology, manufacturing, or a related technical field - or equivalent hands-on experience in semiconductor assembly, test, or a comparable precision manufacturing environment.
  • Comfortable working under microscopes and with small-geometry parts requiring fine motor precision.
  • Basic computer skills: data entry, spreadsheets, and test-system software.
  • Able to read and follow detailed technical work instructions.
  • Strong attention to detail and consistent documentation habits.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Wire bonding experience - ball or wedge bonding.
  • Die attach experience - automated die bonder operation and programming.
  • Electromigration (EM) or reliability test experience - stress test setup, high-temperature ovens, parametric data collection.
  • Experience with automated assembly equipment setup, recipe editing, and vision/alignment systems.
  • Background in a cleanroom or controlled manufacturing environment.

Physical & Work Environment:

  • Extended periods of microscope work and seated precision assembly.
  • Cleanroom gowning as required.
  • Handling of chemicals and adhesives per site safety procedures and SDS requirements.
