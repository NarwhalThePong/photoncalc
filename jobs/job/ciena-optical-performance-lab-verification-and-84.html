The Opportunity: 8-month work term, starting in January 2027.


Ciena develops world-leading optical technology that helps carry information across the networks connecting people, businesses, and communities. The Optical Performance team develops and validates photonic hardware and system technologies that enable reliable, high-capacity optical communications. This co-op role provides hands-on experience with optical communications testing, laboratory instrumentation, test automation, and analysis of complex optical systems.


How you will make an impact:

  • Configure, maintain, and improve optical communications lab testbeds, including optical hardware and instruments such as optical spectrum analyzers and oscilloscopes.
  • Develop and refine automated test workflows using tools such as MATLAB, Python, and instrument or REST APIs to improve repeatability, efficiency, and measurement capture.
  • Execute verification tests, collect system data, and assess optical performance under various test conditions.

The must haves:

  • Education: Actively enrolled in an undergraduate degree in electrical engineering, physics, computer engineering, or a related discipline,
  • Some understanding of scientific measurement or data analysis, and an interest in learning optical communications and laboratory testing.
  • Experience using MATLAB for technical computing, scripting, or data analysis.
  • Analytical problem-solving, communication, teamwork, and attention-to-detail skills.
  • Ability to work patiently and carefully with delicate optical equipment in a commercial lab, including handling fragile assembled modules, assembling complex test systems, routing optical fiber, and methodically validating functionality.

Nice to haves:

  • Hands-on experience with laboratory instruments or test automation, such as optical spectrum analyzers or oscilloscopes.
  • Experience coding in Python.
  • Experience with Linux.
  • Familiarity with Git or other version control tools, or with REST APIs.
  • Experience with using AI, GitHub Copilot, and designing and implementing AI agents and workflows.
