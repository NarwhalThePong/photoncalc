Job Description

Equipment Operation & Process Execution

  • Operate and monitor manufacturing equipment
  • Execute process recipes according to engineering specifications
  • Monitor process parameters during production runs

Inline Inspection & Metrology

  • Perform inline quality measurements 
  • Record measurement data accurately 
  • Flag out-of-specification results promptly to responsible engineers

Material & Chemical Handling

  • Handle and manage production materials
  • Ensure proper chemical storage, labeling, and disposal in compliance with EHS regulations
  • Manage material inventory and timely replenishment requests

Documentation & Reporting

  • Accurately complete shift logs, process records, and equipment checklists
  • Report equipment abnormalities, process alarms, and quality issues to engineers promptly

Engineering Support

  • Assist process engineers in:
    • DOE sample preparation and process trials
    • New recipe qualification runs
    • Failure analysis sample collection and preparation
    • NPI process trials and prototype runs
  • Execute engineer-directed process adjustments and parameter changes

Qualifications

 

  • College and above
  • Preferred Major - Electronics, Mechanical, Chemical Technology, Materials, or related
  • Prefer operation experience in semiconductor, HDD, LCD, or related manufacturing 
  • Excellent communication skills, verbal and written
