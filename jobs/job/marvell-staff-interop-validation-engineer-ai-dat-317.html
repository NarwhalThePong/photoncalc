Your Team, Your Impact

The ever-growing demand for higher bandwidth, lower power, and smaller footprint driven by AI, cloud services, video streaming megatrends requires advanced solutions. Marvell's field-proven PAM4 DSPs offer an ideal solution for high-speed optical interconnects in cloud data centers and carrier networks.

What You Can Expect

  • Evaluate hardware and software features from DSP and come up with implementation plans for qualify/test.
  • Collaborate with vendors/manufacturers in defining and developing test plans for Optical Modules and DSP chips.
  • Automate optical test plans and qualify optical modules from different vendors with different switches.
  • Engineering dashboards and status reporting: Build dashboards and regular status reports that track optical module and DSP qualification progress, interop lab readiness, and key performance metrics across vendors and switch platforms. These give engineering and manufacturing teams a clear, current view of where each qualification stands.
  • Adapt and benchmark open-weight language models to speed up lab data review. Use them to pull answers from specs and past test records, make sense of electrical and optical measurements, sort failures by likely cause, and produce first-draft reports for engineers to refine.
  • Build end-to-end automation that gathers specs and prior test records, reviews lab logs, and runs Python scripts and statistical checks on each result set.

What We're Looking For

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering or related fields and 3-5 years of related professional experience, OR Master's degree and/or PhD in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering or related fields with 2-3 years of experience.
  • 2+ years of experience with transceivers and IEEE standards.
  • 2+ years of experience developing test scripts for validation and characterization.
  • 1+ years of experience working with networking protocols and technologies.
