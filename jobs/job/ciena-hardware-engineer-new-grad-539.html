Join the Wavelogic team developing advanced high-speed analog circuits used in Ciena's optical fiber transmission solutions. The role contributes to mixed-signal IP blocks for the Wavelogic family of products, including high-speed DAC and ADC circuits, SERDES solutions, transimpedance amplifiers, modulator driver circuits, and precision analog circuits for control and monitoring functions. Work spans advanced deep-submicron CMOS and/or BiCMOS design and characterization in Ciena's state-of-the-art lab.

 

How you will make an impact:

  • Design complete and detailed analog blocks under the guidance of a senior analog design engineer.
  • Collaborate with layout team members on overall implementation through full GDSII delivery to Ciena’s integration partner.
  • Provide regular status updates, participate in team meetings, and share experience with the group.
  • Characterize analog circuits in Ciena’s state-of-the-art lab, from test chips through full product implementation, with members of the Analog Macro Integration team.

The must haves:

  • Education: Working towards completion of Bachelors, Masters or PhD in electrical engineering, computer science or other applicable scientific study.
  • Experience: Previous internship, co-op, or applied project experience relevant to the role.
  • Experience leading designs in advanced BiCMOS and/or CMOS technology.
  • Ability to methodically address technical problems.
  • Excellent organization, written and oral (English) interpersonal skills.
  • Proficiency with use of applicable design tools from Cadence, Mentor and Synopsys for analog design (eg. Virtuoso, Calibre, STAR-RC, MMSIM).

Nice to haves:

  • Experience with 2.5D or 3D E-M tools such as HFSS or EMX.
  • Experience with mixed-signal design validation using state-of the art probing and test equipment.
