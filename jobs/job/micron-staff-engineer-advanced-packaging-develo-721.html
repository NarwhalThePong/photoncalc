Staff Engineer, Advanced Packaging Development - Design Enablement & Layout

Micron · Boise, ID - Main Site · posted 2026-10-16

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

Micron is seeking a highly experienced Staff Engineer for Design Enablement and Layout in Advanced Packaging Technology Development. This role involves coordinating cross-functional engineering teams across geographies to develop and optimize design rules, requirements, design, and layout from early development through manufacturing!


Job Description:


Responsibilities will include, but are not limited to:

  • Develop and refine Design Rules, Requirements, and Test Structures for advanced packaging.
  • Create layout and DBR test vehicle and product designs to support technology development.
  • Ensure robust Design Rule Checks (DRC) and appropriate responses to deviations.
  • Analyze large datasets to extract meaningful insights and drive data-driven decision-making.
  • Leverage of artificial intelligence to reduce development time.
  • Manage structure evaluation to support development and quantify process margins.
  • Strategically partner with teams to resolve process issues related to layout and prioritize solutions.
  • Document and communicate complex problem resolutions and drive cross-node design rule alignment.
  • Maintain effective communication across Process Integration, Product Engineering, Design, and Advanced Mask teams.

Minimum Qualifications:


  • BS/MS/PhD in Electrical Engineering, Microelectronics, Physics, or related field.
  • 5+ years of experience in semiconductor industry in areas such as Process Integration, Yield Enhancement, Product Engineering, Design, Test Structure Development, or Unit Process Development.
  • Strong understanding of semiconductor memory process flows and TSV based packaging.
  • Hands on experience and proficiency in EDA tools (Cadence Virtuoso Layout and Schematic Editor, Calibre, Hspice etc.)
  • Experience with Design Rule Characterization and validation on Silicon.
  • Proficiency in Python, R, or MATLAB, with experience supporting CAD-related activities, data post-processing, database/reticle data transfers, and a proven ability to troubleshoot complex technical issues while communicating effectively in high-pressure environments.

Additional Nice to have skills:


  • Perform semiconductor design engineering assignments including engineering and designing chip layout circuits, circuit checking, documenting specifications, modifying and evaluating semiconductor devices and components.
  • Reviews product requirements and logic diagrams for device definition. Typically responsible for projects, or portions of projects, to design, fabricate, modify, and evaluate semiconductor devices and components.
