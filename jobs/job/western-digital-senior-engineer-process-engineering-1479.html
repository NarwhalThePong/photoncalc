Job Description

  • Job Summary
    • To ensure smooth execution of back-end, post-sputter processes in compliance to process specification
    • To carry out engineering studies to improve post-sputter processes.
  • Essential Duties & Responsibilities
    • To monitor daily process KPOV and KPIV
    • To trigger for process or machine maintenance as scheduled or on ad hoc basis
    • To ensure compliance to operating procedures and process parameters
    • To carry out evaluations for process improvement
    • To ensure quality of parts that leave the process and own up any excursions and action items to mitigate the impact and solve the issue.

Qualifications

  • Required
    • Bachelor's degree in Engineering or Science or equivalent
  • Preferred
    • 2-5 years experience as a Process Engineer or Manufacturing Engineer
    • Familiar with SPC and Design of Experiments.
  • Skills
    • Minitab/JMP , Spotfire or other data analytics tool, SPC, DOE, Problem solving
