Job Description:

Broadcom is looking for a senior level high speed analog mixed-signal designer. In this highly visible role, you will be designing wireline high-speed analog mixed-signal circuitry for data center connectivity applications.

Qualifications include:

  • Bachelors in Engineering and 5+ years of related experience Masters degree in Engineering and 3+ years of related experience

  • Research and apply cutting edge design and validation for analog/mixed-signal integrated circuits for high-speed

  • Serial link applications such as: fiber optic communication systems, Gigabit Ethernet (GE), enterprise storage and wireless network routers. 

  • Research novel circuit techniques to achieve maximum speed and performance, while minimizing the power and area requirements. 

  • Design, develop, modify and evaluate electronic parts, components, or integrated circuitry for electronic equipment and other hardware systems. 

  • Determine design approaches and parameters. 

  • Analyze equipment to establish operating data; conduct experimental tests and evaluate results. 

  • Select components and equipment based on analysis of specifications and reliability.

  • Layout/design work in a non-ASIC product environment. 

  • Work on complex issues where analysis of situations or data requires an in-depth evaluation of variable factors. 

  • Exercise judgement in selecting methods, techniques and evaluation criteria for obtaining results. 

  • Network with key contacts outside own area of expertise. 

  • Characterize and debug analog designs for manufacturability. 

  • Create circuit models to define package and pc board requirements. 

  • Develop circuit test plans of testing. 

  • Understand system level architectural design of broadband communication SoCs.

Special Skill Requirements:

  • 1. Analog circuit design;

  • 2. CMOS device operating regions & characteristics;

  • 3. Common circuit topologies used in CMOS analog circuits, particularly in circuits running at 1 GHz and above;

  • 4. Analog circuit design trade-offs and parameters (e.g. noise & distortion, frequency-response, mismatch, bandwidth, stability or settling limitations);

  • 5. Signal processing in analog filters, analog to digital converters, digital to analog converters and feedback controlled loops;

  • 6. Simulation tools, custom layout tools and common lab equipment (oscilloscope, spectrum analyzer or similar).

Highly Desired Qualifications:

  • Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the specific ability to speak to various technical and management levels.

  • Proactive, collaborative and creative approach to innovation, technical development and consensus facilitation to influence optimal project results.

  • Excellent time and task management, and interpersonal skills.
