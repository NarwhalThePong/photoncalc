SENIOR ENGINEER - HBM VERIFICATION

Micron · Jalisco, Mexico · posted 2026-10-14

Apply on Micron's site →

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.


Join a team at Micron where you will have the chance to own verification and end-to-end analysis of complex full-chip custom builds. Our advanced low-power and power management technologies span multiple categories, including high-speed memories. Collaborate closely with global development and verification team members, many of whom have decades of experience in memory engineering. This role will also offer cross-functional tasks that can broaden your abilities.


Responsibilities


  • Lead and define the approach for the verification activities within your areas of expertise in any project the team engages in.
  • Provide verification support to build projects by simulating, analyzing, and debugging pre-silicon full-chip builds.
  • Design test cases/stimuli aimed at broadening the functional coverage for HBM emerging memory architectures and features.
  • Take part in the development of verification methodology and environments for advanced HBM and emerging memory products.
  • Collaborate with international colleagues on building new verification flows to address challenges in HBM and emerging memory development.
  • Develop and maintain test benches and test vectors using simulation tools and run regressions for coverage analysis and improvements.
  • Apply AI for automation of technical and non-technical processes.

Minimum Qualifications


  • More than 5 years of experience in pre-silicon verification.
  • BE or MTech in Electronics Engineering or related field.
  • Proficiency in establishing and simulating verification environments.
  • Proficient understanding of basic digital circuits, simulation, troubleshooting, and evaluation.
  • Established skill in managing and steering verification processes within specific areas of knowledge.
  • Experience crafting test cases/stimuli to broaden functional coverage.

Preferred Qualifications


  • Experience with a high-level understanding of UVM.
  • Knowledge of DFT features, including custom DFT and standard scan chains.
  • Exposure to critical timing and data path timing analysis.
  • Experience handling complete verification duties and detailed end-to-end analysis of complex full-chip custom projects demonstrating excellent power, performance, and bandwidth.
  • Using Artificial Intelligence to drive innovation and efficiency.
