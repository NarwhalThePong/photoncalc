Senior Laser Engineer

Atom Computing · Boulder, CO · posted 2026-09-29

Apply on Atom Computing's site →

At Atom Computing, we build quantum computers using arrays of optically trapped neutral atoms that will empower customers to achieve unprecedented computational breakthroughs. Join a world-class team of scientists, engineers, and business professionals to advance the state-of-the-art in quantum computing.

As a Senior Laser Engineer, you'll independently own the precision laser and optical systems — diode and fiber lasers, nonlinear optical frequency conversion — that keep our quantum computers running reliably. You'll bring deep expertise in one or more laser/optics domains to our hardest technical problems, set the bar for documentation and technical rigor, and partner closely with physicists, hardware, and software engineers as we scale.

Job Responsibilities

  • Own alignment, tuning, and troubleshooting of laser and optical systems — diode and fiber lasers/amplifiers, nonlinear optical systems — with minimal supervision
  • Design, characterize, phase-lock, and optimize laser and nonlinear optical systems for new hardware builds and upgrades
  • Lead technical projects end-to-end: gather requirements, build action plans, and drive prioritization and schedule
  • Analyze optical test data — plotting, fitting, interpreting — to guide design and troubleshooting decisions
  • Set the documentation and technical-writing standard for the team, and review the work of other engineers
  • Partner with experimental physicists, hardware, and software engineers, and report progress to the Laser Engineering Manager

Experience & Education

  • Bachelor's degree in Physics, Optics, Chemistry, Engineering, or a related field; advanced degree a plus
  • 5+ years of experience using, maintaining, and independently developing optical, opto-mechanical, and/or laser systems

Required Qualifications

  • Demonstrated mastery in at least one laser/optics domain (e.g., diode/fiber lasers and amplifiers, nonlinear optical systems, phase-locking), evidenced by concrete project contributions
  • Track record of leading laser/optical projects independently, from requirements through completion
  • Experience designing and modeling nonlinear optical or laser/amplifier systems
  • Strong data analysis skills, and comfort producing and working from engineering documentation (change orders, assembly drawings, schematics, wiring diagrams)
  • Excellent communicator, able to set the documentation and technical-writing bar for a cross-functional team of physicists and engineers

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience mentoring or reviewing the work of junior engineers
  • Proficiency with Python and Git
  • Proficiency with Zemax, Onshape, and/or SolidWorks/CAD
