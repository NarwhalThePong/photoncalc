Project Title

Fab 10 Metallization Conversion and M77R New Product Introduction Program Management

Project Description

The intern will undertake a project focused on the Fab 10 Metallization sister fabrication facility conversion and the M77R new product introduction.

The project provides practical exposure to semiconductor manufacturing, process integration, program planning, risk management, and stakeholder coordination. The intern will work with engineering and manufacturing teams to track project readiness, analyze technical information, and develop clear program-management materials.

Objective of the Project

To establish a structured program-management approach for the Fab 10 Metallization conversion and M77R product introduction, enabling effective milestone tracking, early risk identification, manufacturing-readiness assessment, and cross-functional communication.

Opportunities for Full Time Employment

Successful interns may be considered for future full-time opportunities within Micron’s engineering and manufacturing organizations. Consideration will be subject to internship performance, role availability, and business requirements.

Project Scope

  • Develop project plans and milestone-tracking methods for the Fab 10 Metallization conversion and M77R product introduction.
  • Evaluate process readiness, manufacturing capability, qualification progress, and key program dependencies.
  • Identify project risks and develop appropriate mitigation and escalation plans with relevant stakeholders.
  • Analyze engineering and manufacturing information to identify opportunities for cycle-time improvement and risk reduction.
  • Prepare dashboards, project reports, and management-level communication materials to facilitate decision-making.

Learning Opportunities

  • Gain practical exposure to semiconductor manufacturing, process integration, and new product introduction.
  • Learn project-management methods covering planning, scheduling, risk assessment, execution tracking, and change management.
  • Develop experience working with cross-functional engineering and manufacturing teams.
  • Strengthen data analysis, stakeholder communication, and technical presentation skills.

Deliverables

  • A comprehensive project-tracking system covering Fab 10 Metallization conversion and M77R startup milestones.
  • A risk assessment and mitigation framework for key program activities.
  • A dashboard or workflow solution that improves project visibility and execution tracking.
  • A final presentation summarizing project outcomes, lessons learned, recommendations, and business impact.

Impact of the Project

  • Improve visibility of key milestones, risks, dependencies, and qualification progress.
  • Strengthen coordination across the engineering and manufacturing organizations involved in the program.
  • Enable earlier identification and management of potential execution risks.
  • Establish reusable project-management methods for future conversion and new product introduction activities.

Skillsets Required

  • Strong interest in semiconductor manufacturing, process engineering, and fabrication facility operations.
  • Basic understanding of semiconductor process flows and manufacturing concepts.
  • Knowledge of project planning, scheduling, risk management, and stakeholder coordination.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, Power BI, PowerPoint, or comparable analytical and visualization tools.
  • Familiarity with AI-Enabled tools for document preparation, information analysis, or workflow improvement.

Course of Interest

The ideal candidate should be pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronic Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Materials Science, Computer Engineering, Data Science, Project Management, or a related discipline.

Duration of Period

The ideal candidate should be able to commit to a full time internship period of 6 months.
