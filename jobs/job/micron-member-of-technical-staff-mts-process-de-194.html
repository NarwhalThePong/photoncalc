Role Summary

The MTS advances thin film process development for NAND cell film by leading project initiatives, executing complex experiments, conducting material characterization, and supporting process integration/fixing, while promoting manufacturability and continuous improvement. Operates as a key technical contributor delivering measurable results at department/site level; mentors early-career staff aligned to TLP entry expectations; strengthens SOP/knowledge systems.

Main Responsibilities

  • Lead project-level initiatives for cell film process development; execute complex experiments and integration support under senior guidance.

  • Maintain thorough records; prepare comprehensive technical reports; participate in multi-functional problem solving; drive continuous improvement and optimization.

  • Contribute to guidelines/SOPs/knowledge management; stay current on emerging technologies and advances in thin film processing/characterization.

Other Responsibilities

  • Mentor early-career engineers and provide leadership aligned with senior technical expectations.

  • Support continuous improvement and optimization of existing processes.

  • Integrates AI-assisted tools and insights into daily work to improve efficiency, quality, or effectiveness, exercising sound judgment and complying with organizational standards and legal requirements.

  • Contributes to a culture of continuous improvement by identifying, testing, and sharing AI-enabled enhancements within one’s scope of work.

Candidate Profile

Minimum Qualifications / Experience

  • PhD ≥ 8 yrs , or Master’s ≥ 11 yrs, or Bachelor’s ≥ 1 3 yrs of relevant experience in
    Electrical Engineering, Materials Science, Chemical Engineering, Physics, or related field,

  • Experience in thin film R&D for microelectronic devices, with demonstrated contributions to complex technology development

  • Familiarity with electrical test methods and fab metrology tool

Must-Have Technical Skills

  • Strong h ands-on experience with ALD/CVD thin film processes for microelectronic devices, including recipe setup, process tuning, monitoring, and fixing in an R&D or fab environment.

  • Proficient in film and fab metrology tools (e.g., ellipsometry, XRR, SEM/TEM, CD ‑ SEM, thickness measurement, particle counters) to support process monitoring, defect analysis, and yield improvement .

  • Solid understanding of process integration , with the ability to use SPC, DOE, and root cause analysis to optimize processes, resolve excursions, and implement corrective actions.

  • Working knowledge of NAND cell structures, basic device physics , plasma processes, vacuum systems, and chemical reactions, with the ability to relate process conditions to material and device behavior .

  • Experience supporting technology transfer from R&D to manufacturing , developing SOPs and documentation, and communicating technical findings clearly across cross ‑ functional teams while adhering to safety and quality standards.

Must-Have Soft Skills

  • Structured problem-solving and learning agility in an R&D environment.

  • Clear communication and cross-functional collaboration.

Highly Desirable / Preferred

  • Publications/patents and strong record of technical contributions.
