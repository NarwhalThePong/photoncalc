Job Description:

A mechanical engineering position responsible for developing thermal management and robust socketing solutions for test and validation PCBs for Broadcom ASIC products targeted at hyperscale data center applications. Broadcom’s market leading products are enabling the next generation of AI and cloud storage.


Essential Duties and Responsibilities:

  • Collaborate with PCB electrical design engineers to provide robust ASIC socket and thermal solutions for their designs.
  • Perform mechanical component design and design verification using Solidworks.
  • Perform 3d mechanical extractions from Allegro PCB design software.
  • Interface with device package designers and generate device warpage profiles for socket designs from raw simulations data.
  • Measure actual device warpage to validate warpage profile simulations using high precision profilometry equipment.
  • Manage socket hardware vendors, review, provide feedback, and approve designs.
  • Troubleshoot mechanical and electrical contact issues in lab and test floor environments.
  • Design and review cooling solutions (air, liquid, cold plate) for test hardware.
  • Evaluate and manage procurement of mechanical and test hardware such as thermal control units, socket resistance measurement platforms, etc. 
  • 3d print the team’s way out of headaches and usability issues. 

The Successful Candidate Will:

  • Have a strong sense of design ownership. You enjoy collaborating with the engineers using your mechanical solutions and working with them to generate improvements.
  • Have good mechanical prototyping skills.
  • Be extremely detail oriented.
  • Be familiar with the issues involved in socketing and cooling very large, high power, high ball-count ASIC devices.
  • Have experience with pogo-pin and elastomeric IC socket solutions.
  • Have good mechanical debug intuition and be comfortable with all basic hand tools, micrometers, calipers, etc.
  • Have base knowledge of ATE test hardware mechanical configurations.
  • Have familiarity with Keyence profilometry equipment.
  • Have skills in manipulating large data sets in tools such as Excel or in scripts in Python.
  • Have a passion for continuous improvement and a commitment to creating quality output.
  • Exhibit strong teamwork skills as part of a fast moving, multi-site team.
  • Have excellent interpersonal skills and be comfortable managing vendors and internal team customers alike.

Qualifications: 

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Mechanical Engineering and 8+ years of experience (or a Master’s Degree + 6 years of experience) developing semiconductor socket and cooling solutions. 
  • In depth experience using Solidworks.
  • Experience using Ansys Icepack and/or Solidworks FLOW thermal analysis and simulation software.
  • Experience using Ansys mechanical simulation software.
  • Experience managing vendors in the test hardware manufacturing process.
