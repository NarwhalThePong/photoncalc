Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

We are looking for Layout Design engineer at our Micron Technology’s HBM Team in Guadalajara, Mexico. As a Layout Design engineer, you will be working for intensive applications such as artificial intelligence and high-performance computing solutions, High Bandwidth Memory. As a Layout Design Engineer, you will be collaborating with peer teams crossing Micron global footprint, in a multiple projects-based environment.


Responsibilities:


  • Craft and develop IC layouts for leaf cells, standard cells, and sub-blocks, with attention to performance, power, area, capacitance, and design efficiency.
  • Interpret Design Rule Checking (DRC), Layout Versus Schematic (LVS), and other physical verification results to support accurate custom layouts.
  • Apply a systematic, detail-focused approach to problem-solving and physical verification while planning work efficiently.
  • Collaborate with internal engineering teams across global locations to drive successful layout projects.
  • Continue to learn and develop your semiconductor design skills while contributing to innovative HBM solutions.


Minimum Qualifications:


  • Bachelor’s, master’s, or PhD degree in Electronics, Mechatronics, Robotics Engineering, Nanotechnology, or a related technical field.
  • Graduation from a bachelor’s, master’s, or PhD program within the past two years.
  • Strong understanding of electronic circuit concepts and proficiency in electronic circuit simulation or design tools.
  • Effective interpersonal skills, communication, and problem-solving abilities, with conversational English proficiency.
  • Interest in semiconductor technology, with motivation to learn, collaborate, and develop technical expertise.

Preferred Qualifications:


  • Internship or academic project experience related to pre-silicon circuit design, analog circuits, physical design, or analog layout.
  • Knowledge of MOS fundamentals, semiconductor fabrication, and the effects of layout on circuit speed, capacitance, power, and area.
  • Experience with Cadence VLE/VXL, Siemens/Mentor Graphics Calibre DRC/LVS, or similar layout and physical verification tools.
  • Proficiency in programming or scripting languages such as C++ or Python.
  • Experience using or developing AI-enabled methodologies for automation and engineering problem-solving.
