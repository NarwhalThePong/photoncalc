Sr . Principal Verification Engr

NXP · Bangalore · posted 2026-10-25

Apply on NXP's site →

Job Summary:

We are looking for an experienced Senior SoC Verification Lead with 15+ years of expertise in pre-silicon SoC verification. The ideal candidate will have strong hands-on experience in UVM, SystemVerilog, Verilog, and C, and a proven track record of leading end-to-end SoC verification, developing robust verification environments, and driving silicon readiness


Job Qualification:

  • At least 15 years of experience in SoC verification.
  • Strong expertise in UVM, Verilog, System Verilog, C
  • Defined complex pre-silicon verification environments
  • Developed direct and constrained-random stimulus for SoC
  • Expert in analyzing RTL code, functional, assertion coverage results
  • Good knowledge of digital design
  • Awareness of best practices adopted in verification

Job Responsibilities:

  • Lead pre-silicon SoC verification activities end-to-end
  • Define and execute SoC-level verification strategies and plans
  • Develop and maintain UVM based verification environments.
  • Create verification plans from architectural requirements
  • Low power intent verification using CPF, UPF
  • Netlist verification
  • Hardware-software verification
  • Plan and track the verification tasks and ensure silicon tape out readiness.
  • Defines tasks for team members to meet the project timelines
  • Lead and mentor verification engineers
  • Drive verification methodology improvements
  • Collaborate with architecture, RTL design and firmware teams.
