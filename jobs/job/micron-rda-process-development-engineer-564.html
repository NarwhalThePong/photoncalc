Title: RDA Process Engineer

Area: TD RDA

 

As a Realtime Defect Analysis (RDA) Technology Development Engineer at Micron, you will learn how to use state-of-the-art defect detection equipment and processes to troubleshoot and solve issues. This time, we are going to launch project in full swing for future DRAM products and HBM Technology. As an RDA member of this project, you will be responsible for device development to establish device and bring it high volume manufacturing level. We need engineers who are passionate about detecting problems and eager to make changes for the better. You will have the opportunity to learn defect detection equipment applications and also use data analysis skills to identify and solve process issues. This position will provide the opportunity for you to collaborate with engineers in process integration, process development, data science, and yield analysis within and outside Technology Development. As a Technology Development RDA engineer, your ideas will have the ability to impact the team globally. You will be responsible to support experimentation, data analysis, baseline health metrics, and systematic and random defect improvement through partnering with others in Technical Development and High Volume Manufacturing.

 

Minimum Qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Electrical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Materials Science & Engineering, or related field.
  • Proactive and passionate about learning (training and coursework)
  • We need an engineer drawn to creative problem solving and innovative solutions
  • We need someone who will investigate process anomalies, root cause analysis, data manipulation, and correlations
  • We desire someone ready to learn and apply basic Data Science and Data Manipulation (scripting, coding, or programming)
  • Excellent technical, communication, and analytical skills
  • Good verbal and written English communication skills
  • Preference for experience with metrology or inspection
  • Ability to improve operational efficiency and solve problems by leveraging AI tools (such as Anthropic Claude and Microsoft 365 Copilot)

Responsibilities include, but not limited to:

  • Reviewing and analyzing experimental process flows looking for defect anomalies and quarantining process tools that are out of control
  • Possess a continuous improvement process (CIP) demeanor, looking for ways to improve operations, and how to make a difference today!
  • Recognize, identify, and raise awareness of process anomalies with the motivation of "who needs to know this now!"
  • Work with new inspection tools and strategies to drive towards earlier detection of problems and identify the sources
  • Build and improve upon techniques and identify new ways to use existing tools in order to identify relevant process issues
  • We expect active participation in technical meetings and then synthesize from it new opportunities or novel approaches
  • Collaborate with the global network to develop best known methods and maintain alignment between sites
