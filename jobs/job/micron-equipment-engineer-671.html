Our Opportunity Summary:

 


Department: Micron's Fab Engineering team brings together process and equipment engineers who start up, develop and optimize the manufacturing processes behind Micron's products.


Position: As a Fab Engineer, you will improve product quality, reliability, yield and productivity, lower costs, manage risk and resolve manufacturing line problems.

 


(Disclaimer ) While you may not exhibit all of the characteristics/skills listed below today, we are highly interested in a team member who is anxious to grow in both technical breadth and depth. If you are open to learning from and being a valued member of a team of top-tier engineers, we are eager to help build on your existing foundation and expand your individual and collaborative skills in this exciting and unique opportunity.



What’s Encouraged Daily:


 

  • Develop and optimize process conditions, technology and wafer fabrication recipes to improve process capability, yield and product quality.
  • Set up and adjust process parameters across a variety of semiconductor equipment.
  • Find, diagnose and resolve process and manufacturing line problems through root cause analysis using failure analysis, FMEA, 8D and SPC methods.
  • Partner with shift engineering and other process areas to control process deviations and investigate long-term defect issues.
  • Lead and take part in yield improvement, cost reduction and productivity projects.
  • Evaluate, plan and qualify new equipment, materials and process baselines so products meet design and performance specifications.
  • Manage, audit and work with material suppliers to meet quality, cost and risk management goals.
  • Use AI tools where they help, and contribute to developing Agentic AI workflows for current or future processes.


How To Qualify:


 

  • Bachelor's degree in Chemical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Materials Science, Chemistry, Physics, Mechanical Engineering or a related technical field, or equivalent technical military experience.
  • 3+ years of experience in semiconductor process or equipment engineering, gained through professional work, internships, research or military service.
  • Hands-on experience using structured problem-solving methods (FMEA, 8D, SPC or failure analysis) to resolve process issues.
  • Skill in analyzing process data to find trends, deviations and improvement opportunities.


What Sets You Apart:


 

  • Experience in one or more process areas: Photo, CVD, PVD, Diffusion, Implant, Etch, CMP or Wet.
  • Experience leading new process qualifications, yield improvement or cost reduction projects in high-volume manufacturing.
  • Experience with tool installation and qualification.
  • Experience with supplier management or material quality audits.
  • Experience and/or interest in developing Agentic AI workflows into current or future processes.
