PsiQuantum's mission is to build the first useful quantum computers: machines capable of delivering the breakthroughs the field has long promised. Since our founding in 2016, our singular focus has been to build and deploy million-qubit, fault-tolerant quantum systems.  

Quantum computers harness the laws of quantum mechanics to solve problems that even the most advanced supercomputers or AI systems will never reach. Their impact will span energy, pharmaceuticals, finance, agriculture, transportation, materials, and other foundational industries.  

Our architecture and approach is based on silicon photonics. Photonics offers natural advantages for scale: photons don't feel heat, are immune to electromagnetic interference, and integrate with existing cryogenic cooling and standard fiber-optic infrastructure.

Job Summary:

We're seeking an NPI Test Engineer to define how product quality is verified and protected throughout the manufacturing lifecycle. This role owns manufacturing test strategy, production test architecture, test coverage, deployment, and optimization across global manufacturing locations — working at the intersection of hardware, software, manufacturing, quality, operations, and suppliers. Unlike development validation roles that prove a design works, this role focuses on enabling scalable manufacturing: understanding product failure modes, developing efficient screening approaches, and implementing test solutions that support global production at commercial scale.

Responsibilities:

  • Define manufacturing test strategy — production screening philosophies, test coverage requirements, release criteria, and critical failure modes.
  • Develop manufacturing test solutions: test fixtures, automated test equipment, production test software, diagnostics, and calibration methodologies that are scalable and globally deployable.
  • Lead deployment of test systems across supplier and CM environments — station qualification, readiness assessments, operator training, and production support.
  • Continuously improve test effectiveness — reduce false failures and cycle time, enhance coverage, and drive cost-of-test reduction.
  • Develop diagnostics that support fault isolation, correlate test results with failure modes, and support root-cause investigations.
  • Build manufacturing intelligence from test data — yield trends, failure Pareto reviews, correlation studies, and operational reporting.
  • Support production ramp through EVT/DVT/PVT and launch: monitor performance, troubleshoot, and drive corrective actions.
  • Partner with Contract Manufacturers, Product/Process/Quality/Software Engineering, and automation/equipment suppliers — influencing outcomes without direct authority.

What Success Looks Like

  • First 6 months: Develop a strong understanding of product architectures and manufacturing workflows, build relationships with manufacturing partners, and assess manufacturing test performance.
  • First year: Manufacturing test systems successfully deployed globally, coverage targets achieved, escape and false-failure rates improved, and production ramp objectives achieved.
  • Long-term: Manufacturing test becomes a competitive advantage, test architectures scale with product complexity, and quality risks are detected early.

Experience/Qualifications:

Required Qualifications 

  • Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Physics, or related discipline.
  • 5+ years of Test Engineering experience.
  • Experience with automated manufacturing test systems.
  • Experience supporting manufacturing environments.
  • Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Master's degree; semiconductor, photonics, or automation experience.
  • Python, LabVIEW, or equivalent software development; manufacturing analytics or reliability testing experience.
