Job Details:

Job Description: 

As a GPU Design Verification Engineer, you will be at the forefront of advancing Intel's world-class graphics technologies. You will play a pivotal role in verifying the functionality of cutting-edge graphics logic components, ensuring their performance meets stringent specifications. By collaborating with architects, design and performance experts, you will contribute to delivering robust and reliable products and IPs.

 

Key Responsibilities

  • Work closely with architects to define functional verification strategies and execute detailed test plans.
  • Develop scalable and reusable IP verification plans, test benches, and architectures to meet microarchitecture specifications.
  • Independently own the development of test environments, feature verification, and functional coverage closure.
  • Perform debugging and issue replication, root cause analysis, and corrective actions.
  • Validate design functionality through simulations, power and timing analysis, and bug identification.
  • Implement assertion-based verification techniques to strengthen reliability metrics.
  • Collaborate with design and verification teams to continuously improve infrastructure and methodologies.
  • Drive technical reviews and documentation of verification strategies and plans.

Qualifications:

You must possess the below minimum qualifications to be initially considered for this position. Preferred qualifications are in addition to the minimum qualifications and are considered a plus factor in identifying top candidates.

 

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field, plus 4+ years of relevant experience; OR Master's degree with 3+ years of relevant experience; OR PhD with no prior professional experience.
  • Experienced mentioned above should be in the following areas:
    • System Verilog and UVM.
    • Verification test bench development and functional coverage closure.
    • Python or similar scripting languages.
    • A assertion-based verification methodologies.

 

Preferred Qualifications

  • Exposure to CPU or GPU architecture fundamentals and principles of computer architecture.
  • Debug experience with industry-standard frontend design and verification tools and flows.
  • Prior experience in IP development teams.
  • Effective communication skills, teamwork abilities, and a mindset of continuous learning.
  • Experience with AI tools.
  • Experience with formal verification methodologies.
