Key Responsibilities

  • Carry out daily production control activities including wafer lot starts, lot monitoring, FOUP management, offload operations, and WIP management in alignment with Fab priorities and output commitments.
  • Use data analytics and AI-enabled tools to monitor production flow, identify bottlenecks, prioritize actions, and support timely decision making on material movement
  • Support offload send/receive operations, ensuring accurate documentation, system transactions, and compliance with established manufacturing procedures.
  • Monitor production performance indicators including WIP levels, aging risks, cycle time impacts, carrier utilization, and capacity constraints; proactively identify abnormalities and bring up potential risks to Production Control Engineers and Manufacturing teams.
  • Leverage AI-assisted insights and digital dashboards to analyze production trends, detect operational exceptions, and recommend actions to improve workflow efficiency and manufacturing performance.
  • Coordinate with Manufacturing, Warehouse, Equipment Engineering, Automation, and other cross-functional teams to resolve material flow issues including carrier shortages, routing discrepancies, system mismatches, and production disruptions.
  • Perform accurate transaction processing within MES, SAP, and other manufacturing systems to maintain inventory accuracy, recipe compliance, and data integrity.
  • Support first-level troubleshooting of dispatch, logistics, automation, or system-related issues by using available diagnostic tools, AI-assisted knowledge resources, and standard troubleshooting methodologies.
  • Use AI-enabled tools to generate shift summaries, handover reports, operational updates, and issue documentation to improve communication quality and reduce manual administrative effort.
  • Discover opportunities to improve operational efficiency through process standardization, workflow automation, and effective adoption of AI and digital manufacturing solutions.
  • Contributes to a culture of continuous improvement by identifying, testing, and sharing AI-enabled enhancements within one’s scope of work.

Job Requirements

  • Nitec / Higher Nitec / Diploma in Engineering or Science is required, or relevant hands‑on experience in semiconductor manufacturing and fab operations.
  • Experience working in a semiconductor fab environment (Manufacturing, Operations, Warehouse, or related functions) is preferred.
  • Willing and equipped to work non‑standard working hours, including compressed schedules and 12‑hour night shifts, as required by manufacturing operations.
  • Willing and capable of working in a cleanroom for most of the working time, including working fully smocked in accordance with cleanroom and safety requirements.
