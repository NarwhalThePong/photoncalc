Job Description

ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:


Responsible in Head-Disk-Interface Integration related work for product improvement (yield/quality/test time)

  • Responsible in Head-Disk-Interface Integration related work for product improvement (yield/quality/test time)
  • Lead and manage Head-Disk-Interface (HDI) integration efforts aimed at improving product yield, quality, and test time
  • Support tribology improvement initiatives in sustaining products to enhance performance and longevity
  • Actively engage in the early ramp-up of HAMR (Heat-Assisted Magnetic Recording) products, driving improvements related to tribology and related areas
    Conduct failure analysis, providing insightful feedback to relevant teams for continuous improvement.
  • Collaborate with FJ HDI Development, HDIFW and Head/Media teams to enhance product detectability and meet quality standards
  • Partner with Head/Media teams to optimize processes, focusing on yield, quality, cost, and supply chain efficiency
  • Support the qualification process for Head & Media Engineering Changes (ECs)

Qualifications

REQUIRED:

  • Master’s degree in Computer Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Electrical Engineering
  • 0 - 2  years experience in  programming for data analytics (Python, C++, SQL)

PREFERRED:

  • Knowledge of HDD failure analysis, reliability, HDD components, processes, and technologies.
  • Familiarity with quality management practices in the HDD industry.

SKILLS:

  • Strong communication skills in English and the ability to collaborate effectively with a global team.
  • Proficient in basic analytical tools (e.g., Excel, JMP, Minitab).
  • Strong problem-solving, creativity, flexibility, and initiative.
  • Leadership abilities and a positive, proactive mindset.
  • Good presentation skills (Animation, Video editor)
