Senior Analog and Mixed Signal Design Engineer

Job Details:

Job Description: 

The Group

Intel's Advanced Design Foundational IP Organization (AD-FIP) within Design Technology Platform (DTP) drives pathfinding and development of the analog/mixed-signal and high-speed I/O IP that defines what Intel's leading-edge process technologies can deliver. The High-Speed I/O team develops SerDes and die-to-die interface reference designs and test chips that co-optimize Intel process technology with next-generation wireline standards, including high-speed PAM4 electrical links, PCIe, UCIe, and HBM.

The Role

We are seeking a senior technical expert in high-speed SerDes circuit design and SerDes system architecture. You will own SerDes link architecture on Intel's leading technology test chips, from link budget and equalization strategy down to transistor-level design of the critical analog front-end and clocking blocks. You will also translate link requirements into device, interconnect, and design-rule requirements that shape future Intel process nodes. This is a senior individual-contributor role with technical leadership across design, layout, and measurement engineers.

Responsibilities

  • Define SerDes link architecture for next-generation interfaces (224G/448G-class PAM4 electrical links, PCIe Gen7/Gen8, UCIe): channel analysis, link budget, jitter and noise budgeting, and equalization partitioning across TX FFE, RX CTLE, DFE, and ADC/DSP-based receivers.
  • Build and maintain system-level link models (statistical and time-domain, in MATLAB or Python; COM and IBIS-AMI based) and use them to derive block-level specifications for TX, RX, ADC, and clocking circuits.
  • Lead transistor-level design of critical SerDes blocks: TX drivers (SST/CML) with FFE, RX AFE (CTLE, VGA, termination, T-coil and ESD co-design), DFE and slicers, time-interleaved ADC front ends, CDR and phase interpolators, LC and ring PLLs, and clock distribution.
  • Drive test-chip definition, integration, tapeout readiness, and silicon bring-up; correlate measured BER, eye, jitter, and power against models, and lead root-cause analysis of silicon-to-model gaps.
  • Translate SerDes requirements into process and PDK requirements (device fT/fmax and parasitics, BEOL RC, inductor Q, matching, ESD capacitance budget, reliability and aging) and drive design-technology co-optimization with process, PDK, and template-library teams.
  • Provide technical leadership: mentor engineers, lead design reviews, and represent Intel Foundry in technical engagements with IP partners and foundry customers.

Qualifications:

You must possess the below minimum qualifications to be initially considered for this position. Preferred qualifications are in addition to the requirements and are considered a plus factor in identifying top candidates.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelors Degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or Electrical and Computer Engineering with 6+ years of industry experience in high-speed wireline SerDes circuit design experience -OR- Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or Electrical and Computer Engineering with 4+ years of industry experience in high-speed wireline SerDes circuit design, -OR- Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or Electrical and Computer Engineering with 2+ years of industry experience in high-speed wireline SerDes circuit design.
  • Experience in Transistor-level design of SerDes TX, RX, or clocking circuits in at least one silicon-proven design at 28 Gb/s per lane or higher.
  • Experience in at least one of the following:
    • SerDes link architecture: channel and link-budget analysis or equalization trade-off studies used to set block-level specifications.
    • Lab validation of high-speed links: BER, eye, or jitter measurement and correlation to simulation.
    • Behavioral or statistical link modeling in MATLAB or Python.

 

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with Silicon-proven designs at 56 Gb/s PAM4 or higher, in FinFET or GAA CMOS; multiple tape-outs with block ownership.
  • Experience across all three areas above (architecture, lab validation, and link modeling).
  • Experience in ADC/DSP-based receivers at 112 Gb/s PAM4 or higher, including time-interleaved ADC design. - 224 Gb/s-class link development (IEEE 802.3dj, OIF CEI-224G). - PCIe Gen6/Gen7 PHY or UCIe die-to-die PHY development.
  • Design experience on 5nm-class or more advanced nodes, with design-technology co-optimization experience with process teams.
  • Experience in package and channel co-design: package/PCB channel modeling, signal and power integrity, crosstalk.
  • Experience with first-author publications at ISSCC, VLSI Symposium, CICC, or JSSC; patents in SerDes circuits or architecture.
  • Technical leadership of a multi-engineer SerDes design team through a full PHY delivery.
