Your Team, Your Impact

Marvell is the leader in data movement interconnects between and inside data centers. We move big data fast, around the globe, with high quality and reliability. We offer semiconductor components and optical subsystems to our networking original equipment manufacturer (OEM), optical module, cloud and telecom service provider customers.

As a Silicon Photonics Intern in the Optical Interconnect team, you will contribute to silicon photonics devices and integrated circuits that power next-generation high-speed optical links for AI and cloud data centers.

What You Can Expect

  • Work with the optical component / photonic integrated circuit design flow used in industry.
  • Work with the design constraints and trade-offs that must be considered in industry.
  • Design, simulate and model innovative silicon photonic devices (e.g., modulators, photodetectors, waveguides, couplers, filters) and high-speed optical interfaces, taking industry requirements into account.
  • Support characterization and validation of silicon photonic devices and correlate measurements with simulation and models.
  • Analyze and present data and ideas to the team and to a diverse audience.
  • Collaborate with design, packaging, test and product teams.

What We're Looking For

  • Student in a Ph.D. degree program in Electrical Engineering, Photonics, Physics, Optical Engineering or a related field.
  • Solid understanding of one or more of the following: 1. Semiconductor optical devices such as modulators, detectors, lasers and other waveguide devices, with familiarity or direct experience of optical simulation software tools and/or optical device modeling. 2. Semiconductor packaging/integration technology such as signal/power integrity, heterogeneous integration, mechanical and assembly processes.
  • Ability to present data and ideas to a diverse audience.
  • Preferred: Python/MATLAB for data analysis and automation; hands-on experience with optical lab measurements.
