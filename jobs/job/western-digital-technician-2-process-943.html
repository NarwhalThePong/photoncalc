Job Description

Job Summary:
The Sputter Process Technician is responsible for executing process characterization activities, ensuring sputter process meets key performance parameters, and supporting production through process monitoring and adjustments. 

ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:

  • Perform process characterization for sputter equipment and materials.
  • Conduct buy-off by verifying key process input variables (KPIV), including pressure, temperature, and voltage.
  • Perform measurements and data collection for characterization samples.
  • Monitor key process output variables (KPOV) during production to ensure process stability and product quality.
  • Perform process adjustments as needed to maintain specifications and resolve deviations.
  • Assist in troubleshooting process and equipment issues to minimize downtime.
  • Maintain accurate records of process parameters, test results, and adjustments.
  • Adhere to safety guidelines and cleanroom protocols at all times.

Qualifications

REQUIRED:

  • Diploma or certificate in Engineering, Manufacturing Technology, or related field.

PREFERRED:

  • At least 0-3 years of experience in semiconductor manufacturing or sputter process operations is an advantage.

SKILLS:

  • Knowledge of process control parameters and measurement techniques.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
