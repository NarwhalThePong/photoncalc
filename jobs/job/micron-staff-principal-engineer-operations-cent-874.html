Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

As the Operations Central Team (OCT) Dry Etch Tool Installation Qualification (TIQ) Engineer at Micron Technology, Inc., you will be building growth mindset, facilitate and drive improvement on Micron TIQ engineering globally, driving global business processes, and enabling the leverage of world-wide resources to deliver industry benchmark methods. And you are also responsible for leading the activities to achieve the Best in Class (BIC) TIQ performance (cost and schedule) across the network and industry. You are encouraged to work closely with the key partners (sites, central team partners and suppliers) to identify areas of opportunities and analyze their performance-to-plan data to improve TIQ performance. Micron is attracting the diversified and dedicated candidate to build innovative solution for all aspects.

Responsibilities

  • Understand short-term and long-term Cost goals within Front End and ensure alignment to corporate business goals. Initiate and drive multi-functional programs to achieve department business goals
  • Facilitate the definition of program scope, goals, work, timeline and resource requirements in collaboration with sponsors and partners
  • Manage the definition, refinement, and implementation of core methodologies and tools for business process including reporting
  • Collaborate with project teams to understand the business value of projects and programs in place and how they contribute to BIC plan.
  • Provide program and project performance updates periodically with communication across functional groups.
  • Ensure strategic roadmaps with important measures for Schedule and Cost are developed, maintained, and tracked to latest BIC targets.
  • Lead and continuously improve the TIQ business processes, continual improvement project and BKM alignment.
  • Prioritize and lead numerous projects based on Fab and corporate objectives. Move projects effectively through project lifecycle – definition, planning, implementation, acceptance and review.
  • Facilitate and drive global peer groups to align strategy and roadmap to achieve area objectives. Build metrics with clear line of sight to strategic objectives. Drive alignment to demonstrate performance improvement and hold accountability to those decisions.
  • Review and lead current Micron Sites TIQ performance, identify gaps and opportunities to achieve BIC performance.
  • Lead TIQ alignment and improvement to meet Micron TIQ objectives. Align procedures and define requirements based on technical assessment and lesson learns.
  • Drive continuous improvement in multiple aspects such as safety, Capex, cash cost, equipment performance, sustainability, new technology introduction, first of a kind tool enablement and etc.
  • Develop and improve Micron TIQ documentations when is needed, includes TIQ specification, TIQ configuration and related process qualification data.
  • Demonstrating Artificial Intelligence and digital tools to improve operational efficiency, enable data-driven insights, and accelerate business outcomes.

Requirements

  • Masters or Bachelor or equivalent experience in engineering or equivalent experience
  • 3+ years of experience with tool installation of Dry Etch equipment
  • Preferred hands on experience in cost management
  • Good communication in English
  • Willing to travel
