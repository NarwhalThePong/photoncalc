Lead Logic Design Engineer – UALink IP/Subsystem  

Experience  

6–10 years  

Job Summary  

Lead RTL and microarchitecture development for UALink-based IP/subsystems, focusing on high-bandwidth, low-latency data movement and scalable interconnect design.  

Key Responsibilities  

  • Lead UALink IP/subsystem RTL development.  
  • Translate protocol requirements into detailed microarchitecture.  
  • Design high-performance control and data-path logic.  
  • Work on buffering, arbitration, flow control, ordering, and error handling.  
  • Develop and review SystemVerilog RTL.  
  • Drive performance, area, power, and latency optimization.  
  • Collaborate with verification, SoC integration, and physical design teams.  
  • Debug complex functional and integration issues.  
  • Review RTL and mentor engineers.  
  • Support silicon debug and issue resolution.  

Required Skills  

  • Strong RTL and microarchitecture experience.  
  • Experience with high-speed interconnects.  
  • Strong understanding of data movement, flow control, arbitration, and buffering.  
  • Excellent SystemVerilog/Verilog skills.  
  • Experience with CDC, lint, synthesis, and timing.  
  • Strong debugging and technical leadership skills.  

Preferred  

  • Direct  UALink  experience.  
  • Experience with PCIe, CXL, CHI, CCIX, Ethernet, or similar technologies.  
  • Experience with AI/accelerator or high-performance compute SoCs.
