Job Description

ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:

  • Normal Shift but expect come into support for unschedule equipment down during odd hours.

  • Perform preventive maintenance on machines and maintain accurate maintenance records.

  • Act as the first line of response for machine unscheduled downtime and quality issues.

  • Coordinate and communicate with cross-functional departments to meet project timelines

  • Provide clear and structured reports on major equipment downtime, including chronological events and systematic continuous improvement on chronic issues.

Qualifications

REQUIRED:

  • Degree in any Engineering.

PREFERRED:

  • Minimum 3 years working experience

  • Applicants who demonstrate early-career level experience are also encouraged to apply especially in machine programmable language and general computer coding such as C++ etc

SKILLS:

  • Strong team player with effective communication skills.

  • In-depth knowledge of Automation and Control Systems.

  • Ability to handle sudden changes and work effectively under pressure to restore machine operations within short notice.

  • Knowledge of Statistical Process Control (SPC) is an added advantage.

  • Understanding of machine software such as PLC or C++ will be an added technical advantage.
