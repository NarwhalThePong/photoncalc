As a Test Process Technician, you are responsible for the installation and maintenance of manufacturing equipment. Sustain and improve key performance indices of the equipment through continuous improvement activities


Responsibilities:

  • Validate test floor deviation lots and investigate deviations.
  • Determine root causes of test-related issues and classify problems appropriately.
  • Provide proper disposition of affected lots and follow up on corrective actions.
  • Escalate issues to relevant engineering or support teams when required.
  • Debug testing-related issues and support manufacturing during troubleshooting activities.
  • Provide guidance to manufacturing personnel on lot handling and testing requirements.
  • Monitor test process performance and ensure compliance with quality standards.
  • Support continuous improvement initiatives to improve yield, quality, and productivity.

Requirements:-

  • Diploma in mechanical engineering & Electronic/Electrical Engineering
  • Fresh graduates are encouraged to apply
  • Some understanding of Semiconductor Testng processes will be added.
  • A good teammate equipped with excellent interpersonal and communication skills.
  • Leveraging AI tools to: Retrieve technical information and Support troubleshooting
