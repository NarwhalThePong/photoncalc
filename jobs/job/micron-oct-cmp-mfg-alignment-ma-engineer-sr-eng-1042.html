As an OCT CMP Mfg. Alignment (MA) Engineer/Sr Engineer at Micron, you will ensure flawless operation and improvement of manufacturing tools and processes. Located in Taiwan, this role offers an outstanding chance to work in a dynamic and collaborative environment. Your contributions will be key to achieving our world-class standards.

Responsibilities:

  • Improve tool utilization to meet outstanding (BIC) standards by accomplishing processes that eliminate low utilization tools and enable cost savings through cold/warm shutdowns.

  • Generate globally aligned warm shutdown procedures that ensure safety and seamless recovery to production.

  • Standardize practices and best-known methods (BKMs) across process areas by setting up global alignment teams and crafting a library of BKMs for equipment and process engineers.

  • Align and integrate Preventive Maintenance (PM) procedures across the Micron network, ensuring accessibility and efficiency through a standardized system.

  • Deploy and sustain SEAL system BKMs globally, ensuring critical processes are standardized and maintained.

Minimum Qualifications:

  • A Bachelor's degree in Engineering or a related field, or equivalent experience.

  • Proven experience in a manufacturing or engineering role, with a focus on tool and process optimization.

  • Strong problem-solving abilities and the capacity to collaborate effectively within a group setting.

  • Excellent communication skills and the ability to maintain alignment with various collaborators.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Advanced degree in Engineering or a related field, or equivalent professional experience.

  • Experience with global standardization of manufacturing processes.

  • Experience with SEAL systems and Preventive Maintenance (PM) protocols.

  • Demonstrated leadership experience and the ability to mentor and guide teams to success.

  • AI-Assisted Data Analysis
  • AI Agent Utilization and Development, Workflow Redesign with AI
