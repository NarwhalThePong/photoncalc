Process Technician

Micron · Manassas, VA - Fab 6 · posted 2026-10-19

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

As a Process Technician, you will support wafer manufacturing by monitoring operations, reviewing data, applying structured problem-solving, and partnering across engineering, manufacturing, and production teams to enhance yield, quality, and efficiency .

RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Monitor process and equipment performance using SPC charts, dashboards, alarms

  • Manage semiconductor equipment, perform technical evaluations, recognize abnormal conditions and resolve issues

  • Follow defined troubleshooting and escalation paths

  • Partner with Engineering to improve and stabilize processes

  • Collect and document production and process data to analyze and interpret using computer-based systems

  • Prepare data visualizations to surface yield and process trends

  • Partner and collaborate with teams (Process Engineering, Manufacturing, Quality)

  • Provide technical guidance to line operators, participate in shift pass-down

  • Follow established work safety procedures with minimal supervision

MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS
Shown through education, internships, research, military, manufacturing, or professional experience.

  • High school diploma or equivalent

  • Willing and able to work 12-hour shifts, including nights and weekends (24/7 fabs ).

  • Experience and/or interest in developing Agentic AI workflows into current or future processes

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS
Shown through education, internships, research, military, manufacturing, or professional experience.

  • Analytical thinking

  • Attention to detail

  • Disciplined execution

  • Structured problem-solving

  • Effective communication

  • Time management

  • Ability to work independently

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS
Experience or exposure to one or more of:

  • Associate degree, technical certification, military training, or applied coursework in science, engineering, or manufacturing.

  • Disciplined execution or data-driven work in manufacturing, laboratory, cleanroom, or high-reliability environments.

  • Process monitoring, data interpretation, or Statistical Process Control (SPC) concepts.
