The Opportunity: 4-month work term, starting in January 2027.


Step into reliability engineering and contribute to evaluating the durability, performance, and lifespan of electronic components and circuit boards used in telecommunications technologies. The role combines hands-on laboratory testing, data analysis, and knowledge management using Excel, Python, and AI/RAG technologies.


How you will make an impact:

  • Investigate the durability and performance of discrete components and PCBAs through accelerated life testing and corrosion studies designed for harsh operating environments.
  • Design and set up test circuits while monitoring critical electrical parameters and component behavior.
  • Analyze the lifespan and reliability of electronic components on complex circuit boards using data-driven methodologies and Excel-based tools.
  • Transform reliability data into accessible, searchable knowledge repositories using Excel, Python, and AI/RAG technologies.
  • Gain hands-on experience in a collaborative lab environment, with onsite work at Ciena’s Ottawa campus required 3–4 days per week.

The must haves:

  • Education: Actively enrolled in a degree program in Physics, Electrical Engineering, Reliability Engineering, or a related discipline,
  • Strong working knowledge of Excel, Excel VBA, and Python.
  • Knowledge of Large Language Models (LLMs) and AI/RAG frameworks.
  • Understanding of basic electronic circuits and electronic components.
  • Experience using digital voltmeters (DVMs) and common laboratory instrumentation.
  • Self-motivated, curious, and eager to learn.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills and a collaborative mindset and passion for solving technical challenges.

Nice to haves:

  • Programming experience with JavaScript, Node.js, Git, Arduino controllers.
  • Experience optimizing AI model performance, token consumption, and cost efficiency.
  • Soldering experience.
  • Understand electrical schematics.
