The Process Control Engineer (Systems) develops, deploys, and sustains Micron’s Process Control Systems (PCS) across Assembly & Test manufacturing. This role supports (not limited to) :

  • SPACE Statistical Process Control (SPC)

  • Applied Materials E3 : Fault Detection and Classification (FDC) and Run to Run (R2R)

  • MES (Manufacturing Execution System) , MESCentral , and MAM (Manufacturing Application Management)

  • RMS (Recipe Management Systems)

The position works closely with Process Engineering (PE), Equipment Engineering (EE), Automation/IT, Manufacturing, and Global Quality to ensure proactive control, strong governance, and audit readiness.

Main Responsibilities:

1) PCS Systems Ownership

  • Configure and sustain Applied E3 Process Control (Trace, UVA, models, alarms, data health).

  • Build and maintain SPACE SPC charts, limits, and automated reactions.

  • Ensure stable SECS/GEM communication and equipment-to-host connectivity.

  • Maintain MES/MAM/RMS interfaces with strong data integrity and version control.

2) SPC Strategy & Governance

  • Lead SPC lifecycle : variable selection, chart setup, limits, automated responses, and chart health reviews.

  • Ensure alignment with Micron Global SPC Standards and quality system requirements.

3) FDC & OCAP Management

  • Develop and maintain FDC models, classifiers, traces, and limits.

  • Validate UVA and reduce false alarms for stable production performance.

  • Govern OCAP (Out of Control Action Plan) logic and changes via ECN (Engineering Change Notice).

4) MES / MAM / RMS Engineering

  • Support MES and MAM logic, events, data collection, and equipment states.

  • Govern RMS recipe/version consistency supporting SPC and FDC execution.

  • Troubleshoot system logic and collaborate with Automation Technology ( ATAuto ) on improvements.

5) Monitoring & Continuous Improvement

  • Track key metrics: OOC (Out of Control) rate, false alarms, FDC coverage, reaction compliance/TAT (Turnaround Time), system reliability.

  • Perform structured Root Cause Analysis (RCA).

  • Automate tasks using SQL, Python scripts.

6) Collaboration & Audit Readiness

  • Partner with PE, EE, PDE, MCT, AT Auto, and Global Quality.

  • Maintain documentation, entitlement controls, and audit-ready evidence.

  • Share and adopt best practices through global PCS forums.

Other responsibilities:

  • Integrates AI-assisted tools and insights into daily work to improve efficiency, quality, or effectiveness, exercising sound judgment and complying with organizational standards and legal requirements.

  • Contributes to a culture of continuous improvement by identifying, testing, and sharing AI-enabled enhancements within one’s scope of work.
     

Job Requirements, Qualifications/Experience:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Electrical & Electronic Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Manufacturing Engineering, or related field.

  • Knowledge of SPC concepts, capability indices, OOC vs. OOS (Out of Spec).

  • Experience owning E3 models and SPACE SPC frameworks.

  • Experience with FDC/OCAP in Assembly & Test or Frontend facilities.

  • Exposure to predictive analytics, or smart manufacturing platforms.

  • Familiarity with SPC governance and audit processes. Experience with at least one Micron system: Applied E3 FDC/R2R, SPACE SPC, SECS/GEM, MES/ MESCentral , RMS.

  • Skills in scripting (SQL, Python) and process/equipment data interpretation.

  • Strong analytical, logical, and critical thinking skills 

  • Effective communicator, able to collaborate across all levels 

  • Growth mindset with a passion for continuous learning 

  • Internship or experience in the semiconductor industry is a plus 

  • Demonstrated leadership and a track record of impact are highly desirable 

  • Interest in and knowledge of the semiconductor industry and Micron

  • Ability to apply baseline digital fluency and role ‑ appropriate AI literacy to use AI ‑ enabled tools responsibly and effectively for research, analysis, content creation, problem ‑ solving, operational tasks, and achieving business outcomes.
