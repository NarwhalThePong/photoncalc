Description

As a Process Control System Engineer at Micron, you will play a crucial role in developing smart manufacturing solutions that set the standard for Industry 4.0. This outstanding opportunity will allow you to gain knowledge in innovative areas such as Machine Learning, Advanced Control Solutions, and Deep Learning. You will be responsible for crafting comprehensive solutions that encompass requirements gathering, data analysis, and system development and integration, leading to network-wide implementation. Your work will have a direct impact on our company's success by improving process capabilities and ensuring quality output through advanced process control measures. Collaboration with various process areas will be key to understanding their tools and challenges, allowing you to develop effective process control models and strategies.

Responsibilities

  • Develop, implement, and improve smart control solutions to achieve efficient manufacturing performance.
  • Provide technical oversight, mentorship, and support for process control implementation.
  • Conduct intensive data analysis and modeling to identify process variations and translate findings into control solutions.
  • Support PDE KEG team in Classification, Characterization, and Categorization, defining data collection requirements and performing integration and setup in the E3 Environment.
  • Define analysis and setup requirements, establish limits, and engage in periodic reviews with the PCS team on best-known methods.
  • Develop Run-to-Run control requirements and build controllers for real-time closed-loop process tuning in assembly processes.
  • Deliver training on control systems and related topics to ensure team proficiency.

Requirement

  • Doctorate, Master's, or Bachelor's level education in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or Physics, or relevant professional experience.
  • Passion for programming and data analysis.
  • Familiarity with Machine Learning.
  • Preferred experience with Python, R, SQL, C#, Hadoop ecosystem, and/or web-based application development.
  • Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills.
  • Proven project management abilities.
  • Ambitious with the ability to multitask and manage dynamic priorities.
  • Strong interpersonal skills and a great teammate.
  • Ability to work in a multi-functional team environment.
  • Current supervisor recommendation as a viable candidate.
  • Willingness and capability for international travel, as needed.
  • Leveraging AI to automate the work & ability to work/develop AI predictive systems
  • Experience in semiconductor manufacturing and packaging is advantageous.
