Job Description:



Role Overview

Broadcom is seeking a motivated Hardware Applications Engineer to join our AI Networking Infrastructure team on-site, working with Enterprise, high-growth Frontier Labs, and Hyperscalers. In this evolving role, you will collaborate with cross-functional engineering teams to provide hardware technical support, board-level debug assistance, and laboratory validation for Broadcom’s industry-leading semiconductor solutions.



Key Responsibilities

  • Technical Support & Design Guidance:
    • Provide hardware design support and technical guidance throughout the product lifecycle.
    • Assist with schematic and PCB layout reviews, component selection, and reference board bring-up.
    • Help create and maintain customer-facing technical documentation guiding board design, including design guides, application notes, and layout recommendations.

  • Hands-on Lab Testing & Troubleshooting:
    • Perform hands-on board bring-up, debugging, and functional testing in the lab.
    • Utilize standard lab test equipment (oscilloscopes, multimeters, power supplies) to diagnose and resolve hardware issues.
    • Assist with basic board rework and electrical characterization.


Qualifications & Requirements

  • Education:
    • Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering (BSEE), Computer Engineering (BSCE), or related engineering field.

  • Experience:
    • 2–6 years of experience in hardware engineering, applications support, board design, or hardware validation.

  • Core Requirements:
    • Solid understanding of general electrical engineering and PCB design fundamentals.
    • Hands-on experience working in a hardware lab with standard test and measurement equipment (oscilloscopes, DMMs, power supplies).
    • Basic understanding of power management, thermal principles, and board-level interconnects.
    • Ability to create clear, customer-facing technical documentation and design guidance.
    • Strong communication and collaborative problem-solving skills.

Preferred / Nice-to-Have Qualifications


  • Master’s degree in Electrical or Computer Engineering.
  • Familiarity with high-speed serial interfaces (such as SerDes) and signal integrity fundamentals.
  • Experience evaluating DC-DC power converters or power delivery networks (PDN).
  • Familiarity with AI-coding tools (Claude/Codex)
  • Experience with high-speed PCB stack-up and impedance control design rules.
