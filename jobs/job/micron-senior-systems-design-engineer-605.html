Senior Systems Design Engineer

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

Micron Semiconductor Products, Inc.


Work closely with the Tier 1 customers, OEMS, and chipset vendors to propose solutions based on state-of-the art and emerging memory technologies as they are applied to current and future generation automotive infotainment, telematics, and sophisticated driving assistance and autonomous platforms. Develop product requirements to build, drive, and implement the next generation leading memory products. Research, develop, document, and share reusable solution designs with partners and customers. Evangelize joint customer-Micron solutions across the Micron sales and field application team to facilitate growth for both Micron and assigned customer(s). Partner with product line management and product architecture to ensure that the voice of the customer and requirements influence product and solutions roadmaps; work with segment marketing and business development to improve the global impact of solutions. Provide thought leadership; keep up with industry trends; influence strategy based on understanding of ecosystem and market. Act as the primary technical evangelist within assigned partners and customer(s) for future, emerging, and next-generation products and solutions. Position requires up to 15% domestic and 5% international travel.


Employer will accept a Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or related field and 60 months of experience in the job offered or in a related occupation.

Position requires:
1. Automotive electrical and electronics including Autonomous/ADAS, infotainment, and centralized or zonal architectures
2. Software-defined vehicle platforms, automotive computers, or high-performance systems
3. Embedded systems architecture and engineering
4. Automotive functional safety and security
5. Working with Automotive Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) and Tier 1
6. Systems experience, including automotive hardware architecture, design, test, and troubleshooting
7. DRAM/managed NAND architecture in systems/platforms.
