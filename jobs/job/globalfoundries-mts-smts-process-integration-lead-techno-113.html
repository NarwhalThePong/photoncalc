Introduction:

Process integration lead in development, optimization, and qualification of semiconductor devices/processes to meet performance requirements.

Your Job :

  • Lead process integration activities for the development, optimization, and qualification of RF semiconductor technologies, ensuring device performance, manufacturability, yield, and reliability targets are achieved.

  • Design, execute, and analyze Design of Experiments (DOE) to drive process understanding, optimization, and technology development.

  • Partner closely with process module, device, yield, reliability, and manufacturing teams to improve product performance and process capability.

  • Own and drive complex technical problem-solving activities, utilizing data-driven methodologies to identify root causes and implement robust solutions.

  • Serve as the primary technical interface for internal and external customers, addressing technical inquiries and supporting customer engagements.

  • Define and establish technology design rules, process monitors, test structures, characterization plans, and qualification methodologies for new technology platforms.

  • Drive continuous innovation through the development of novel integration schemes, methodologies, and process improvements to enhance technology competitiveness.

  • Mentor junior engineers and provide technical leadership across cross-functional development programs.

Other Responsibilities: 

  • Perform all activities in a safe and responsible manner and support all Environmental, Health, Safety & Security requirements and programs

Required Qualifications :

  • Bachelor’s, Master’s, or PhD degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering or equivalent.

  • Minimum 6 years of relevant semiconductor industry experience with a PhD, or 8 years with a Bachelor’s/Master’s degree.

  • Strong wafer fabrication process knowledge with proven experience in FEOL process integration and technology development.

  • Demonstrated ability to lead cross-functional technical projects and drive issues to resolution in a fast-paced development environment.

  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and data interpretation skills.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Hands-on experience in RF-SOI technology development, integration, characterization, and qualification.

  • Understanding of semiconductor device physics, RF device behavior, and RF characterization techniques (e.g., S-parameters) is a plus.

  • Experience in process optimization, yield enhancement, reliability qualification, and root-cause analysis methodologies.

  • Self-motivated, resourceful, and adaptable, with a strong commitment to continuous learning and technical excellence.

  • Excellent communication, leadership, and interpersonal skills, with the ability to influence and collaborate effectively across multidisciplinary teams.

  • Experience interfacing with customers and presenting technical results to senior management and external stakeholders.
