Internal Job Description

Staff Product Engineer – RF & Mixed-Signal Systems

Summary:
Join our industry-leading product engineering team driving next-generation RF transceiver technologies. As a Senior Product Engineer, you will play a critical role in evaluating, characterizing, and ensuring the performance of advanced mixed-signal ICs and transceiver systems. This position requires deep technical expertise, hands-on lab experience, and strong collaboration across design, test, applications, and marketing teams.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead evaluation and characterization of ADCs, DACs, high-speed serial interfaces, and RF systems for cutting-edge transceiver products.
  • Debug and analyze complex mixed-signal and RF systems across all operating conditions; document and present findings to design and cross-functional teams.
  • Perform functional and performance testing of new silicon and embedded software, ensuring readiness for production release.
  • Develop and automate test solutions using Python, MATLAB, LabVIEW, and other programming tools for RF and mixed-signal verification.
  • Utilize precision measurement equipment (network analyzers, spectrum analyzers, oscilloscopes, signal generators) to establish RF, analog, and digital performance metrics.
  • Provide recommendations on testability, manufacturability, and qualification readiness; support field quality initiatives.
  • Interface as a central resource with design, applications, and marketing teams; engage directly with customers to resolve product issues.
  • Analyze measurement data and contribute to system-level solutions for transceiver products.

Minimum Qualifications

  • BS or MS in Electrical Engineering or related field, with 8+ years of industry experience in RF/mixed-signal product engineering.
  • Proven expertise in automating RF tests and working with advanced lab equipment (network analyzers, spectrum analyzers, signal generators, oscilloscopes).
  • Strong programming and debug skills in Python, MATLAB, LabVIEW, and familiarity with C/C++ or similar languages.
  • Deep knowledge of analog and RF circuits, high-speed serial interfaces, ADCs/DACs, and mixed-signal systems.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills; ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • Excellent communication skills and ability to work effectively in multi-disciplinary, global teams.
