Job Description:

Job Requirements:

  • Minimum 8+ years of experience with a Bachelor's degree or 6+ years of experience with a Master's degree, strong layout knowledge.
  • Proficiency in using Cadence tools (Virtuoso Layout, Layout XL, Cadence Schematic Capture, and verification tools - Calibre & Hercules).
  • Strong layout knowledge in advanced submicron FinFET processes, including TSMC 5nm, 3nm, 2nm, A14 or Samsung lower FinFET nodes.


Key Responsibilities:

  • Understand and apply layout guidelines, new process rules, and technical requirements for high-quality layouts.
  • Manage scheduling, timelines, and layout floor-planning.
  • Ensure end-to-end deliverables are completed with high quality and within the planned schedule.
  • Adapt quickly to new methodologies and communicate effectively with the SOC and library teams.


Skill Set(Memory):

  • Proven ability to manage schedules, timelines, and end-to-end deliverables.
  • Experienced in leading teams, training junior engineers, and documenting layout methodologies and guidelines.
  • Proficient in Cadence tools, including Virtuoso Layout, Layout XL, VXL, EXL, and Cadence Schematic Capture.
  • Strong experience in memory layout design and physical verification (LVS, DRC, ERC, Antenna, Electro Migration) in CMOS processes.
  • Extensive experience with physical verification tools such as Calibre and Hercules (DRC, LVS, ERC, Antenna, and Softcheck Errors).
  • Proficiency in floor-planning, hierarchy layout, and chip integration.
  • Solid understanding of memory architecture and RC requirements, with the ability to plan signal and power routing.
  • Ability to lead teams, train junior engineers, and manage projects effectively.
  • Strong knowledge of memory layout topology; experience with memory compilers is preferred.
  • Knowledge of AI, layout automation, and script/SKILL programming is preferred.
  • Ability to lead new technology reviews to document layout methodologies, flows, and guidelines.
  • Self-reliant with the ability to work both independently and collaboratively.
