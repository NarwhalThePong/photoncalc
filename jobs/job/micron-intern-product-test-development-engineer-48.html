Intern - Product Test Development Engineer

Micron · Boise, ID - Main Site · posted 2026-10-27

Apply on Micron's site →

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

Do you love the interaction of cutting-edge hardware and innovative software solutions? In today's data economy, Micron's memory and storage products are essential to advancing state of the art computing technology. Continued scaling of memory technology presents challenges that require complex and creative testing solutions. As a Core Test Development Engineer in the Test Solutions Engineering department, you will be responsible for the development, debug and maintenance of test programs that help deliver Micron's world-class DRAM portfolio to market. A 'Core' test development engineer is specifically focused on the health and quality of the memory cells [DRAM features 16 billion cells in less than 80mm^2 of area]. This role will leverage AI and Generative AI enabled tools where appropriate to accelerate technical problem solving, software development, data analysis, and engineering productivity. As test engineers, we must constantly evolve and apply the right technology and testing solution, including emerging AI capabilities, to be successful! We need your passion and excitement to help take the team to the next level!


Responsibilities

A successful test development engineer must develop a good understanding of test equipment hardware, device knowledge and software systems.

  • Develop, debug and support test programs from new product introduction phase up through high volume manufacturing
  • Write test programs and support scripts in C++, Python
  • Utilize AI and Generative AI enabled tools to improve software development, test program debug, data analysis, technical problem solving, and engineering productivity
  • Contribute input and feedback to help define next generation 'design for test' capabilities
  • Analyze device and test flow data, including the use of AI enabled methods where appropriate, to make informed test coverage decisions
  • Balance test time objectives while maintaining optimal yield and quality
  • Collaborate with partners in Product Engineering, Fab, Manufacturing, Yield Enhancement, Global Quality, Systems Integration Group, and Equipment Engineering teams to deliver the right test solution

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related discipline.
  • Demonstrated understanding of basic resistance, inductance, and capacitance (R/L/C) circuit concepts, digital systems, logic gates, and CMOS transistor operation.
  • Intermediate or advanced programming capability in C++ or Python.
  • Working knowledge of Unix/Linux environments and basic statistics and data analytics.
  • Demonstrated ability to communicate technical information effectively and collaborate with others.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience applying electrical circuit and CMOS fundamentals to hardware testing, characterization, or troubleshooting.
  • Experience developing, debugging, or maintaining engineering or test software using C++ or Python.
  • Experience analyzing engineering or test data using statistical methods to identify patterns, trends, or anomalies.
  • Experience working with software development and data-analysis workflows in a Unix/Linux environment.
  • Proficiency using AI or Generative AI tools to support technical problem solving, software development, data analysis, or engineering productivity.
