Job Description  

We are looking for Digital Design Engineers with experience developing micro-architecture and implementation of the front-end circuit design, including RTL, synthesis, IP integration for high performance network controllers. The candidate is preferred to have knowledge of communication/interface protocols such as PCI-Express (Gen-3 and above), Ualink, Esun, Ethernet etc.  

Basic Qualifications:  

  • Strong academic and technical background in electrical engineering. A Bachelor’s degree in EE is required, and a Master’s degree is preferred.  
  • Professional attitude with the ability to prioritize a dynamic list of multiple tasks.  
  • Entrepreneurial, open-mind behavior and can-do attitude.  
  • Authorized to work in the US and start immediately.  

Required Experience:  

  • Hands-on, prefer knowledge of high-speed protocols like PCIe, Ualink, Esun, Ethernet, etc.  
  • Proven front end design skill – architecture, RTL, simulations and synthesis  
  • Experience with Synopsys and/or Cadence digital design tools/flows  
  • Knowledge of design for test (DFT)  
  • Familiarity with UVM based design verification  
  • Silicon bring-up and debug experience  

Preferred Experience:  

  • Scripting with Python or other equivalent programming languages.  
  • Development/support for PCIe or Ethernet Switch products.
