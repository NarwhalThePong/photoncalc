Senior Design Engineer

Micron · San Jose, CA · posted 2026-09-27

Apply on Micron's site →

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

As a Senior Design Engineer at Micron, you will play a pivotal role in highly technical teams! You will be responsible for crafting and analyzing digital circuits used in memory product development. This role demands technical expertise and leadership to efficiently implement and optimize NAND circuits. You will collaborate with various internal and external teams to ensure our products meet high standards of performance, quality, and reliability!

Responsibilities

  • Interpret device and system specifications to deliver required functionalities

  • Participate in the conception and development of new circuits

  • Define and design composition architecture specifications for new features.

  • Support the creation of Firmware, schematics and/or RTL blocks

  • Define best known design and verification practices and communicate them to the department

  • Perform Firmware, RTL and synthesized netlist (gate level) verification at block, subsystem, and fullchip level

  • Facilitate netlist bring up to achieve basic functionality

  • Responsible for delivering design simulation including post layout

  • Design verification plans for new and existing features and check desired behavior in Verilog

  • Collaborate with Product Engineering on validation plans and review silicon results

  • Assist in silicon design validation, reticle experiments, qualification, and tape-out revisions as needed

  • Proficiency in using AI tools, creating AI-based agents.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering or Electrical Engineering
  • 5+ years of experience in digital circuit design and development.
  • Proven track record to meet performance, area, and power targets.
  • Experience with RTL and gate-level verification processes.
  • Strong collaboration skills with multi-functional teams.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Master’s degree or Ph.D. in Computer Engineering or Electrical Engineering

  • Experience in technical leadership or project management roles.

  • Knowledge of non-volatile memory technologies, particularly NAND.

  • Familiarity with building architecture and verification guidelines.

  • Excellent documentation and training skills.
