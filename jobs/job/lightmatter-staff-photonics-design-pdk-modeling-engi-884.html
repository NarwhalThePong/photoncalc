We are hiring a Photonics PDK Modeling Engineer to join our team. In this role, you will develop our internal Process Design Kit (PDK). You will act as the architect of our component library, bridging the gap between photonics device design and system-level verification. You will develop robust compact models for active and passive components, ensuring that our simulation environment accurately predicts the behavior of our physical layouts. You will work heavily in Python, EDA tools, applying software engineering rigor to the challenges of photonic hardware design.

Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain a library of pcells using Python-based frameworks. Ensure strict synchronization between physical layout, logical netlists, and behavioral models.
  • Create and verify physics based behavior models for passive and active devices.
  • Design test structure, develop test plans for device FOMs extraction. Work with test engineers to get silicon data.
  • Validate models against lab measurements. Perform data fitting and statistical analysis to align model parameters with real-world silicon performance.
  • Automated Verification: Implement regression testing and Continuous Integration (CI) pipelines to ensure PDK updates do not break legacy designs or connectivity logic.
  • Actively collaborate with design, analog, digital and architecture engineers to deliver high impact tape-outs

Qualifications

  • Master’s +5 years experience or PhD +3 years experience in Electrical Engineering, Photonics, Physics, or related field.
  • Deep understanding of photonic device physics and experience in device simulation. 
  • Experience building behavioral models for circuit simulators (e.g., Lumerical INTERCONNECT, Cadence, Synopsys OptSim, or custom Python solvers).
  • Experienced in Python for modeling, automation, and data analysis. Comfortable with object-oriented programming and scientific libraries (NumPy, SciPy).
  • Python based layout experience.
  • Demonstrated strong problem solving skills for problems without obvious solutions

Preferred Qualifications

  • Proficiency with python-based layout tools such as gdstk, GDSFactory, and KLayout
  • Experience with Python-native circuit simulation ecosystems (e.g., SAX, Photontorch, Simphony).
  • Experience with verilogA/spectre equivalent circuit modeling for photonics device
  • Prior experience in using foundry-provided process design kits (PDKs), adhering to specific design rules and constraints, well understanding of DRC, LVS, etc.
  • Experience working on large-scale designs using industry standard environments such as Cadence Virtuoso
  • Experience in taping out and testing large-scale photonic integrated circuits, including familiarity with relevant testing equipment and methodologies.
  • Possess strong project execution skills, demonstrating the ability to effectively manage multiple design priorities, adhere to project timelines, and meet critical milestones.
