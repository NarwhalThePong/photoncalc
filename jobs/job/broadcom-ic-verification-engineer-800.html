Job Description:

Job duties include:

  • Develop Test plans for all features for Block/Core/SOC and Write Functional coverage for these features.

  • Architect and develop scalable and re-usable test benches, using the framework of the verification methodology

  • Build pseudo-random tests to verify and get full Functional coverage

  • Debug Regression failures, analyze Functional Coverage gaps and improve tests to cover the gaps

  • Lead the documentation of verification strategy including Test plans, Verification Environment, pseudo-random tests, etc. Lead reviews with design/architecture.

  • Drive improvements in Verification in terms of quality and efficiency.

Requirements:

  • Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering or related degree with 8+ years relevant experience or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering or related degree with 6+ years related experience

  • 6+ years of experience in writing and debugging complex test benches

  • Must have deep understanding of all aspects of Verification from building Testbenches, developing Test plans and pseudo-random tests, Functional coverage.

  • Must have proficiency in System Verilog and Verification Methodologies like UVM/VMM/OVM

  • Should have exceptionally good command of Object-Oriented Programming principles.

  • A good understanding of a complex protocol like PCIe or other multi-layered protocol

  • Capability to work independently and deliver.

  • Knowledge of PCI Express protocol is desired
