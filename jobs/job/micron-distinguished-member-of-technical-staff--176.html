Job / Role Summary

The DMTS provides enterprise ‑ level technical leadership, defining photolithography strategy and shaping industry ‑ relevant technology directions. This role leads & influences development & integration strategy, mentors senior engineers, and drives innovation across development teams.

Main Responsibilities

  • Sponsor long-range pathfinding initiatives for future nodes and architectures

  • Establish and steward enterprise ‑ wide, long ‑ term photolithography technology vision and strategic roadmaps.

  • De ‑ risk novel lithography concepts and platforms.

  • Serve as a technical authority across R&D and manufacturing.

  • Influence strategy at executive and industry levels.

Other Responsibilities

  • Mentor next-generation technical leaders

  • Steward intellectual property and standards

  • Set internal technical standards

  • Integrates AI-assisted tools and insights into daily work to improve efficiency, quality, or effectiveness, exercising sound judgment and complying with organizational standards and legal requirements.

  • Contributes to a culture of continuous improvement by identifying, testing, and sharing AI-enabled enhancements within one’s scope of work.

Candidate Profile

Minimum Qualifications / Experience

  • PhD ≥ 1 5 yrs , or Master’s ≥ 1 7 yrs, of relevant experience in
  • Electrical Engineering, Materials Science, Chemical Engineering, Physics, or related field, with demonstrated experience in semiconductor photolithography process development.
  • Industry recognized innovation leader

Must ‑ Have Technical Skills

  • Advanced proficiency in photolithography process & equipment roadmaps ; cutting edge photolithography equipment & metrology tools experience
  • Advanced knowledge in optics, immersion lithography, polarized illumination, and low k 1 imaging
  • Advanced understanding of semiconductor process flows and memory architecture
  • Multi-node, Multi-site program leadership
