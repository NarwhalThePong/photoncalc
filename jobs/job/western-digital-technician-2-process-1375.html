Job Description

Job Description for MRE Engineering Technician

  • Perform all types of DET Guzik & QMT test
  • Ensure no delay in DET testing and that the test results are available on time
  • Buyoff measurement result is shown consistent and stable to meet Test Criteria
  • Compile and upload data to Online SPC timely to meet expected Test TAT
  • Continuously update all test request and other necessary tracking
  • Receive from previous shift and pass down information to next shift clearly
  • Perform head change whenever required
  • Perform tester conversion buyoff and tester troubleshooting
  • Support data package compilation for NPI, SMO, Time Slice and by request.
  • Support reference & head issuance and inventory management
  • Support reference generates and head screening activities
  • Perform and maintain MRE lab 7S and follow all safety and health rules
  • Carry out all jobs/tasks as assigned by Engineers
  • PN1-PN2 cross plants travelling to support whenever required
  • Able to work in cleanroom environment and rotating shift pattern 12 hourly
  • Able to support different shift and work location as assigned

Qualifications

Required :

  • Diploma or Advanced/Higher/Graduate Diploma in Engineering (Mechanical), Engineering (Mechatronic/Electromechanical), Engineering (Electrical/Electronic) or equivalent
  • 1-2 Years working experience in manufacturing environment with machine operation, data collection & analytical skills is preferred.
  • Working experiences in sputter metrology tool and knowledge of Magnetic materials used in HDD would be an added advantage.
  • Understanding of failure mechanisms, ability to isolate & detect process changes causing machine alarms.

Preferred :

  • Proficient in MS Windows Software (Word/Excel/Power Point/Outlook). Macro in excel would be an added advantage
  • Effective team player with proven relationship building & communication skills, including cross-functionally with Production, Process, Yield, and Quality.

SKILLS:

  • Junior/Senior Level Process Technician
  • Problem Solving – Proficiency in troubleshooting process controls, process adjustments, data readback from machine, control limits.
  • Communication - Ability to communicate technical information effectively to colleagues and engineers.
  • Safety – Strong understanding of safety protocols and regulations in the manufacturing environment.
  • Teamwork – Ability to work collaboratively with engineering, production, and quality teams.
  • Adaptability - Proficiency in handling emergencies and unexpected equipment failures.
