Role Summary

The Staff Engineer owns and delivers wet process module execution for advanced NAND, translating device and integration requirements into robust process windows and control strategies, and driving cross ‑ functional actions to close yield, reliability, and cost gaps. Holds stron g module ownership , leading complex experiments and driving cross ‑ functional closure of process issues. Acts as a technical contributor who can lead teams/sites/suppliers on complex projects within their module scope.

Main Responsibilities

Process Development & Margin Improvement

  • Own process characterization and recipe/process optimization to meet yield/quality requirements for advanced NAND technologies.

  • Quantify benefits of new chemistries, process sequences, or equipment changes and drive early adoption to improve margin and manufacturability.

  • Independently lead experiments to widen process windows and validate next ‑ node solutions.

Cross ‑ functional Integration & Troubleshooting

  • Lead cross ‑ functional troubleshooting and integration activities to improve yield and quality; translate future device needs into process requirements.

  • Coordinate with global TD peers (including Boise) on node choices and portability/transfer readiness.

Innovation / IP / Supplier Engagement

  • Drive portions of research, hardware evaluations, and novel application testing; contribute to patents/technical papers where applicable.

  • Lead or represent wet process in supplier discussions supporting breakthrough solutions.

Monitoring, Control & Documentation (Governance)

  • Identify and drive process simplification opportunities; integrate best ‑ known manufacturing methods into early development.

  • Design and implement advanced monitoring and control methodologies within module scope.

  • Lead teams/sites/suppliers in quality/yield improvement meetings and ensure clear technical documentation and compliance with IP policy.
