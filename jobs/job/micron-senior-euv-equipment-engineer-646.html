Senior EUV Equipment Engineer

Micron · Boise, ID - ID1 · posted 2026-10-20

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

Our team enables the technologies that power the future of semiconductor innovation. Operating at the forefront of Extreme Ultraviolet (EUV) lithography, we collaborate across process engineering, manufacturing, facilities, and supplier organizations to deliver world-class equipment performance that supports Micron's technology roadmap. Through advanced engineering practices, data analytics, and AI-enabled solutions, we drive the capability, reliability, and scalability required for next-generation semiconductor manufacturing.

 

As a Senior EUV Equipment Engineer, you will serve as a technical leader responsible for maximizing the performance, reliability, and capability of EUV lithography systems. This role plays a critical part in advancing semiconductor technology through equipment optimization, systematic problem solving, supplier engagement, and cross-functional collaboration. You will partner closely with manufacturing, process engineering, facilities, and equipment suppliers to sustain operational excellence, support technology development, and drive continuous improvement initiatives.

 

Responsibilities

  • Own the performance, reliability, availability, and productivity of assigned EUV lithography systems, driving improvements in uptime, throughput, and manufacturing efficiency.
  • Lead equipment installations, upgrades, qualifications, preventive maintenance strategies, and major maintenance activities to ensure world-class operational performance.
  • Troubleshoot complex mechanical, electrical, vacuum, optical, automation, and software-related issues while partnering with suppliers, including ASML, to implement corrective actions and long-term solutions.
  • Analyze equipment and process data to identify improvement opportunities, reduce defectivity, improve imaging performance, and prevent future failures through structured root cause analysis.
  • Leverage AI-assisted analytics, AI-enabled monitoring systems, and data-driven methodologies to support predictive maintenance, anomaly detection, operational optimization, and engineering efficiency.
  • Effectively communicate technical findings and recommendations to engineering, manufacturing, and management teams.
  • Must be flexible to support shift rotations and varying schedules as business needs require.

 

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Physics, Materials Science, Chemical Engineering, or a related technical field.
  • 3+ years of semiconductor equipment engineering, process engineering, manufacturing engineering, or related semiconductor industry experience.
  • Demonstrated experience troubleshooting and improving complex semiconductor manufacturing equipment and systems.
  • Proven ability to analyze technical data, solve complex problems, and implement measurable performance improvements.
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills with experience working across cross-functional engineering and manufacturing teams.

 

Preferred Qualifications

  • Direct experience with ASML EUV lithography systems in a semiconductor manufacturing environment.
  • Expertise in overlay control, focus performance, imaging optimization, scanner matching, defectivity reduction, contamination control, and lithography process performance.
  • Experience with equipment monitoring systems, automation, scripting, data analytics, and AI-enabled engineering solutions.
  • Knowledge of semiconductor facilities infrastructure supporting EUV operations, including vacuum systems, specialty gases, purification systems, and abatement technologies.
  • Experience applying AI-assisted analytics, Artificial Intelligence tools, or Agentic AI solutions for predictive maintenance, root cause analysis, anomaly detection, yield improvement, or operational optimization.
