Pluggable Modem Optical Test Engineer

Ciena is seeking a Pluggable Modem Optical Test Engineer to contribute to the quality, performance, and manufacturability of optical communication products. This role plays a key part in executing optical test activities, resolving production issues, and supporting product lifecycle initiatives in collaboration with cross-functional teams. The position contributes to reliable product delivery through data-driven testing, analysis, and continuous process improvement.


How you will make an impact:

• Implement optical test plans and procedures for pluggable modem products in accordance with technical specifications.

• Resolve factory technical issues and provide guidance for product rework activities.

• Collaborate with design, manufacturing, and product development teams to address optical performance issues.

• Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives related to manufacturing optical test processes.

• Participate in new product introduction and ongoing product lifecycle support activities.

• Document test results, generate reports, and communicate findings to cross-functional teams.

• Evaluate product repairability and serviceability from an optical testing perspective.


The must haves:

• Education: Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Optical Engineering, Physics, or a related field.

• Experience: 2+ years of experience in optical test development and execution.

• Application of optical communication principles and technologies.

• Utilization of optical test equipment including optical spectrum analyzers, power meters, and tunable lasers.

• Experience with manufacturing processes and quality control methodologies.

• Knowledge of product development and product lifecycle management (PLM) concepts.

• Thai language required. Fluent English writing and speaking required.


Nice to haves:

• Programming experience with MATLAB.

• Programming experience with Python.

• Adaptability to evolving project requirements and technological advancements.

• Problem-solving abilities applied to technical challenges.

• Detail-oriented approach to testing and reporting accuracy.

• Experience working within a collaborative team environment.
