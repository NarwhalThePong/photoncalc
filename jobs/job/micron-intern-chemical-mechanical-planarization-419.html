Project Title
AI-Enabled Chemical Mechanical Planarization Process Optimization and Equipment Characterization

Project Description
The intern will undertake a structured engineering project focused on Chemical Mechanical Planarization process optimization, equipment characterization, and manufacturing excellence within a semiconductor manufacturing environment.

The project will provide practical exposure to process and metrology data analysis, statistical methods, process capability assessment, material or equipment qualification, and Artificial Intelligence-Enabled engineering workflows. The intern will collaborate with cross-functional engineering teams to develop and evaluate a solution that improves process performance, yield, cost efficiency, engineering productivity, or data accessibility.

  • Generate engineering recommendations for process optimization, equipment characterization, or qualification activities.
