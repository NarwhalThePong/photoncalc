ABOUT THE ROLE

Eliyan is the chiplet interconnect pioneer behind NuLink, the industry's most efficient chiplet-to-chiplet PHY technology. With Series C funding from leading hyperscalers and AI infrastructure providers, we are scaling our digital design organization to support multiple concurrent IP programs across advanced process nodes. 

We are looking for an engineer who can own the intersection of design quality, automation, and design integration. In this role you will define and enforce signoff criteria for lint, CDC, and synthesis across all active programs, build the waiver management and tracking infrastructure, and develop AI-augmented tooling that turns thousands of tool violations into actionable, designer-ready reviews. You will report into the digital design team and work directly alongside RTL designers.

Design quality and automation are the core of this role. IP and chip-level integration ownership is scope you can grow into as you ramp — we're looking for someone who wants it.

    KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

    Design Quality & Signoff

  • Define and maintain lint, CDC, and synthesis elaboration signoff criteria across all active design programs.
  • Build and own the waiver management system — structured flow with owner attribution, justification, expiration, and audit trail.  
  • Analyze synthesis results for area, timing, and power regressions; trace QoR anomalies to specific RTL changes.  
  • Collaborate with project design leads to develop and maintain project-specific rule decks, synthesis constraints, and signoff checklists.  

    • IP & Chip Integration

  • Own RTL integration of digital IP into PHY and chip-level hierarchies — instantiation, parameterization, hierarchy assembly, and interface consistency across IP boundaries.
  • Build automated integration checks: top-level connectivity, tie-off and floating-input detection, clock and reset connectivity, and interface consistency between IP and SoC.
  • Automate register map and interface collateral generation (e.g. PeakRDL, IP-XACT) and keep RTL, firmware headers, and documentation in sync.
  • Own IP release packaging and delivery — versioned collateral, dependency tracking, and reproducible builds from a tagged release, with handoff evidence for internal and customer deliveries.
  • Run lint, CDC, RDC, and elaboration at full-chip scope, and drive cross-IP and integration-level findings to closure with the owning designers.  

    • Flow Automation and CIC

  • Own the Jenkins-based CI pipeline for RTL quality: lint, CDC, RDC, elaboration, synthesis, and STA.
  • Build the dev-to-release promotion pipeline with automated gating, clean-run qualification, and handoff evidence generation.
  • Develop per-project dashboards for QoR tracking, regression visibility, and release readiness.  

    • AI-Augmented Tooling 

    • Develop LLM-based violation triage pipelines that classify lint and CDC output, suppress known-benign patterns, and surface real issues with context.  
    • Build automated pull requests with violations pre-filtered, grouped, and annotated for designer review.
    • I mplement spec-to-RTL consistency checking and CI-integrated documentation generation using LLM-driven pipelines.  
    • Benchmark AI output quality, measure false-negative rates, and establish guardrails the design team can trust.  

    MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS

  • Bachelors, Masters or PhD in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
  • 5+ years in ASIC or SoC design or design automation, with hands-on experience running and analyzing lint, CDC, or synthesis tools.
  • Familiarity with Lint/CDC/RDC tools like VC SpyGlass or QuestaLint/CDC. 
  • Familiarity with Synthesis tools like Genus or Fusion Compiler.
  • Working knowledge of SystemVerilog and the RTL front-end flow — able to read violations, assess severity, and disposition findings.
  • Proficiency in Python, Tcl, shell scripting, Git, Linux, and Makefiles.
  • Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines (Jenkins, GitLab CI, or GitHub Actions).
  • Authorization to work in the United States or Canada; visa sponsorship may be considered for exceptional candidates.
  • On-site Monday through Friday at our Bay Area, Vancouver, or Toronto office.

    PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

  • Experience building waiver management systems, violation databases, or design quality dashboards.
  • Experience with register map automation and IP packaging standards (PeakRDL, SystemRDL, or IP-XACT).
  • Knowledge of UPF and power intent.
  • Knowledge of Static Timing Analysis.
  • Practical experience with LLM APIs or agent frameworks (e.g., Anthropic Claude, OpenAI) applied in a production or team-facing context.
  • Hands-on experience integrating and releasing digital IP — hierarchy assembly, connectivity closure, versioned collateral, and handoff to internal or external consumers.
  • Exposure to PHY, SerDes, die-to-die interconnect, or chiplet-based SoC design.
  • Open-source contributions to design automation, hardware verification, or developer tooling.
