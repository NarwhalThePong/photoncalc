Job Description

  • Monitor equipment performance, identify issues, and fix tools.
  • Certify and train maintenance, chemical technicians, and equipment tool owners.
  • Establish written procedures and specs for maintenance technicians.
  • Work with process engineering team to provide resolution/reduction of rework and scrap issues.
  • Lead investigations for equipment-related excursions and provide a solution.
  • Provide 8D and executive summary to management and various department stakeholders.
  • Provide safety recommendations on equipment in addition to ensuring tools meet QA site requirements.
  • Utilization of Micro-computer applications – Excel, Power Point and industry standard system reporting.
  • Ability to understand electrical schematics and trouble-shoot equipment.
  • Understand Photo equipment process, optics, electrical, mechanical and robotics knowledge.
  • Provide equipment training for the maintenance team.
  • Utilize Ai tools for equipment improvement/up time and efficiency in day-to-day work.
  • Provide equipment performance reports, 8D resolutions and follow-through on action items.
  • Establish parts inventory, research second source options and engineer parts if necessary to support equipment.
  • Work with equipment vendors to ensure tool performance and support as well as escalation of line down situations.

Qualifications

REQUIRED

  • MUST HAVE THESE Qualifications
  • Minimum 3 to 7 years of experience in Photo equipment engineering, preferably in magnetic head manufacturing.
  • BS degree in electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, physics, chemistry, materials science, or a related field.
  • Must have experience in Photolithography equipment tools – ASML XT Twin Scan, ASML PAS5500, Canon and Nikon Steppers.
  • In-depth knowledge of electronics, electro-mechanics, resist, develop, optics, robotics and Photo tools.
  • Analytical, logical, and data-driven, and demonstrated ability in fixing and providing equipment resolutions. 
  • Detail-oriented and willingness to learn and thrive in a cross-functional manufacturing environment.
  • Able to work and interact with various interdepartmental engineering and other support organizations.
  • Demonstrated ability to manage projects with set milestone and timeline, and ability to execute under minimal supervision.
  • A responsible team-player.
  • Excellent communication skills, both written and oral.
