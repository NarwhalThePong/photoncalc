The Engineer, Customer Validation supports the development and maintenance of automated validation infrastructure used to assess Enterprise SSD interoperability, functionality, and reliability across customer-relevant storage environments. This role contributes to cross-platform test automation, characterization of test environments, and validation of SSD products across different platforms and OSes. Success in this role requires structured problem-solving, and collaboration with cross-functional teams to improve validation coverage, product quality, and release readiness.


Main Responsibilities 

  • Develop, integrate, and maintain interfaces for new test equipment, validation tools, and automation frameworks.
  • Define, plan, and execute automated test strategies that improve coverage, scalability, and validation efficiency.
  • Collaborate with firmware, hardware, test, and product teams to develop robust validation solutions aligned with product features and customer requirements.
  • Troubleshoot SSD, software, and platform integration issues by reproducing failures, identifying root causes, and driving corrective actions.
  • Evaluate and enhance validation methodologies to improve defect detection and shift failure discovery earlier in the development lifecycle.
  • Identify and implement automation opportunities that improve test scalability, repeatability, and execution efficiency.

 

Other Responsibilities 

  • Maintain clear documentation of test methodologies, workflows, validation results, and lessons learned to support knowledge retention and traceability.
  • Support Agile development activities including planning, execution tracking, and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Collaborate effectively with stakeholders across global sites and time zones.

 

AI Responsibilities 

  • Integrates AI-assisted tools and insights into daily work to improve efficiency, quality, or effectiveness, exercising good judgment and aligning with organizational standards and legal requirements.
  • Contributes to a culture of continuous improvement by identifying, testing, and sharing AI-enabled enhancements within one’s scope of work.

 

Qualifications 

  • B.Sc. or higher in Electronic/Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science or equivalent. 

 

Technical Skills 

  • Python programming and object-oriented software design principles.
  • Working knowledge of Linux and/or Windows operating systems.
  • Structured problem analysis, root cause investigation, and corrective action implementation.

 

Soft Skills 

  • Structured problem-solving and analytical thinking.
  • Clear technical communication and documentation skills.
  • Cross-functional collaboration and teamwork.
  • Adaptability in a dynamic and rapidly changing environment.
  • Ability to manage priorities across multiple activities and stakeholders.
  • Growth mindset with a willingness to learn, seek feedback, and continuously improve technical and professional skills.
  • Positive and resilient attitude when facing challenging problems, setbacks, or changing priorities.

 

Preferred Skills 

  • Experience with Windows or Linux kernel, driver, or low-level operating system components.
  • Experience with datacenter server platforms, storage systems, and hardware validation/debug activities is highly desirable.
  • Knowledge of operating system APIs including process control, memory management, and device driver interactions.
  • Understanding storage systems, NVMe architecture, PCIe, firmware, drivers, CPU, memory, and operating system interactions is an added advantage.
  • Experience working within Agile product development environments.
