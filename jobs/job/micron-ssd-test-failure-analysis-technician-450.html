Responsibilities:

Manufacturing Test Support
- Perform failure analysis for New Product Introduction (NPI) and High-Volume Manufacturing (HVM) test failures and identify root cause/defect location in a timely manner
- First contact point and first-level failure analysis for manufacturing test-related issues
- Prioritize & expedite failure analysis for high priority/DDT lots to meet output goals for business demand
- Escalate & work with engineers on issues that require engineer dispo/response, eg: mtSBL trigger, test high fallout etc
- Communicate shift pass-down on test matters
- Assist the engineering team to identify and reject test failure need for engineer checkout for further engineering eval/investigation
- Support special engineering evaluation activity request approved by FA supervisor

FA WIP Management
- Monitor and maintain FA WIP to meet the target set by FA supervisor for every shift

Test Equipment/Lab Management
- Perform yearly Production Tester GR&R and Product GRR
- Work with PE to set up a new FA debug bench for New Product Introduction (NPI)
- Perform preventive maintenance and calibration for FA debug tools/bench
- Maintain 6S & ESD compliances in FA lab

Manufacturing Test Issues & Yield Monitoring
- Monitor test failures/issues and feedback on abnormal new trends of test issues/failures to the engineering team
- Escalate to equipment engineers on tester software/hardware issues causing poor yield

Continuous Improvement Projects for Cost/Cycle Time/NPI
- Support test yield continuous improvement projects for cost reduction.
- Identify opportunities to improve FA process for cycle time improvement.
- Assist NPI FA analysis from PE special engineering request.

- Leverage AI-enabled tools and data analytics solutions to improve failure analysis efficiency, cycle time, and decision-making.
- Propose and execute improvement projects for FA/Test to desired results.

- Contribute ideas and continuous improvement projects that incorporate digital, automation, or AI technologies to improve FA/Test processes.
- Collaborate with equipment engineers on projects to improve overall test equipment efficiency and effectiveness.

Digital & AI Utilization
- Utilize approved AI and digital productivity tools to improve troubleshooting, data analysis, documentation, and workflow efficiency.
- Maintain awareness of emerging AI technologies and participate in training initiatives to improve digital and AI proficiency.
- Ensure responsible and compliant use of AI tools in accordance with company policies on confidentiality, security, and intellectual property protection.

Requirements:

  • Diploma/Advanced Diploma in Electrical & Electronics/Computer engineering,
  • Working experience in SSD test failure analysis would be an advantage.
  • Basic knowledge or experience with AI-assisted tools such as Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, or similar technologies.
  • Experience in statistical process control system.
  • Experience working in Linux operating system.
  • Ability to work on 12-hours rotating shift.
  • Understand and able to communicate in English with good interpersonal skills in building positive working relationship.
