Job Description:


Who We Are — Physical Design Team in APD

Broadcom’s ASIC Products Division (APD) is the global leader in custom high-performance silicon solutions. We partner with the world’s most innovative technology companies—spanning hyper-scale cloud providers, AI pioneers, and tier-1 networking infrastructure leaders—to co-design and manufacture complex, custom ASICs that power the modern digital backbone.


The Physical Design team within APD is the world-class execution engine behind the industry's most advanced custom silicon. We push the limits of silicon technology by combining deep architectural expertise, advanced IP, cutting edge physical design methodologies in 3nm/2nm nodes, and advanced 2.5D/3D chiplet integration to solve the most demanding compute and bandwidth challenges.We transform ultra-complex architectures into fully realized, high-yielding, first-pass silicon success.


Broadcom is searching for an Senior Physical Design Engineer to join the ASIC Products Division. This position involves working with the latest technology to continue driving next generation Artificial Intelligence and PCIe Switch Products. More specifically, this position will require in-depth knowledge and expertise in all Physical Design aspects of taking RTL to silicon tape-out.


Key Responsibilities

Full-Flow Implementation: Lead block-level and top-level implementation from floorplanning to GDSII stream-out using Cadence Innovus

Advanced Node Execution: Drive floorplanning, placement, CTS, routing, and timing/power optimization on advanced technology nodes (5nm, 3nm, 2nm) using multi-patterning and complex metal stacks.

Timing & Power Closure: Perform multi-corner multi-mode (MCMM) static timing analysis (STA), signal integrity (SI) optimization, and power grid optimization (static/dynamic IR-drop, EM) for signoff using PrimeTime, Voltus, or RedHawk.

Qualifications & Requirements

Education: B.S. or M.S. degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related technical field.

Experience: 8+ years of hands-on physical design experience successfully taping out complex high-frequency, ultra-large-scale networking or AI ASICs.

Deep Sub-Micron Expertise: Direct experience executing tapeouts in TSMC advanced nodes (N7, N5, N3) or equivalent foundry processes.

EDA Tool Mastery: High proficiency in major physical design tool flows:

P&R: Cadence Innovus

Timing/STA: Synopsys PrimeTime or Cadence Tempus

Extraction & IR/EM: StarRC / QRC, RedHawk

DRC/LVS: Siemens Calibre

Automation Skills: Advanced TCL, Python, and Unix shell scripting for flow automation and database manipulation.

Communication: Excellent technical leadership, problem-solving, and cross-functional communication skills.

Preferred Qualifications

Strong background in high-speed clock network implementation (H-tree, mesh, low-power CTS).

Experience with power-aware implementation flows (UPF/CPF, multi-voltage domains, clock/power gating).
