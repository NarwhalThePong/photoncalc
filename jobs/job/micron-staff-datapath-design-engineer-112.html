Staff Datapath Design Engineer

Micron · San Jose, CA · posted 2026-10-26

Apply on Micron's site →

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

Lead the design, analysis, and optimization of digital and analog circuits for new memory products, ensuring outstanding performance, power, and area (PPA) optimization for NAND memory products. Define architecture and chip plans, including placement and routing optimization, to achieve ambitious goals. Design high-speed Datapath circuits at the block level, model parasitic, and supervise layout to ensure world-class performance. Perform circuit simulations using industry-standard simulators to validate functionality and performance. Collaborate with domain experts and partners to bring together and review the final design details, ensuring designs operate within safe reliability operating ranges. Contribute to the development of new product opportunities by assisting with the overall design, layout, and optimization of memory/ logic/ analog circuits. Perform verification processes with modeling and simulation using industry standard simulators. Contribute to cross group communication to work towards standardization and group success.


Employer will accept a Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science or a related field and 5 years of progressive, post-baccalaureate experience in the job offered or in a related occupation.


Position requires:
1. High-speed circuit performance, power and area optimization
2. Circuit simulation with System Verilog, hspice or other spice simulators
3. Silicon data analysis and circuit change capability
4. Circuit Reliability verification for Aging, Electromigration & self-heating
5. Layout review and STARRC extraction
