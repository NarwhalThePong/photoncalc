Job Summary:
A Fab Support Engineer responsible for sustaining and maintaining critical fab support systems, including vacuum pumps, point-of-use scrubbers, and chillers/heat exchangers, to ensure stable manufacturing operations. Leads and manages a team of technicians to provide compressed shift coverage for production lines, while establishing and improving process conditions, interlocks, and safety systems for both support and main tools. Drives process capability upgrades and cost reduction initiatives, manages operational budgets, and leads process management projects utilizing control and monitoring systems such as RMS and SCADA. Evaluates, introduces, and supports new OEM equipment and materials, and performs abnormal analysis to implement continuous improvement and reliability enhancements.

Main Responsibilities: 

  • Sustaining and Maintaining Fab Support Tools (Vacuum Pump, Point of use Scrubbers, Chillers/Heat exchanger)
  • Lead a team of technicians for compressed shift coverage for manufacturing line
  • Establish and improve process condition and technology on Support and Main tool system setup (interlocks and safety setup)
  • Upgrade process capability and reduce production cost. Control department spending for daily operation.
  • Establish and modify process management projects, understand the control and safety systems (RMS, SCADA, monitoring dashboards)
  • Evaluation, promotion and planning of new equipment / materials. New OEM equipment introduction and operation/maintenance.
  • Abnormal analysis and improvement.
  • Integrates AI-assisted tools and insights into daily work to improve efficiency, quality, or effectiveness, exercising sound judgment and complying with organizational standards and legal requirements.
  • Contributes to a culture of continuous improvement by identifying, testing, and sharing AI-enabled enhancements within one’s scope of work.

Job Requirements, Qualifications/Experience:

  • Bachelor’s or advanced degree in Engineering or Science is required
  • Strong analytical, logical, and critical thinking skills 
  • Effective communicator, able to collaborate across all levels
