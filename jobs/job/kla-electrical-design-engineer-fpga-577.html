Group/Division

The Semiconductor Products and Customers (Semi PC) business unit designs, builds and sells KLA’s product portfolio to help chip manufacturers meet high-quality standards for technologies like AI, data centers, automotive and electronic devices. Our inspection, metrology, and analytics systems detect and monitor defects across logic and memory chips, specialty semiconductors, wafers, reticles, advanced packaging, process equipment and materials. We also develop technologies for specialty processes, IC substrates, and PCB manufacturing, including etch and deposition, component inspection, and imaging and analytics. The Surfscan® division develops unpatterned wafer inspection systems that detect nanoscale particles and surface defects early in semiconductor manufacturing. Its systems monitor wafer quality across a wide range of applications, enabling faster issue identification and improved process control.

What You'll Do

In this role, you will play a key part in advancing business priorities by delivering high-impact work across your area of expertise.


The KLA Surfscan Electrical Engineering team is looking for a talented Electrical Design Engineer to help develop next-generation inspection systems. This role focuses on high speed PCB design, FPGA development, and system integration.

The ideal candidate is a motivated engineer who can execute and drive projects from requirements and concept development through design, implementation, testing, integration, and troubleshooting. Working within a multidisciplinary teams, the engineer will contribute to PCB and FPGA/DSP designs, high-speed data acquisition, motion and system control, and system-level validation to deliver innovative and reliable solutions.

Main Responsibilities

· Design, simulate, implement, verify, and optimize high-speed FPGA solutions, including high-bandwidth data acquisition, real-time data processing, and high-speed communication interfaces.

· Perform bench-level, subsystem-level, and system-level integration, testing, debugging, and troubleshooting of FPGA-based hardware.

· Develop test fixtures, test procedures, and automated test scripts for PCB prototypes, FPGA functional testing, qualification, and characterization.

· Participate in technical design reviews, including PCB and FPGA design reviews, and provide constructive feedback to improve design quality, reliability, and manufacturability.

· Follow the DFX design process to generate and maintain design documentation, test reports, and engineering release packages for manufacturing.

· Collaborate closely with systems, software, electrical, mechanical, and manufacturing engineers to ensure successful product integration and validation.

Qualifications

· Experience in FPGA development using AMD Xilinx or Intel (Altera) platforms, including Verilog and/or VHDL design, simulation, synthesis, implementation, and hardware debugging.

· Experience with high-speed digital hardware, FPGA, and embedded processor development.

· Solid understanding of FPGA-to-PCB interface design, clocking, timing analysis, timing closure, and constraint development.

· Hands-on experience with hardware and firmware integration, board bring-up, debugging, and system-level testing of prototype hardware.

· Experience designing FPGA-based PCBs, including power, clocking, configuration, high-speed interfaces, signal integrity, and board bring-up, is a plus.

· Familiarity with engineering development tools such as OrCAD, Xilinx Vivado/ISE, Intel Quartus, Synplify, Questa/ModelSim, Python, and MATLAB.

· Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to diagnose and troubleshoot complex issues in semiconductor equipment and instrumentation systems.

· Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to work effectively as a team player in a multidisciplinary engineering environment.

· Demonstrated ownership, urgency, and accountability, with a strong commitment to delivering high-quality engineering solutions..

Minimum Qualifications

  • BSEE with 3+ years, or MSEE with 2+ years or PhD EE in electrical engineering
