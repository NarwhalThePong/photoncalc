Metrology & Inspection

  • Operate and maintain advanced metrology tools including CD, Overlay, Thin Film, Defect Inspection, and Optical/Electrical Measurement systems.
  • Perform wafer measurements and inspections to monitor critical process parameters and detect process excursions.
  • Support equipment setup, qualification, calibration, preventive maintenance, and troubleshooting activities.
  • Ensure measurement integrity and sustain tool performance through statistical monitoring and continuous improvement.

Process Optimization & Yield Improvement

  • Analyze process and metrology data to identify opportunities for yield enhancement, defect reduction, and productivity improvement.
  • Apply SPC, FMEA, 8D, Root Cause Analysis, and Failure Analysis methodologies to investigate and resolve manufacturing issues.
  • Lead or participate in process optimization projects to improve quality, reliability, cycle time, and manufacturing efficiency.
  • Support new process qualification, baseline establishment, and process control plan development.

Data Analytics & AI-Driven Manufacturing

  • Utilize data analytics, statistical modeling, and visualization tools to identify process trends, abnormal signatures, and yield loss drivers.
  • Leverage AI and machine learning solutions to improve defect classification, process monitoring, virtual metrology, and predictive analytics.
  • Develop workflow automation solutions to reduce manual engineering effort and improve operational efficiency.
  • Apply Generative AI tools (e.g., Microsoft Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, and AI Agents) to accelerate engineering analysis, report generation, knowledge management, and decision-making processes.
  • Drive digital transformation initiatives through integration of AI-assisted workflows, automated data pipelines, and intelligent manufacturing solutions.

Cross-functional Collaboration

  • Collaborate with Process Integration, Manufacturing, Equipment Engineering, Quality, Data Science, and Supplier teams to drive continuous improvement.

Documentation & Reporting
