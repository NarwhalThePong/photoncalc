Lead Logic Design Engineer 

Experience

6-10 years

Job Summary

We are seeking a Lead Logic Design Engineer with deep expertise in micro-architecture and RTL design to lead the development of complex IPs/subsystems. The candidate will provide technical ownership across architecture, microarchitecture, RTL implementation, integration, and silicon bring-up.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead RTL/microarchitecture development for complex connectivity IP/subsystems.
  • Translate specifications and system requirements into robust hardware architecture.
  • Design high-performance and scalable logic for controllers/subsystems.
  • Work across connectivity IP / subsystem layers including Transaction, Data Link, and Physical interface integration as applicable to the role.
  • Develop and review RTL using SystemVerilog/Verilog.
  • Develop robust mechanisms for flow control, ordering, buffering, arbitration, error handling, and reliability.
  • Drive performance, latency, area, and power optimization.
  • Collaborate with verification teams to develop verification strategies and debug complex failures.
  • Work with SoC integration and physical design teams to ensure successful implementation.
  • Lead technical reviews and mentor senior engineers.
  • Support post-silicon debug and root-cause analysis.

Required Skills

  • Extensive experience in complex connectivity IPs such as PCIE, UALink, Ethernet, CXL, UCIe.
  • Strong RTL design experience using SystemVerilog/Verilog.
  • Deep knowledge of PCIe architecture and protocol.
  • Strong understanding of high-speed data paths, FIFOs, arbitration, flow control, and error handling.
  • Experience with simulation, lint, CDC, synthesis, and timing considerations.
  • Strong debugging and problem-solving skills.

Preferred

  • Experience of working in switching silicon.
  • Experience of working on entire cycle from concept to silicon production.
