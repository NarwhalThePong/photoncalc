Optical Bonding Technician

Atom Computing · Boulder, CO · posted 2026-10-15

Apply on Atom Computing's site →

At Atom Computing, we build quantum computers using arrays of optically trapped neutral atoms that will empower customers to achieve unprecedented computational breakthroughs. Join a world-class team of scientists, engineers, and business professionals to advance the state-of-the-art in quantum computing.

We are seeking an Optical Bonding Technician to perform precision bonding of optical and optomechanical assemblies for neutral-atom quantum computers. This hands-on role prepares surfaces, applies and cures adhesives, and inspects completed bonds using approved procedures and engineer-defined alignment references.

Put your precision craftsmanship to work on the optical assemblies inside a quantum computer. As an early member of Atom's new manufacturing organization, you'll work closely with optical engineers and help refine the bonding practices that future builds will rely on.

Responsibilities

  • Verify components, surface condition, approved materials, and current bonding instructions before beginning an assembly.
  • Clean and prepare optics and mating surfaces, protecting coatings and preventing contamination or damage during handling.
  • Prepare and dispense approved adhesives; maintain material identification, shelf-life controls, and required process records.
  • Set up bonding fixtures and position components with optical engineers, preserving the required alignment and bond geometry.
  • Execute controlled curing procedures and record specified settings while protecting component position and optical cleanliness.
  • Inspect completed bonds for workmanship and visible defects; support post-cure alignment checks and report deviations to Engineering and Quality.
  • Maintain component and adhesive traceability, completed work records, and inspection results; perform rework only under approved instructions.
  • Maintain clean bonding stations, dispensing tools, fixtures, and material storage; support engineer-led process improvements and follow laboratory and chemical-safety procedures.

Experience & Education

  • Four-year degree in science, applied research, or technology or equivalent certificates/industry experience
  • 3+ years of hands-on precision optical bonding or closely related adhesive-based microassembly using controlled procedures.
  • Experience with optical cleaning, microscope-assisted work, precision dispensing, fixturing, controlled curing, and visual inspection.
  • Experience bonding coated optics or optomechanical assemblies for lasers, quantum hardware, or scientific instruments preferred.

Qualifications

  • Strong manual precision when handling small optical components, dispensing adhesives, and working with microscopes and assembly fixtures.
  • Ability to read assembly drawings and follow bonding instructions, including orientation, protected surfaces, and inspection points.
  • Practical knowledge of adhesive handling, shelf-life controls, curing records, and contamination prevention.
  • Ability to recognize bond defects, movement, or optical surface damage and report concerns before work continues.
  • Accurate process documentation and effective teamwork with optical engineers, process engineers, and Quality.
  • Capable of lifting and moving objects that weigh up to 25 pounds
