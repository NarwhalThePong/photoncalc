Staff Engineer, ASIC Analog Design

Micron · Bengaluru, India · posted 2026-10-22

Apply on Micron's site →

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

Key Responsibilities

  • Be part of a highly skilled and challenging high-speed PHY design team working on advanced technology nodes (12nm and below).
  • Own analog sub-blocks within the PHY and drive specification, design, simulation, reliability, and mixed-mode verification.
  • Leverage AI-enabled tools to improve productivity and decision-making.
  • Drive layout implementation through mask designers while ensuring compliance with process rules and DFM requirements.
  • Participate in design reviews with internal teams and customers, explaining design choices and robustness.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams across multiple global locations.
  • Create IP EDA views including Behavioral models, Verilog-A, timing views, and abstracts.
  • Participate in silicon bring-up, characterization, and correlation of silicon results with models and simulations.
  • Mentor and guide junior engineers.
  • Competitive compensation and equal opportunity workplace.

Required Skills

  • Strong analog/mixed-signal CMOS circuit design experience in deep sub-micron technologies (2nm to 28nm).
  • Experience in High-Speed Memory Interfaces (DDR3/4/5, LPDDR5/6, GDDR5/6/7) or SerDes Interfaces (PCIe, MIPI, HDMI, USB, SATA, etc.).
  • Full ownership experience of at least one critical PHY sub-block such as:
    • PLL
    • DLL
    • CDR
    • High-speed Receiver with Equalization
    • Transmitter Front-End
    • High-Speed Data Path
  • Deep understanding of analog and mixed-signal design concepts with high-speed link experience.
  • Mandatory experience in SerDes and/or Parallel Interfaces.
  • Mandatory experience with TX/RX Equalization techniques (FFE, CTLE, DFE).
  • Experience with CDR and clocking architectures.
  • Hands-on experience in FinFET technologies (10nm and below).
  • Ability to develop block-level specifications based on link budgets.
  • Experience in system-level modeling using Verilog-A and/or Python.
  • Experience working with Packaging and Board teams.
  • Knowledge of ESD design considerations.
  • Experience in lab testing and collaborating with validation teams for data analysis.
  • Experience designing high-speed I/O cells such as DDR, LVDS, HSTL, and CML.
  • Experience with power delivery circuits including Bandgap, LDO, and Bias circuits.
  • Strong understanding of AMS design, advanced CMOS, and FinFET technologies.
  • Knowledge of mismatch analysis, Monte Carlo simulations, transistor-level noise analysis, and deep sub-micron device physics.
  • Experience supervising complex analog layouts and defining IP floorplans.
  • Hands-on experience with industry-standard tools such as Cadence ADE, Spectre, AMS Verification, EM/IR flows, MATLAB, and Calibre.
  • Working knowledge of mixed-mode simulation, signal integrity, and ESD analysis.

Education & Experience

  • M.S./M.Tech or B.S./B.E. in Electronics.
  • Experience Required: 5+ Years.
