Principal SoC Verification Engineer

NXP · Austin (Oakhill, Office) · posted 2026-10-23

Apply on NXP's site →

We are looking for an experienced SoC Verification Engineer to drive verification of next-generation automotive SoCs used in safety-critical applications. The role involves end-to-end ownership of verification planning, testbench development, execution, debug, and sign-off for complex SoC platforms.


Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and execute SoC verification plans and strategies.
  • Build and maintain SystemVerilog/UVM based verification environments.
  • Verify complex SoC features including:
    • ARM-based subsystems
    • Interconnects (AXI/ACE/CHI)
    • DDR, PCIe, Ethernet, Security, and Boot
    • Low Power and Clock/Reset architectures
    • Functional Safety mechanisms (ISO 26262)
  • Drive functional, code, and assertion coverage closure.
  • Debug RTL, testbench, and system-level issues.
  • Collaborate with Architecture, Design, Firmware, Validation, and Safety teams.
  • Support post-silicon debug and customer issue resolution.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in EE, CE, CS, or related field.
  • 5-4 years of experience in SoC/IP verification.
  • Strong expertise in:
    • SystemVerilog, UVM, SVA
    • Functional and code coverage
    • Simulation and debug tools (VCS, Xcelium, Verdi)
  • Experience with ARM-based SoCs and industry-standard protocols (AXI, PCIe, DDR, Ethernet).
  • Strong debugging and problem-solving skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Automotive SoC experience.
  • Knowledge of ISO 26262 Functional Safety.
  • Experience with Low Power (UPF), Formal Verification, GLS, and Emulation.
  • Experience leading technical initiatives and mentoring engineers.
