Intern - ASIC Digital Design

Micron · Minneapolis, MN · posted 2026-10-19

Apply on Micron's site →

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

The ASIC Logic Design Team plays a critical role in developing the custom silicon that powers Micron's industry-leading memory and storage solutions. Our team designs high-speed, high-performance ASICs and memory controller IP, collaborating closely with engineers across design, verification, validation, and product development to bring innovative technologies from concept to production.

As a Digital Design Engineer Intern, you will work alongside experienced engineers to help design and develop next-generation ASIC solutions. This internship provides hands-on exposure to advanced semiconductor technologies and the complete ASIC development lifecycle, including digital, analog, and mixed-signal design. You will have the opportunity to contribute to meaningful projects while building technical expertise in a collaborative and innovative environment.

What You Will Do

  • Support the design and implementation of high-speed digital logic for tester ASICs and memory controller IP.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams including Design Verification, Analog Design, Synthesis, Physical Design, and Lab Validation.
  • Assist with integrating digital designs alongside custom analog circuitry and third-party IP solutions.
  • Participate in design reviews and contribute to technical documentation.
  • Present technical concepts and project updates to engineering teams.
  • Explore and leverage AI-enabled design methodologies, including Generative AI and AI-assisted engineering workflows, to improve productivity and innovation.
  • Learn industry-standard design processes and best practices used in advanced semiconductor development.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor's, Master's, or Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field.
  • Understanding of digital logic design, verification methodologies, and high-speed digital interfaces.
  • Experience with programming or scripting languages such as Python, Linux Shell, or similar.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
  • Ability to work effectively in a collaborative team environment.
  • Must be enrolled in a degree program and not graduate before August 2027.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Exposure to analog and mixed-signal circuit concepts, including PLLs, DLLs, CDRs, CMOS, or CML-based designs.
  • Familiarity with semiconductor design tools used for synthesis, linting, simulation, formal verification, or implementation.
  • Previous academic, research, personal, or internship projects related to digital IC design, computer architecture, or semiconductor technologies.
  • Demonstrated curiosity, initiative, and a passion for learning new technologies.
