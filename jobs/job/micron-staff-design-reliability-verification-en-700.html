Staff Design Reliability Verification Engineer

Micron · Boise, ID - Main Site · posted 2026-10-16

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

Drive and develop reliability verification with design and product teams by planning, overseeing, and developing stress patterns for increased reliability coverage. Be proficient on product reliability requirements and customer usage conditions for different DRAM products. Perform Verification Processes with Modeling and Simulation Using Industry Standard Simulators. Contribute to Cross Group Communication To Work Towards Standardization And Group Success. Working with Marketing, Quality, And Product Engineering Groups To Ensure Accurate Manufacturability Of Product. Proactively Solicit Guidance From Standards, Cad, Modeling, And Verification Groups To Improve The Design Quality. Drive Innovation Into The Future Memory Generations Within A Dynamic Work Environment. Drive customer usage model alignment across different functional teams. Work with technology teams to ensure high-risk reliability mechanisms are evaluated. Be advised on all silicon reliability related issues. Support product teams during silicon validation and qualification phase. Analyze and debug circuit down to device level in relation to degradation mechanism. Develop and improve reliability verification tools and methodologies. Engage in technical forums for continuous improvement of design for reliability methods. Train and mentor junior engineers.


Employer will accept a Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering or related field and 60 months of progressive, post-baccalaureate experience in the job offered or in a related occupation.


Position requires:

1. CMOS operation and circuit design

2. Analog/digital simulation tools such as HSPICE, VerilogHDL, FINESIM, SimVision, or WaveViewer

3. CMOS degradation mechanisms such as NBTI or CHC

4. Reliability modeling and simulation tools

5. Python
