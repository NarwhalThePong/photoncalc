Senior Manager/ Manager - Memory Circuit Design

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

Unique Opportunities

  • Lead the development of next-generation DRAM and LPDDR memory technologies that enable AI, mobile, automotive, and high-performance computing applications.
  • Drive design innovation across memory sub-systems including array, datapath, sensing, repair, redundancy, IO, power management, and reliability features.
  • Influence product architecture and technical roadmaps in collaboration with global engineering teams and technology leaders.
  • Build and mentor a team of highly capable engineers while shaping the future design capabilities of the organization.
  • Work closely with experts across design, modeling, layout, verification, product engineering, manufacturing, and system architecture.
  • Lead adoption of advanced engineering methodologies, automation frameworks, and AI-enabled design workflows to accelerate development cycles and improve design quality.
  • Help drive Micron's AI transformation through the practical application of Generative AI, Agentic AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), and data-driven engineering solutions.

Responsibilities

  • Define and drive strategic technical direction for memory circuit design activities across multiple projects and technology nodes.
  • Build, lead, mentor, and develop a high-performing engineering team capable of delivering world-class memory products.
  • Own execution, schedule, quality, and risk management for critical design deliverables.
  • Partner with architecture, verification, layout, product engineering, characterization, and manufacturing organizations to achieve program objectives.
  • Drive design reviews and technical decision-making for complex circuit, timing, power, reliability, and performance challenges.
  • Establish best-known methods, design methodologies, and automation strategies across the organization.
  • Identify technical risks early and develop mitigation plans to ensure successful product execution.
  • Enable design productivity through improved simulation methodologies, automation infrastructure, and AI-assisted engineering workflows.
  • Coach and develop future technical leaders and managers within the organization.
  • Represent the design organization in executive reviews and provide recommendations on key technical and business decisions.
  • Foster a culture of innovation, accountability, technical excellence, inclusion, and continuous learning.

Core Requirements

  • Proven experience leading circuit design teams and successfully delivering complex semiconductor products.
  • Strong understanding of CMOS circuit design fundamentals and advanced memory architectures.
  • Demonstrated expertise in DRAM, LPDDR, DDR, SRAM, or related memory technologies.
  • Strong knowledge of transistor-level circuit design, timing analysis, power optimization, and reliability methodologies.
  • Experience driving cross-functional teams across multiple geographic locations.
  • Excellent communication, collaboration, mentoring, and organizational leadership skills.
  • Strong problem-solving and analytical capabilities for complex technical challenges.
  • Experience in project planning, resource management, and technical risk mitigation.
  • Proficiency with simulation and design tools such as SPICE, Verilog/SystemVerilog, Cadence, PrimeSim, Spectre, or equivalent EDA platforms.
  • Demonstrated ability to leverage AI-enabled engineering tools, Generative AI, Agentic AI workflows, Large Language Models (LLMs), and automation solutions to improve engineering productivity and decision-making.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Recognized technical expertise in one or more memory design domains such as: Datapath DesignSense Amplifier DesignArray DesignRedundancy and RepairLow Power DesignIO and Interface DesignReliability EngineeringHigh-Speed Design
  • Experience with LPDDR5, LPDDR6, DDR5, HBM, GDDR, or emerging memory technologies.
  • Experience leading large-scale design programs from concept through silicon validation and volume production.
  • Experience developing automation frameworks using Python, Perl, Tcl, machine learning, or AI-based solutions.
  • Experience building globally distributed engineering teams.
  • Track record of driving innovation, patents, publications, or significant technical breakthroughs.
  • Prior experience managing managers or leading multiple engineering teams.

Education

  • Bachelor's Degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Computer Engineering, or related field required.
  • Master's Degree or PhD preferred.
  • Typically 12+ years of semiconductor design experience with significant technical leadership and people-management responsibilities.
