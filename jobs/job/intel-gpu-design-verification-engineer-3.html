The Role and Impact
As a GPU Design Verification Engineer at Intel, you will be at the forefront of verifying cutting-edge graphics logic components used in industry-leading integrated, discrete, and data center AI products. You will design and execute scalable verification plans, develop reusable test benches, and ensure the robustness and reliability of our innovative graphics IP. By collaborating closely with architects, RTL developers, and physical design teams, your contributions will drive Intel's leadership in high-performance computing and deliver impactful solutions used worldwide.

Business Group
The Data Center Group (DCG) is responsible for driving innovation in high-performance computing technologies, including the development of next-generation Graphics IP. Our team's focus is on delivering industry-leading GPU hardware intellectual property (IP) blocks that enable groundbreaking solutions across integrated, discrete, and data center/AI applications. We work collaboratively across Intel's broader organization to ensure technical excellence and continuous innovation, supporting Intel's mission to engineer world-class technology.

Key Responsibilities
- Develop and execute scalable IP verification plans, test benches, and methodologies to meet graphics microarchitecture specifications.
- Perform debugging, issue replication, root cause analysis, and corrective measures in pre-silicon environments.
- Conduct system simulations and analyze power, timing, and functional coverage to uncover and resolve design issues.
- Independently create test environments, verify features, and ensure comprehensive coverage closure.
- Collaborate with architects, design, and physical engineering teams to optimize functionality, performance, and power goals.
- Utilize assertion-based verification techniques and industry-standard tools to improve verification reliability.
- Document test plans and lead technical reviews to ensure alignment with architecture goals.
- Maintain and improve functional verification infrastructure and methodologies for continuous enhancement.
