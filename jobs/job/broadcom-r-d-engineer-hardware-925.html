Job Description:

Education degree should be in EE (Electrical Engineering) or equivalent. (MSC advantage).

AT least 8 years of relevant experience.

Solid knowledge in SI/PI.

Solid board level debugging skills.

Experienced in high speed digital design.

Hands-on familiarity with digital interfaces such as DDR, pcie, and SerDes.

Know their way around the lab - competent using spectrum analyzers, oscilloscopes.

Candidate must be a self-starter and must be able to work independently and in a team environment.

Excellent verbal and written communication skills in English.

The position requires a good understanding of board design and tools, including high speed digital design, analog circuit, filters, regulators and controlled impedance implementations.
