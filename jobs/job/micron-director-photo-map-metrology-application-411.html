About Micron

Our vision is Accelerate intelligence to enrich life for all. Micron Technology is a global leader in innovative memory and storage solutions. Through relentless innovation, we transform information into intelligence, empowering the world to learn, communicate, and advance faster than ever.

As the Director of Photo MAP (Metrology, Application, Process), you will lead a global organization responsible for delivering world-class photolithography and metrology performance across Micron's manufacturing network. Your team will drive innovation, standardization, and transformation in lithography productivity, overlay control, and manufacturing excellence to enable both current and future technology nodes in high-volume manufacturing.

In this role, you will develop and execute strategies that optimize manufacturing performance, accelerate capability deployment, and ensure alignment with Micron's business priorities and long-term strategic objectives across all manufacturing sites.

Key Responsibilities

Build and Develop a High-Performing Global Organization

  • Establish, lead, and develop a high-performing global team through coaching, mentoring, succession planning, and talent development.
  • Foster a culture of collaboration, accountability, innovation, and continuous improvement across global stakeholders.

Drive Strategic Business Performance

  • Define and execute global strategies and technology roadmaps that deliver industry-leading performance, productivity, quality, and cost competitiveness.
  • Align organizational priorities with Micron's business objectives and manufacturing strategy.

Lead Worldwide Photolithography and Metrology Excellence

  • Drive global initiatives to improve equipment, process, and application performance across the manufacturing network.
  • Identify and implement breakthrough opportunities that enhance productivity, overlay control, and operational efficiency.

Adoption of AI

  • Champion the adoption of AI, machine learning, advanced analytics, and automation solutions to improve operational efficiency, decision-making, process control, defect reduction, and manufacturing productivity.
  • Drive digital transformation initiatives that leverage data intelligence to accelerate technology development, optimize manufacturing performance, and enable scalable growth across Micron's global factory network.
  • Foster a culture of innovation, continuous learning, and data-driven problem solving to support Micron's leadership in the AI era.

Partner Across the Ecosystem

  • Collaborate closely with manufacturing sites, Technology Development (TD), Process Integration Engineering (PIE), and partner organizations to accelerate technology deployment and manufacturing readiness.
  • Build strong relationships with internal stakeholders and external suppliers to drive innovation and achieve exceptional customer outcomes.

Champion Customer-Driven Transformation

  • Translate Voice of Customer (VOC) insights into meaningful improvements in business processes, standards, systems, and manufacturing capabilities.
  • Lead organizational change and transformation initiatives that enhance execution efficiency and customer satisfaction.

Enable Global Growth and Expansion

  • Support capacity expansions and greenfield projects by ensuring lithography and metrology strategies are scalable, standardized, and future-ready.
  • Drive best-practice deployment across Micron's worldwide factory network.

Qualifications & Experience

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in engineering, Physics, Materials Science, or a related technical field.
  • Minimum of 8 years of leadership experience, including leading managers and global teams.
  • Strong understanding of Front-End semiconductor manufacturing and fab process integration.
  • Using Artificial Intelligence
  • Demonstrated experience leading large-scale strategic and transformational initiatives.
  • Proven ability to manage organizational resources, develop talent, and build high-performing teams.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills with the ability to influence at all levels of the organization.
  • Strong project and program management capabilities with a customer-focused mindset.

Preferred Attributes

  • Deep understanding of Micron's global manufacturing network and operational ecosystem.
  • Strategic thinker with a track record of driving business impact through innovation and operational excellence.
  • Passionate learners, coach, and mentor committed to developing future leaders.
  • Ability to navigate complex technical and business challenges in a highly dynamic environment.
