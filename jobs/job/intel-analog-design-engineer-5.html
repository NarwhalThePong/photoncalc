Analog Design Engineer

Job Description: 

The Role and Impact As an Analog Circuit Design Engineer, you will play a critical role in designing, developing, and optimizing cutting-edge analog and mixed-signal circuits in advanced process nodes. Your expertise will contribute directly to Intel's mission of driving innovation and delivering high-performance, power-efficient, and scalable IP solutions. Joining our team, you'll engage in solving complex technical challenges, collaborate cross-functionally, and influence Intel's success in the semiconductor industry. This role offers you the opportunity to work on groundbreaking technologies that shape the future of computing and communication systems. Key Responsibilities - Design, develop, and optimize analog circuits, including analog behavior models, GPIO, PLLs, LDOs and ADCs for industry-leading IPs. - Perform floor planning, circuit design, and parameter extraction, ensuring robust functionality and adherence to microarchitecture specifications. - Simulate and verify designs to optimize circuit performance, power, area, timing, and yield goals. - Create and execute detailed test plans to verify circuit functionality and evaluate results based on performance metrics. - Collaborate with architecture and layout teams to design circuits that maximize robustness, electrical capabilities, and functionality. - Analyze, track, and resolve performance issues, ensuring designs meet power and electrical specifications. - Report design progress and contribute to cross-functional collaboration for timely delivery of high-quality solutions. - Work towards reducing leakage and optimizing power, performance, and area of circuits across multiple projects.

Qualifications:

Minimum Qualifications - Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field; or equivalent experience per business needs. - 12+ years of experience in analog circuit design with a Bachelor's degree, or 12+ years with a Master's degree. - Proficiency in CMOS technologies, circuit simulation, circuit testing, and analog fundamentals. - Expertise in analog behavior modeling, analog power circuits, and analog electronics. - Technical knowledge of LDO, clocking circuits and analog to digital converters. Preferred Qualifications - Strong problem-solving abilities and attention to detail in optimizing performance, power, and area metrics. - Excellent communication and collaboration skills to work effectively in cross-functional teams. - Demonstrated ability to handle complex circuit design challenges in advanced process nodes. - Passion for innovation and commitment to delivering high-quality designs that align with Intel's core values and goals. Take this opportunity to be part of Intel's world-class engineering team. Apply today and help shape the future of technology.
