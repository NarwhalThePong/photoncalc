Ciena's WaveLogic portfolio powers optical fiber transmission solutions and contributes significantly to innovation in telecommunications. This opportunity enables leadership in the physical implementation of advanced high-speed analog circuits using BiCMOS technologies. The role focuses on delivering high-performance analog layout that supports next-generation broadband fiber-optic modem solutions.


How you will make an impact:

  • Lead block and macro-level layout development, including floor planning, layout creation, and optimization.
  • Evaluate layout feasibility across multiple circuit topologies and collaborate with analog designers to determine implementation trade-offs.
  • Execute physical verification and electromigration checks.
  • Contribute to team success through collaboration, knowledge sharing, and participation in team activities.
  • Mentor junior layout engineers involved in layout implementation activities.

The must haves:

  • Education: a minimum of Bachelor of Engineering, Science, Computer Science, or other applicable scientific discipline.
  • Experience: Experience in a leading analog high-speed layout role in advanced BiCMOS and/or CMOS technologies with industrial experience.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively within a team environment.
  • Ability to address complex technical problems using a methodical approach.
  • Excellent written and verbal English communication skills.
  • Utilization of analog design tools from Cadence, Mentor, and Synopsys, including Virtuoso, Calibre PERC, PEX, etc.
  • History of successful analog circuit product deliveries.

The Nice to haves:

  • Experience in deep-submicron CMOS
  • Development of automation scripts and Pcells to improve layout design flows.
  • Experience with digital synthesis and place and route
