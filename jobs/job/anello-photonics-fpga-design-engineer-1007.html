Title:  FPGA Design Engineer                                                             Reports to: VP of Navigation

ABOUT ANELLO PHOTONICS:

ANELLO Photonics http://www.anellophotonics.com is a leading-edge technology company based in Santa Clara, CA. The company has developed integrated photonic system-on-chip and AI technology for next generation navigation.  At the core is ANELLO's SIPHOG™ gyroscope, based on its patented photonic integrated circuit technology. The result is a product that is higher performance, much smaller size and weight and power, at a fraction of the cost of traditional gyros used in industrial, aerospace and military applications (SWAP-C). Our products enable reliable and accurate navigation and positioning for manned and autonomous applications, including in GPS-Denied environments. Anello is currently shipping product to a growing list of customers in the Construction, Farming, Trucking, Unmanned Aerial Vehicles, Autonomous Vehicles, and National Security segments.  

POSITION SUMMARY:

ANELLO is seeking an experienced FPGA Design Engineer to work on a small team FPGA-based hardware development for advanced electronic products and embedded systems. This is a greenfield project working on our next generation product. This role requires strong expertise in FPGA architecture, RTL design, hardware validation, and implementation skills.

The ideal candidate has a strong background to support FPGA and hardware designs from concept through production, working closely with electrical, firmware, software, manufacturing, and test teams to deliver reliable, manufacturable products.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

FPGA Design & Development

  • Design, implement, simulate, and debug FPGA solutions using Verilog, SystemVerilog, or VHDL.
  • Develop high-performance digital architectures including data acquisition, signal processing, communication interfaces, and embedded control systems.
  • Integrate and optimize vendor and third-party IP, perform timing closure, resource utilization, and power optimization.
  • Support FPGA bring-up, hardware debugging, and system integration using standard lab instrumentation.

Verification & Validation

  • Develop simulation environments and verification methodologies for FPGA validation.
  • Perform functional verification, hardware testing, troubleshooting, and root-cause analysis.
  • Create automated test procedures, validation plans, and supporting documentation.

Cross-Functional Collaboration

  • Work closely with fw, sw, systems, mechanical, mfg, and test engi teams throughout the PLC.
  • Develop technical documentation incl requirement/specs, test procedures, and design reports.
  • Participate in design reviews, risk assessments, field issue investigations, and continuous product improvement efforts.

QUALIFICATIONS:

  • BSEE or MSEE, CE or related field;
  • 3+ yrs of FPGA and embedded hardware development. Simulation, verification, HW debugging
  • Proficiency in Verilog, SystemVerilog, or VHDL.
  • Experience w/FPGA platforms and toolchains ie. AMD/Xilinx, Intel/Altera, Lattice, or Microchip
  • Experience with FPGA simulation, verification, and hardware debugging.
  • Solid understanding of digital electronics, high-speed interfaces, embedded systems, and electronic product development.
  • Experience supporting products from prototype through production. 

Nice to have’s

  • Experience with DDR memory, PCIe, Ethernet, USB, SPI, I2C, UART, CAN, DSP, or real-time signal processing applications.
  • Experience with embedded processors and SoC FPGA platforms.

Additional Attributes

  • Hands-on engineer with strong FPGA, hardware, and system-level problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent troubleshooting and root-cause analysis capabilities.
  • Ability to balance technical execution with product goals, schedules, and business priorities.
  • Strong communication, collaboration, ownership, and accountability.

OTHER:   Applicants must be authorized to work in the United States without sponsorship. (U.S. citizenship or Perm Residence)
