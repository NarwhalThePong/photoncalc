We are seeking a Layout Design Engineering Led to join Micron's organization in Guadalajara, Mexico. In this leadership role, you will guide a distributed team focused on developing complex analog, mixed‑signal, and custom layouts powering next‑generation AI and high‑performance computing solutions.

Responsibilities

  • Lead a team designing and developing critical analog, mixed‑signal, and custom block layouts, including support for full‑chip-level integration.
  • Oversee layout verification activities, including LVS, DRC, Antenna checks, documentation, and quality reviews.
  • Ensure timely and high‑quality delivery of block‑level layouts that meet all project requirements.
  • Coordinate planning, area/time estimation, scheduling, task delegation, and execution for multiple concurrent projects.
  • Mentor and guide layout engineers of all levels.
  • Perform detailed reviews of sub‑block‑level layouts.
  • Collaborate with global engineering teams to drive successful project execution and alignment across Micron’s worldwide footprint.

Minimum Qualifications

  • 10+ years of experience in analog/custom layout design in advanced CMOS technology nodes (Planar, FinFET).
  • 5+ years of experience managing layout engineering teams.
  • Proven ability to lead layout development processes with strong quality oversight.
  • Bachelor’s degree, Master’s degree or equivalent experience in Electronics, VLSI Engineering, or related field.
  • Expertise with Cadence VLE/VXL and Mentor Graphics Calibre DRC/LVS.
  • Hands‑on experience with critical IP blocks (PLL, ADC, DAC, bandgap, temperature sensors, charge pumps, current mirrors, comparators, differential amplifiers, etc.).
  • Strong fundamentals in matching, electromigration, latch‑up, coupling, crosstalk, IR drop, and parasitic effects.
  • Ability to analyze layout impact on speed, area, power, and overall circuit performance.
  • Experience supporting multiple tapeouts

  • Applying Artificial Intelligence

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience working with memories (HBM is a plus), or high‑performance computing products.
  • Strong understanding of advanced physical design methodologies and emerging process technologies.
  • Familiarity with scripting languages (Python, SKILL, Tcl) to automate layout tasks and improve design efficiency.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder‑management skills in fast‑paced, multi‑project environments.
  • Strong interpersonal skills to help building team culture.
