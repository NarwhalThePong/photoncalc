Job Description:

Broadcom’s Wireless Semiconductor Division (WSD) is seeking highly motivated Equipment Maintenance Technicians to join our team along the Front Range in Fort Collins, CO. In this role, you will perform installation, troubleshooting, preventive maintenance, and continuous optimization of complex wafer fabrication equipment in a cleanroom environment. Working across Metrology platforms, you will play a central role in driving equipment reliability, maximizing tool availability, and maintaining high yield across existing and next-generation processes.

Key Responsibilities

Equipment Maintenance & Operations

  • Install, repair, modify, and maintain complex semiconductor manufacturing equipment within a cleanroom environment.

  • Execute routine Preventive Maintenance (PM) tasks and corrective repairs .

  • Conduct projects to optimize performance on established and emerging toolsets.

  • Ensure accurate, timely logging of all daily maintenance activities in Computerized Maintenance Management Systems.

Troubleshooting & Technical Analytics

  • Apply systematic troubleshooting strategies to diagnose complex electro-mechanical, optical, and vacuum sub-system failures, often working with incomplete data.

  • Leverage data-driven tools (SPC, CMMS, Spotfire, JMP) to analyze equipment performance history, drive root-cause solutions, and improve key performance indicators.

  • Assist in writing and refining Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and maintenance documentation for new platforms.

Adaptability & Continuous Improvement

  • Take ownership to resolve equipment issues in assigned areas.

  • Multitask effectively under pressure to address competing high-priority tool calls.

  • Expand skill sets independently through self-directed learning, technical documentation, and cross-training on unfamiliar tool platforms.

Teamwork & Communication

  • Collaborate cross-functionally with Equipment Engineering, Process Engineering, Production, Fab IT, and Facilities to achieve site yield, quality, cycle-time, and uptime targets.

  • Conduct thorough shift handoffs and pass-downs to ensure seamless communication across shift cycles.

  • Act as a mentor to junior technicians while remaining receptive to technical guidance from senior colleagues and engineers.

Qualifications

Required:

  • Education: Associate of Science (A.S.) degree in Electronics Technology, equivalent military technical training, or an approved Journeyman Apprenticeship program.

  • Experience: 0–2+ years of hands-on maintenance, repair, and troubleshooting experience on semiconductor manufacturing equipment or complex industrial systems.

  • Core Technical Knowledge: Strong foundational understanding of electrical, mechanical, hydraulic, pneumatic, and vacuum technologies and diagnostic tools.

  • Flexibility: Willingness to work compressed 12.25-hour night shifts.

  • Work Authorization: Must be legally authorized to work in the United States without sponsorship.
