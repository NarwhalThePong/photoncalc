  • Own and drive custom analog and mixed-signal layout for critical LPDDR PHY blocks, including Datapath, bias networks, IO front ends, and reference circuits, ensuring robust operation across PVT, low noise coupling, and high matching accuracy
  • 3-12 years of solid hands-on experience in custom analog and high-speed layout design, with proven expertise in implementing and delivering critical analog blocks and LPDDR/high-speed interfaces at advanced technology nodes
  • Lead high-speed LPDDR (LPDDRx) interface layout execution, solving complex signal integrity challenges such as skew control, impedance matching, shielding, crosstalk mitigation, and jitter sensitivity in advanced nodes
  • Architect and implement scalable array-based layout strategies for DQ/DQS byte lanes, CA lanes, and replica paths, ensuring strict symmetry, pitch matching, repeatability, and multi-channel scalability
  • Drive integration of custom digital and mixed-signal blocks within the PHY (training logic, calibration engines, test/debug circuitry), ensuring optimal clocking, congestion management, and isolation from sensitive analog regions
  • Lead layout-aware co-design with circuit teams, enabling early closure of timing, noise, IR, EM, and parasitic effects, and influencing design decisions from a layout perspective
  • Establish robust power distribution, grounding, and isolation strategies, including guard rings, deep N-well usage, decoupling, and domain separation for high-speed and analog-sensitive regions
  • Own physical verification and signoff closure:
    • DRC/LVS, parasitic extraction, and simulation correlation
    • Ensure silicon-accurate performance for analog-intensive and high-speed LPDDR blocks
  • Drive post-layout optimization and ECOs, resolving margin, yield, and manufacturability challenges while ensuring JEDEC compliance
  • Collaborate cross-functionally with architecture, circuit, verification, and silicon debug teams to enable first-pass silicon success and high-volume manufacturability
  • Own and drive PPA optimization, delivering measurable improvements in power efficiency, area utilization, and performance
