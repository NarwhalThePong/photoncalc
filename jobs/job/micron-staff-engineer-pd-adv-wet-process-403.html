Main Responsibilities

Process Development & Margin Improvement

  • Quantify benefits of innovative process and/or equipment solutions to enable early experimentation and adoption for process margin improvement.
  • Initiate and manage experiments to widen process margins and validate manufacturability of next‑node solutions.

Cross ‑ functional Integration & Troubleshooting

  • Integrate and troubleshoot processes as part of cross‑functional teams driving yield and quality improvement.
  • Participate with Boise TD peers on technology choices for upcoming nodes and translate future device needs into clear process requirements.
  • Identify disruptive processes early and work with manufacturing and TD partners to identify viable paths forward.

Innovation / IP / Supplier Engagement

  • Support process development activities including fundamental research, hardware evaluations, and testing for novel applications; contribute to IP generation where applicable.
  • Participate in supplier engagement workstreams supporting breakthrough solutions.

Monitoring, Control & Documentation (Governance)

  • Identify process simplification opportunities and support cross‑functional implementation.
  • Incorporate best‑known manufacturing methods into early development of upcoming nodes.

  • Communicate and collaborate locally and globally; strict adherence to Micron IP protection policy is required.
