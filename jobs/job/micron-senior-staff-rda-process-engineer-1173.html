Senior / Staff RDA Process Engineer

Micron · Hyderabad - Phoenix Aquila, India · posted 2026-09-27

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

As an Senior / Staff RDA Process Engineer, you will play an important role in monitoring inline defectivity, identifying process anomalies, and driving defect reduction initiatives. You will learn to leverage advanced inspection and metrology tools, analyze large data sets, and collaborate with cross-functional teams to improve manufacturing outcomes. This role offers the opportunity to influence high-volume manufacturing processes, transfer new technologies into production, and contribute to yield and quality improvements across global operations.



Responsibilities:

  • Analyze process flows, defect data, and inline signals to identify anomalies, drive corrective actions, and improve defect performance
  • Support experimentation, baseline health monitoring, and defect reduction initiatives 
  • Utilize inspection and metrology tools to detect process issues early and identify sources of variation
  • Partner with global manufacturing, process integration, process development, and yield teams to develop and deploy best-known methods
  • Drive continuous improvement through data analysis, root cause investigations, and process optimization


Minimum Qualifications:


  • Masters’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Materials Science and Engineering, or a related technical field
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills
  • Ability to investigate process anomalies, perform root cause analysis, and interpret complex data sets
  • Demonstrated willingness to learn new technologies, tools, and manufacturing processes
  • 3+ years of experience working within Semiconductor Manufacturing


Preferred Qualifications:


  • Master’s degree or PhD in Electrical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Materials Science and Engineering, or a related field
  • Experience with data analysis, scripting, programming, or other data science methodologies
  • Experience with semiconductor inspection, metrology, or Realtime Defect Analysis (RDA) processes
  • Proven ability to innovate, drive change, and collaborate effectively in a fast-paced manufacturing environment
  • Experience using AI-enabled tools, machine learning, automation platforms, or data analytics to improve engineering decisions and productivity
