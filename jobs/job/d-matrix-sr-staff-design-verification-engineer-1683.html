Role Overview

d-Matrix is building a company and culture designed to last — one grounded in transparency, inclusiveness, and intellectual honesty, where continuous learning is part of the job. We've built the industry's first highly programmable in-memory computing architecture, applicable across a broad range of applications from cloud to edge. In this role, you'll work on a groundbreaking architecture alongside a highly experienced team that has a proven track record of building successful businesses. This is a unique opportunity to help shape a company at an early stage and grow into a future leader in an industry poised for global impact.

What You Will Do

  • Develop and execute verification plans across the SoC/IP verification cycle, from architecture definition through tape-out and silicon bring-up

  • Build and maintain UVM/OVM-based verification environments for complex ASIC/SoC designs

  • Write and debug SystemVerilog testbenches, leveraging randomization, functional coverage, and assertion-based methodologies

  • Drive root-cause analysis and debug of verification failures in close collaboration with design and architecture teams

  • Define and track coverage closure goals to ensure comprehensive verification sign-off

  • Partner cross-functionally with design, architecture, and physical design teams to align verification strategy with tape-out schedules

  • Contribute to the evolution of verification methodology and infrastructure to support next-generation in-memory computing architectures

What You Will Bring

  • BS in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field with 12+ years of industry experience (MS preferred with 7+ years)

  • Experience across the full SoC/IP verification cycle — from architecture through tape-out and bring-up

  • Hands-on ASIC/SoC design verification test development and debug experience

  • Fluency with SystemVerilog (randomization, constraints, coverage, assertions) and verification methodologies such as UVM/OVM/VMM

  • Strong problem-solving skills and a passion for tackling complex technical challenges, particularly in the AI domain

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with C/C++ and SystemC

  • Track record leading the creation and implementation of multiple SoC verification environments and driving tape-out efforts
