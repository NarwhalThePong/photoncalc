Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

Micron’s DRAM Design Engineering Group (DDEG) – Technology is a global organization dedicated to advancing memory and storage innovation. The team collaborates across fields to solve complex semiconductor challenges and deliver world-class memory solutions through creativity, continuous learning, and engineering excellence.


The Design Engineer is responsible for designing and analyzing digital and analog circuits for Micron memory products. This role supports silicon-to-systems development, working closely with global design, verification, and manufacturing partners to ensure products meet cost, quality, reliability, and time-to-market objectives throughout the product lifecycle.


Responsibilities

  • Design, analyze, and optimize digital, analog, memory, and logic circuits, including schematic development, layout, and verification
  • Perform semiconductor design engineering activities such as chip layout design, circuit checking, parasitic modeling, and documentation of specifications
  • Support design validation, reticle experiments, tape-out revisions, and evaluation of semiconductor devices and components
  • Lead layout efforts including floorplanning, placement, and routing, and modify designs based on product requirements and logic diagrams
  • Collaborate with Product Engineering, Test, Probe, Process Integration, Assembly, Marketing, and verification teams to ensure manufacturable and high-quality designs, including leveraging AI Assisted tools to enhance design analysis and debug efficiency

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering
  • Experience in developing and evaluating digital and analog circuits for memory products
  • Ability to model and simulate integrated circuits using SPICE and Verilog
  • Working knowledge of Synopsys tools and Cadence Virtuoso
  • Experience reviewing product specifications and logic flowcharts to define and implement semiconductor devices
  • Experience working in AI-Enabled environments or willingness to leverage AI tools to support engineering productivity, automation, and data-driven workflows

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in the design and development of volatile or nonvolatile memory technologies
  • Knowledge of advanced memory technologies such as ferroelectric memory, wafer bonding, and advanced packaging
  • Familiarity with small-signal sensing techniques
  • Understanding of high-voltage reliability considerations in semiconductor devices
  • Exposure to Applying AI or AI Assisted techniques, including data-driven automation, to improve design or verification efficiency
