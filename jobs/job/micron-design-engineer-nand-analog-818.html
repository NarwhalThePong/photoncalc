Design/Senior Design Engineer – NAND Analog (NVE Engineering)


Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

department Introduction

The Non-Volatile NAND Design Engineering group is responsible for the design and optimization of analog, digital and mixed-signal circuits enabling advanced NAND Flash memory products. Close collaboration with multi-functional teams ensures scalable, manufacturable, and reliable solutions across technology nodes.

Position Overview

As a Design Engineer in the NVE Design Engineering Analog group at Micron Semiconductor Italia, you will contribute to the development of memory products that are best-in-class, using your specialized knowledge and Micron proprietary methods of designing and analyzing core and mixed circuits used in the development of memory products.

Responsibilities

Responsibilities include, but not limited to:

• Design, implement, and validate analog and mixed-signal circuits (e.g., charge pumps, regulators, amplifiers, ADC/DAC, bandgap, power management blocks) across the full development lifecycle.

• Translate system requirements into circuit architectures, specifications, and transistor-level implementations optimized for performance, power, area, noise, and reliability.

• Perform comprehensive circuit simulation and verification (DC, AC, transient, noise, Monte Carlo, PVT corners) including parasitic-aware validation.

• Collaborate with multi-functional teams to optimize product performance, power consumption, die size, and manufacturability.

• Develop user documentation and contribute to design reviews, silicon validation, and root-cause analysis activities.

• Layout automation

AI Enablement and Productivity

The candidate is expected to use AI-powered tools and data-driven methodologies to improve engineering productivity, accelerate problem solving, support design analysis, and enhance technical decision making. Responsibilities may include:

• Using AI-assisted engineering tools for code generation, review, debug, and documentation.

• Applying AI-enabled analysis techniques to large design, verification, characterization, or silicon datasets.

• Finding opportunities to automate repetitive engineering tasks through AI technologies.

• Evaluating and adopting emerging AI capabilities to improve efficiency, quality, and innovation within the design process.

• Collaborating with multi-functional teams to integrate AI automated workflows into engineering activities.

Min Qualifications

• M.S. or Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering, Electronics, or a related field.

• Senior role: Proven experience in analog and mixed-signal CMOS circuit design.

• Solid understanding of device physics, variability, noise, reliability, and power integrity.

• Hands-on experience with SPICE-based simulation tools and verification methodologies.

• Strong problem-solving and communication skills, with the ability to work in global multi-functional teams.

Preferred Qualifications

• Experience in NAND Flash or other non-volatile memory technologies.

• Familiarity with design automation, scripting (e.g., Python), or AI-assisted design approaches.

• Understanding of layout constraints and interaction with physical design flows.
