The AMHS Technician is responsible for ensuring the reliable operation, maintenance, and continuous improvement of Automated Material Handling System equipment. This role involves monitoring system performance, performing preventive and corrective maintenance, and troubleshooting mechanical, electrical, and software-related issues to minimize downtime and sustain high equipment reliability. The technician supports equipment installations, upgrades, and calibrations while maintaining accurate documentation and adhering to established procedures. Success in this role requires strong mechanical and electrical aptitude, effective problem‑solving abilities, and the capability to interpret schematics and resolve issues down to the component level. The AMHS Technician must be able to work independently in a fast‑paced environment, collaborate effectively with engineers, operations teams, and vendors, and demonstrate strong initiative, communication skills, and flexibility, including working 12‑hour shifts as required.


Job Description

Responsibilities:

  • Monitor the performance and health of assigned AMHS equipment and identify abnormalities early.
  • Schedule and carry out preventive maintenance to keep systems operating reliably.
  • Troubleshoot and repair mechanical, electrical, and software-related equipment issues.
  • Apply structured problem‑solving to identify root causes and implement long‑term corrective actions.
  • Perform quality workmanship during repairs and verify functionality through post‑maintenance checks.
  • Support equipment installations, relocations, calibrations, and system upgrades.
  • Collaborate with equipment engineers, Shift Operations, and tool vendors to maintain and improve system uptime.
  • Create, review, and update maintenance procedures, work instructions, and checklists.
  • Maintain accurate maintenance logs, service records, and performance documentation.
  • Drive continuous improvement through data analysis, lessons learned, and proactive maintenance enhancements.
  • Contribute to improving uptime, reliability, and workflow efficiency across AMHS operations.

Qualifications:

  • Nitec/Higher Nitec or Diploma in Engineering or relevant working experience
