Job Description

 

  • Design and implement new production processes
  • Analysis and optimize existing manufacturing processes to enhance productivity and reduce costs
  • Conduct process engineering studies and evaluate process data to identify areas for improvement
  • Develop and maintain standard operating procedures (SOPs) for manufacturing processes
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to resolve technical issues and implement process improvements
  • Ensure compliance with quality standards, safety regulations, and environmental guidelines
  • Participate in continuous improvement initiatives and lean manufacturing projects
  • Troubleshoot process-related problems and implement effective solutions
  • Provide technical support and training to production staff on process-related matters
  • Stay updated on industry trends and emerging technologies in process engineering

 

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree or above in Process Engineering, Materials Science & Engineering, Physics, Chemical Engineering, or a related field
  • Strong analytical skills with a detail-oriented approach to problem-solving
  • Understanding of lean manufacturing principles and continuous improvement techniques
  • Excellent communication and teamwork skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively across departments
  • Innovative mindset with a passion for optimizing processes and driving efficiency
  • Familiarity with data analysis and statistical process control techniques
  • Strong project management skills and ability to manage multiple priorities
  • Experience with Six Sigma methodologies is desirable
  • Willingness to work in a dynamic, fast-paced manufacturing environment
  • Excellent English writing, speaking, and presentation skills.
