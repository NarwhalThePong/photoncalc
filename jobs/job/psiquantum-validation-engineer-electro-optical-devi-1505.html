Job Summary:

The alidation Engineer, Electro-Optical devices will be responsible for implementing the methods and technics to validate the performance of various electro-optical subsystems, perform measures, collect data and provide reports. 

Responsibilities:

  • Define figures of merits and validation plans for the cryogenic electro-optical assemblies.
  • Design and build laboratory setups comprised of light sources, detector, switches; function generators, oscilloscopes, etc. to validate design functionality of the electro-optical assemblies in cryogenic environments.
  • Contribute to the development of software scripts to automatically drive the measurements setups and feed data into a database.
  • Perform the measures, provide detailed reports including expected yield indications.
  • Execute the validation plans, including debugging and characterization the cryogenic electro-optical.
  • Review and provide feedback to the electro-optical assembly’s design team.
  • Collaborate with the design and experimental teams across the company to learn from current best known methods.

Experience/Qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s Degree or above in Engineering, Physics or Material Science, and 5+ years of industry experience.
  • Experience with testing in cryogenic environments.
  • Experience working with optical and electrical test equipment such as optical power meters, lasers, optical spectrum analyzer (OSA), oscilloscopes, function generators (AWG), vector network analyzer (VNA).
  • Experience developing test methods and optimization, tuning/calibration algorithms.
  • Experience working with single photon detectors is preferred.
  • Experience working with optical fibers, photonic integrated circuits is preferred.
