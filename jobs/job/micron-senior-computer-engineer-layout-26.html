Senior Computer Engineer, Layout

Micron · Jalisco, Mexico · posted 2026-10-27

Apply on Micron's site →

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

At Micron, our EDA and Design Automation team sits at the center of memory technology development. We build, maintain, and optimize the tools, methodologies, and design flows that enable world-class CMOS design at scale. By partnering closely with design, layout, and process engineering teams, we solve complex technical challenges that directly contribute to product innovation and business success.

As a Computer Engineer, you will play a key role in supporting and advancing the design ecosystem used across Micron's engineering organization. You will develop, debug, and enhance both in-house and commercial EDA solutions while collaborating with multi-functional teams to improve engineering productivity, design quality, and workflow efficiency. This position is ideal for someone who enjoys hands-on technical problem solving, software development, and enabling engineers to perform at their best.

Join Micron Technology in Guadalajara. Chip in to innovative memory designs impacting HBM, DRAM, and new technologies.

Responsibilities:


  • Develop and enhance EDA tools, automated design flows, and methodologies that support CMOS design and verification.
  • Debug and evaluate in-house and commercial EDA solutions in Linux/Unix environments, providing reliable support for engineering users.
  • Collaborate with design, layout, and process engineering teams to solve workflow, automation, and tool-integration challenges.
  • Build clear technical documentation and learning resources that help engineering teams use design tools effectively.
  • Apply AI-assisted technologies, where appropriate, to drive efficiency, improve development workflows, and advance design automation.

Minimum Qualifications:


  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related technical field, or equivalent relevant industry experience.
  • Four or more years of relevant experience applying software development, CAD, VLSI, or semiconductor engineering skills to design or automation challenges.
  • Proficiency in programming and software development, with strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Experience using Linux/Unix environments, including shell scripting and workflow automation.
  • Effective written and verbal communication and interpersonal skills, with demonstrated ability to collaborate across engineering teams and learn new technologies.

Preferred Qualifications:


  • Experience developing or applying AI and agentic AI solutions to engineering or automation workflows.
  • Proficiency in Python and SKILL, particularly for engineering automation or design-tool development.
  • Knowledge of Very-Large-Scale Integration (VLSI), integrated circuit design, semiconductor design methodologies, or EDA tools.
  • Experience with schematic capture, physical layout, simulation, verification, or related semiconductor design flows.
  • Understanding of CMOS implementation tradeoffs, including area, performance, and power, with experience improving EDA flows or engineering infrastructure.
