Job Description

Location: WD – Fremont, CA.
Schedule: 12-hour AWS Shift (includes weekends and overtime as required)
Environment: Class 100 Cleanroom

Job Summary

We are seeking a skilled and motivated Equipment Maintenance Technician to support and maintain production tools in our wafer manufacturing line. The ideal candidate is a proactive problem-solver who thrives in a fast-paced cleanroom environment, demonstrates initiative, and works effectively both independently and as part of a team.

Key Responsibilities

  • Perform preventive and corrective maintenance on wafer fabrication tools using established procedures and on-the-job training (OJT).
  • Accurately document all maintenance activities in tool logbooks and CMMS (Computerized Maintenance Management System).
  • Troubleshoot and repair equipment to identify root causes and ensure long-term reliability.
  • Support Tool Owners and Equipment Engineers in improving tool performance and uptime.
  • Maintain comprehensive records, including PM checklists, part usage, and maintenance history.
  • Continuously develop technical skills and certifications through formal and informal training.
  • Follow all cleanroom and safety protocols while maintaining high standards of workmanship.

Qualifications

Required:

  • Strong electro-mechanical aptitude and hands-on maintenance skills.
  • Proficiency with MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook).
  • Excellent communication, documentation, and teamwork skills.
  • Reliable, punctual, and committed to high-quality work.
  • Ability to lift up to 35 lbs. and stand for extended periods (12-hour shifts).
  • Willingness to work overtime, weekends, and flexible schedules as needed.

Preferred:

  • Experience with semiconductor wafer fab equipment (Photo, Thin Films, Plating, Etch, CMP). (Bonus)
  • Ability to read and interpret electrical schematics and mechanical drawings. (Bonus)
  • Knowledge of control systems and PLCs (Programmable Logic Controllers). (Bonus)
  • Familiarity with failure analysis, root cause determination, and repair documentation.
  • Experience using DVMs (Digital Voltmeters) and standard mechanical hand tools.

Key Skills and Attributes

  • Entry-level experience in equipment maintenance or related field.
  • Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving abilities.
  • Understanding of electrical safety and operational theory.
  • Dependable, detail-oriented, and able to work with minimal supervision.
  • Collaborative mindset with a commitment to continuous improvement.

Salary Range: $69,200.00-92,200.00
