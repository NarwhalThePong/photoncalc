Your Team, Your Impact

The Soc Design team is responsible for SoC-level design activities and functionality in Marvell switching SoCs. The SoC level is the creation of the chip-level design. Synchronizing between the design, verification, and physical design teams. The team handles some of the biggest problem solvers in Silicon and has a huge impact on the work done at Marvell.

What You Can Expect

  • Be part of the Soc level team, working on projects in the switching project
  • Design and implement new features across RTL design.
  • Integrate various IP blocks into the SoC, following a thorough understanding of functionality, interfaces, and requirements.
  • Innovate and develop automation flows to optimize runtime, reduce manual effort, and enhance overall design efficiency.
  • Collaborate cross-functionally with verification, physical design, firmware, and software teams to enable smooth system-level integration.
  • Investigate and debug complex simulation and integration failures to identify root causes and drive resolutions.
  • Utilize scripting languages (e.g., Python, Perl, TCL) to automate tasks and streamline development and verification processes.
  • Participate in the design and verification of IP blocks and multidisciplinary systems involving hardware, firmware, and software

What We're Looking For

  • Student for a bachelor’s or master’s degree in computer science, electrical engineering, or related fields, 2nd year and above.
  • Overall top-down system view.
  • At least two years before graduation.
  • Good people relations and ability to work in a team.
  • Excellent problem-solving ability.
  • Well-organized documentation and research process.
  • Clear and concise communication and presentation skills.
  • Self-learning skills and highly motivated to achieve project goals.
