Job Details:

Job Description: 

The Role and Impact As a Module Development Engineer, you will lead the design, development, and optimization of cutting-edge manufacturing processes and equipment solutions that enable Intel's innovative device architectures. Your day-to-day responsibilities will include conducting feasibility studies, driving process integration, and collaborating with cross-functional teams to ensure process readiness and scalability for high-volume manufacturing. Your contributions will play a pivotal role in advancing Intel's technology roadmap while optimizing production efficiency and achieving superior product performance and quality. Business group Intel Corporation's Manufacturing and Operations organization is dedicated to delivering world-class manufacturing processes that support Intel's leadership in semiconductor innovation. The team focuses on developing advanced technologies and manufacturing solutions to meet evolving industry demands. Through collaboration across global teams, this group drives Intel's mission to create transformative technology that powers the future. Key Responsibilities - Lead the development and implementation of technically sophisticated manufacturing processes, including material selection, parameter optimization, equipment metrology, and system design. - Perform process integration and provide solutions to meet desired device specifications for high-volume manufacturing and future technologies. - Conduct research and pathfinding activities to enable the manufacture of innovative device architectures and ensure scalability for high-volume production. - Develop roadmaps and strategies for technology processes that align with Intel's future goals while maintaining cost sensitivity. - Collaborate with equipment and materials suppliers to enable process advancements and enhance production outputs. - Perform feasibility studies using theoretical simulations and practical engineering methods to evaluate process technology capabilities. - Recommend and implement modifications to manufacturing processes and equipment to optimize efficiency, reliability, and yield. - Stay updated on industry trends and technical advancements to drive innovation and maintain Intel's competitive edge.

Qualifications:

 

  • PhD in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Materials Science, Physics, or a related STEM field, with at least 5 years of semiconductor Dry Etch/Plasma Etch process development experience; OR
  • Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Materials Science, or a related STEM field, with at least 7 years of semiconductor Dry Etch/Plasma Etch process development experience; OR
  • Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Materials Science, or a related STEM field, with at least 10 years of semiconductor Dry Etch/Plasma Etch process development experience.
  • 2 years of experience applying Design of Experiments (DOE) and Statistical Process Control (SPC) methodologies in semiconductor process development and optimization.
  • 2+ years of experience leading cross-functional semiconductor process development projects, collaborating across multiple engineering disciplines (e.g., process engineering, integration, yield, and equipment engineering) and driving technical solutions to achieve project and manufacturing objectives.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with advanced Dry Etch/Plasma Etch technologies, including Through-Silicon Via (TSV) processes.
  • Experience working in semiconductor foundry or advanced technology development environments.
  • Experience with process automation systems and advanced process control methodologies.
  • Experience collaborating with semiconductor equipment suppliers on process optimization, development, and implementation.
  • Experience driving process improvements related to yield, defect reduction, process stability, and manufacturing performance.
  • Experience in driving technical work groups solving complex dry etch related problem applying MBPS (model-based problem solving)
  • Demonstrated technical leadership in complex semiconductor process development initiatives involving multiple engineering teams.

    Join a team at the forefront of semiconductor innovation, where your expertise will drive impactful advancements in manufacturing technology. Apply today to shape the future of Intel's technology development and contribute to industry-leading breakthroughs.
