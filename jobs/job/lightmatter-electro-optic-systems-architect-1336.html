We are hiring a Electro-Optic Systems Architect. The selected candidate will partner with external facing teams at Lightmatter and internal engineering teams to deliver groundbreaking products to the market. In this role, you will be responsible for ensuring that Passage’s electrical-optical-electrical links are performant and meet customer requirements. You will engage with our cross-disciplinary engineering teams to model and analyze solutions. You may also test and measure prototypes to validate the models. This role requires a solid understanding of leading-edge CMOS, advanced silicon photonics, high-speed (40+ GHz) electro-optic interfaces, and 3D integration. The role also requires creativity, analytical skills, and clear communication skills. You are encouraged to represent the company at technical conferences as an example of our technical leadership.

Join a tight-knit team where each individual’s contributions directly influence the success of the company and product. You'll have the opportunity to build a new kind of computer from the ground up and to solve groundbreaking challenges along the way. Work with people who love to build and who thrive in technically diverse environments where great ideas are prioritized.

Responsibilities

  • Be the architecture owner of the electro-optic performance of a Lightmatter’s Passage product.
  • Electro-optic system modeling using Cadence photonics-analog simulators (Verilog-A, Spectre, Virtuoso), VPI Photonics, and Keysight ADS
  • Design the end-to-end electrical-optical-electrical system-level link to meet the customer requirements.
  • Co-design the analog and photonic devices with the engineering design teams based on the end-to-end simulation results.
  • Validate the models by designing high-speed RF experiments, conducting proof-of-concept experiments, and analyzing existing data.
  • Author, review, and validate architectural specifications.
  • Collaborate with the product team to develop the technological and product roadmap in the context of the industry trends
  • Identify possible pitfalls or difficulties, as well as mitigation strategies, around the execution of the proposed architecture.

Qualifications

  • A Ph.D. degree in Electrical Engineering or similar discipline with at least 8 years of relevant experience, or Master’s degree with at least 12 years of relevant experience.
  • Minimum 3 years of experience in electronic-photonic-electronic systems simulation and hardware testing/measurement.
  • Proficient in Cadence photonics-analog simulators (Virtuoso, Spectre, Verilog-A), Ansys HFSS, Keysight ADS.
  • Highly proficient in coding simulations in Python or Matlab.
  • Highly proficient in RF/mm-wave analog circuit design as well as modeling optical communication links.
  • Ability to lead and collaborate in a cross-disciplinary team.
  • Expert on corner analysis and Monte Carlo analysis.
  • Demonstrated strong problem solving skills specifically pertaining to problems that do not have obvious solutions.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in product development or projects with industry.
  • Silicon photonic integrated circuit design and test experience.
  • Knowledge of signal processing and signal integrity in optical communications.
  • Knowledge of SERDES and mixed signal interfaces.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Willingness and ability to learn quickly. Self-starter with a “no task is too big or small” attitude.
  • Data-driven and analytical; translates numbers into actionable strategies.
  • Process-oriented and programmatic: builds structure through processes that can be relied upon across the organization.
