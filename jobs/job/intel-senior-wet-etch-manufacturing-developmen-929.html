Job Description: 

Shape the Future of Semiconductor Manufacturing at Intel

Join Intel's newest and most innovative organization—Manufacturing Development Customer Engineering (MDCE)—where you'll bridge the critical gap between breakthrough technology development and high-volume manufacturing. As a Senior Wet Etch Manufacturing Development Engineer, you'll be at the forefront of advancing next-generation semiconductor technologies that power the world's most sophisticated devices.

Organization Impact

MDCE represents Intel's strategic evolution within Intel Foundry Technology Manufacturing (FTM). We're the vital link that transforms cutting-edge research into manufacturable reality, advancing technology nodes from initial qualification to high-yield production while delivering enhanced solutions for foundry customers worldwide.

Your Role in Technology Leadership

Drive technology innovation that defines Intel's future roadmap while enabling advanced manufacturing processes that set industry standards. You'll develop next-generation device architectures, support high-volume production scaling, and collaborate with world-class suppliers to deliver breakthrough solutions that maintain Intel's semiconductor leadership.

Direct Impact

Your innovations will power billions of devices globally, ensuring Intel's continued dominance in delivering world-class silicon solutions that push the boundaries of what's technologically possible.

Qualifications:

The Minimum qualifications are required to be initially considered for this position. Minimum qualifications listed below would be obtained through a combination of industry relevant job experience, internship experience and / or schoolwork/classes/research. Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Chemical Engineering, Chemistry, Electrical Engineering, Materials Science, Physics, Mechanical Engineering, or in a STEM related field
  • 7+ years of semiconductor manufacturing, process development, or technology development experience
  • 1+ years of experience in Design of Experiments (DOE) and statistical process control
  • 2+ years of experience in root-cause analysis capabilities using structured problem-solving and data analytics.
  • 2+ years of experience with module integration and interactions across semiconductor processes including; etch, deposition, CMP, or lithography.
  • Experience in process optimization, defect reduction, and manufacturing improvements
  • Hands-on experience with advanced manufacturing processes (e.g. etching, material science, and/or equipment metrology)

Preferred Qualifications

  • Post Graduate degree in Chemical Engineering, Chemistry, Electrical Engineering, Materials Science, Physics, Mechanical Engineering, or in a STEM related field
  • Experience with cutting-edge technology nodes (sub-10nm logic or leading-edge foundry nodes)
  • Proven track record collaborating with equipment and materials suppliers
  • Cross-functional leadership experience leading task forces for yield improvements and technology ramp
  • History of mentoring junior engineers and serving as technical focal point
  • Strong ownership mindset with ability to drive initiatives from concept to factory deployment
