Job Details:

Job Description: 

The Role and Impact As a CPU Validation Engineer, you will play a key role in validating and verifying next-generation processor designs, ensuring they meet Intel's high-quality standards. You will focus on developing test plans, creating test content and tools, and debugging silicon to identify and resolve issues. This position directly contributes to the successful launch of industry-leading CPU technologies, providing tangible impact on product innovation and performance. Business group Join Intel Corporation's Atom PG Val Tool team, a group dedicated to advancing Intel's processor technology through rigorous system validation and testing. This team plays a critical role in ensuring Intel's CPU designs meet quality benchmarks efficiently, aligning with Intel's broader mission of delivering cutting-edge computing solutions to the market. Key Responsibilities - Run custom designed test content to qualify feature functionality and perform failure triage - Analyze execution data and performance monitors to optimize the running of test content suites and to gauge product health in the shortest amount of time - Debug and isolate system/platform issues - Tune/optimize system hardware and software parameters to improve validation efficiency - Develop new software, automation, indicators and infrastructure tools to improve product validation efficiency and effectiveness

Qualifications:

Minimum Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Electrical/Electronics Engineering, or a related field, with 2-5 years of hands-on experience in CPU validation OR a Master's degree in a relevant field with no prior professional experience.
- Proficiency in Python and scripting languages.
- Strong knowledge of CPU architecture fundamentals, validation techniques, and debug processes.
- Experience working with post-silicon validation tools and methods.

Preferred Qualifications
- Hands-on experience in system-level or post-silicon validation or test development.
- Familiarity with CPU features, embedded systems, or platform debug tools.
- Proficiency scripting languages such as Python.
- Demonstrated problem-solving skills, analytical thinking, and ability to collaborate effectively within cross-functional teams.
- Passion for innovation and delivering high-quality results in alignment with Intel's vision of advancing technology.

Join Intel's CPU Validation team today and contribute to groundbreaking technology that shapes the future of computing.
