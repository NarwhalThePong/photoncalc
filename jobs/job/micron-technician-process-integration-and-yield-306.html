The Process Integration & Yield Technician supports the Process Integration Engineering (PIE) team by performing operational tasks that contribute to manufacturing process stability and yield improvement. The role includes data collection, basic analysis, documentation support, material disposition, product qualifications, and partnering with engineers to investigate production issues and process variability.

Main Responsibilities


Manufacturing Process & Yield Support

  • Assist engineers with collecting production data to support variability reduction and manufacturing process improvement.
  • Perform routine data checks and basic analysis using defined methods or templates provided by engineering.
  • Support yield ramp and quality monitoring activities by preparing data sets and performing preliminary reviews for abnormalities.

Issue Investigation Assistance

  • Provide hands on support during production issue investigations by gathering information, running predefined tests, and consolidating results for engineering review. on support during production issue investigations by gathering information, running predefined tests, and consolidating results for engineering review.
  • Partner with process, test, product, and quality teams by supporting communication flow and preparing required data or checklists.

Defense Line & Variability Reduction Support

  • Assist in executing activities related to Defense Line enhancement and process variability reduction under engineer guidance.
  • Complete assigned tasks to support line stability improvements, feeding observations and measurements back to engineers.

Documentation & Reporting

  • Organize analysis results and compile documentation with clear next steps as directed by engineers, ensuring timely progress toward project milestones.
  • Maintain accurate logs and records of assigned tasks, supporting structured reporting.
  • Integrates AI-assisted tools and insights into daily work to improve efficiency, quality, or effectiveness, exercising sound judgment and complying with organizational standards and legal requirements.
  • Contributes to a culture of continuous improvement by identifying, testing, and sharing AI-enabled enhancements within one’s scope of work.
