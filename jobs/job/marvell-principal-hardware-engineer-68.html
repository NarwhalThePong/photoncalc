Your Team, Your Impact

Marvell’s Hardware Engineering group designs the high-volume production boards and systems that bring our infrastructure semiconductor solutions to market. These boards power the AI data center, network security, and high-speed connectivity products that move, store, and secure the world’s data.

This group works closely with silicon, firmware, and system teams to turn deep system-level knowledge and leading intellectual property into reliable products for the global data economy, whether deployed at the network core or the edge.

What You Can Expect


The Principal Hardware Engineer will lead the design of these boards from concept through production, working on custom ASIC solutions built on 3nm technology and high-speed SerDes for large-scale data center applications.


In this role you will

  • Develop high density, high volume next generation products.
  • Work with Hardware and software engineers to design products, test and debug issues.
  • Provide technical and engineering support to strategic customers, including creation and presentation of power point files for customer review and discussions
  • Work directly with customers to address design challenges, debug failures, and ensure successful product integration.
  • Design high‑performance board‑level systems for storage, network security, and custom ASIC applications.
  • Manage designs with JDM partners to provide design guidance, support build readiness and ensure production quality.
  • Define, design, and review schematics from internal hardware teams and JDM, with emphasis on high‑speed signal integrity, power integrity, and cost‑optimized designs for high‑volume production.
  • Work closely with the internal board development, DVT and SI teams to make sure board issues are addressed, resolved, and closed
  • Conduct design reviews with clear documentation, and provide technical leadership throughout the product development cycle.
  • Collaborate with cross‑functional teams to ensure product performance, reliability, and quality.
  • Facilitate technical discussions and drive resolution of complex engineering issues.

What We're Looking For


  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering or related fields and 10-15 years of related professional experience. OR Master’s degree and/or PhD in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering or related fields with 5-10 years of experience.
  • 5+ years experience designing high speed digital circuitry on complex high density printed circuit board assemblies including Ethernet controllers, microcontrollers and processors
  • 5+ years experience in working with ODMs
  • 5+ years of experience in designing high density boards with fine pitch components and micro vias.
  • Expertise in High Speed designs, Interfaces such as DDR4/5, PCIe Gen5+, 100G Ethernet, QSPI, USB, using medium to high end CPUs
  • Good knowledge of power supply designs, hands on measurement experience
  • Good debug skills and experience in using lab equipment
  • Expertise in design for optimum signal and power integrity
  • Demonstrates customer-facing experience with excellent verbal and written communication skills
  • Understanding mechanical components and their usage in board design is required
  • Familiarity with Cadence Allegro schematics and layout tools
  • Proven critical-thinking abilities, strong problem-solving skills, and a sense of ownership for team and project outcomes
  • Basic Linux proficiency for system test environment setup (highly preferred)
  • Grounded, detail-oriented, always backs up ideas with facts.
