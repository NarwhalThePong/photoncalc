Principal Emulation Engineer — San Jose, CA  

Role Overview  

As a Principal Emulation Engineer, you will build and  operate  emulation environments that let verification teams stress-test complex ASICs before silicon.  You’ll  partner closely with design, verification, firmware, and systems engineers to model hardware-software interactions, exercise high-speed protocols, and isolate issues across RTL, transactors, test environments, and code. Your hands-on leadership will turn difficult debug problems into actionable fixes and improve confidence in each product milestone.  

Astera Labs is accelerating the infrastructure behind AI by delivering purpose-built connectivity solutions for rack-scale systems. In this role,  you’ll  help  validate  the switching, PCIe, Ethernet, memory, and storage interfaces that  connect  the  compute  fabric—enabling faster bring-up, stronger interoperability, and more reliable platforms as the company scales.  

Key Responsibilities  

  • Emulation and Prototyping  
  • Architect, build, and  maintain  emulation environments for complex ASICs and SoCs.  
  • Integrate RTL, transactors, protocol models, software, and test infrastructure to enable meaningful  hardware-software  co-simulation.  
  • Enable verification teams to stress-test designs with scalable, repeatable emulation workflows.  
  • Develop reusable models, utilities, and debug methodologies that accelerate pre-silicon validation.  
  • Protocol and SoC Verification  
  • Verify  hardware-software  interfaces and system behaviors in partnership with logic designers, firmware engineers, and verification teams.  
  • Exercise and debug PCI Express Gen 3 and above, Ethernet, DDR,  NVMe , SPI, I2C/I3C, and similar interfaces.  
  • Analyze protocol behavior, functional coverage, performance, and corner cases across complex  server , storage, and networking systems.  
  • Translate system requirements into practical emulation scenarios, tests, checkers, and measurable verification goals.  
  • Debug and Technical Leadership  
  • Debug design, environment, transactor, software, and test-code issues across the emulation stack.  
  • Identify  software and hardware co-simulation limitations and apply effective isolation and debug techniques.  
  • Partner with cross-functional teams to prioritize a dynamic workload, resolve issues quickly, and drive risks to closure.  
  • Provide technical leadership, communicate clearly, and  operate  effectively with minimal guidance and supervision.  

Basic Qualifications  

  • Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering , Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or  a related  technical field.  
  • 8–12 years of experience supporting or developing complex SoC or silicon products for server, storage, or networking applications.  
  • Experience working with logic designers to architect, specify, and verify hardware-software interfaces on complex SoCs.  
  • Working knowledge of PCI Express Gen 3 or later, CXL,  UALink , Ethernet, DDR,  NVMe , SPI, I2C/I3C, or similar communication protocols.  
  • High  proficiency  in  SystemVerilog  and hardware verification environments.  
  • Experience understanding software and hardware co-simulation limitations and applying effective debug methods.  
  • Programming and scripting experience in Python, Perl, C, or similar languages.  
  • Professional , entrepreneurial, and open-minded approach with a can-do attitude, strong prioritization skills, and a customer-focused mindset.  
  • Authorized to work in the United States and available to start  immediately .  

Preferred Qualifications  

  • Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering , Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or  a related  technical field.  
  • Hands-on experience with Palladium, Zebu, Veloce, HAPS, FPGA prototyping, or similar emulation and prototyping technologies.  
  • Experience developing models for emulation and prototyping and working in non-UVM verification environments.  
  • Experience building protocol transactors, behavioral models, software drivers, or system-level validation infrastructure.  
  • Based in the San Jose area or open to relocation; comfortable working fully in person.  

Salary range is $207,000 to $230,000 depending on experience, level, and business  need . This role may be eligible for discretionary  bonus ,  incentives  and benefits.
