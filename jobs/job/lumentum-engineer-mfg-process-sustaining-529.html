Product Engineering (Core Accountability)

  • Serve as the Product Engineer for assigned Cloud products, owning product definition, technical performance, and manufacturing execution.
  • Act as the primary technical point of contact for product-related topics between Manufacturing and R&D.
  • Drive product-level technical analysis, issue resolution, and change implementation.

Manufacturing & Product Industrialization

  • Support product industrialization from development through volume production.
  • Ensure product readiness for manufacturing, including BOM accuracy, drawings, specifications, and configuration control.
  • Work closely with Process Engineering to ensure effective integration of product design with manufacturing processes.

New Product Introduction (NPI) & Lifecycle Support

  • Execute product engineering activities for NPI, product transfer, and production ramp.
  • Support Design for Manufacturability, Assembly, Test, and Reliability (DfX) in collaboration with R&D and Process Engineering.
  • Support product qualification, documentation release, and engineering change management.

Yield, Quality & Cost Performance

  • Support analysis and improvement of product yield, performance, and quality metrics.
  • Participate in root cause analysis and corrective actions for product-related manufacturing and field issues.
  • Contribute to cost reduction initiatives through product optimization and engineering changes.

Cross-Functional & Global Collaboration

  • Collaborate with local and global engineering teams to support product readiness and execution.
  • Communicate technical status, risks, and data-driven recommendations to stakeholders.
  • Support alignment across functions to ensure smooth manufacturing execution.

Safety, Health & Environmental (EHS) Responsibility

  • Comply with all EHS policies, procedures, and standards.
  • Actively support EHS initiatives and continuous improvement actions.
  • Promote safe work practices and contribute to a Zero Accident Culture.
