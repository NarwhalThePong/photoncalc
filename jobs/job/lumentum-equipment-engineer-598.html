As a member of the Equipment Engineering staff, the Wafer Fab Equipment Engineer will have ownership of one or more tool sets within a wafer fab manufacturing operation. Equipment Engineers are responsible for the overall availability and performance of their assigned equipment sets in accordance with SEMI E10.


Responsibilities will include:


  • Design and implement maintenance programs to improve tool availability and reduce cost of ownership;
  • Analyze equipment performance metrics (MTBF, uptime, MTTR, etc.) to determine areas for improvement;
  • Write PM procedures, checklists, troubleshooting guides, and work instructions for your assigned equipment set;
  • Evaluate and recommend the selection of new process equipment;
  • Plan, manage and execute all assigned equipment installations or upgrades to the Fab or manufacturing equipment;
  • Continuous improvement of reliability, availability, yield and quality as well as cost and scrap reduction support;
  • Assists equipment maintenance technicians in the troubleshooting of critical down tools;
  • Establish and maintain spare part Bill of Materials (BOMs) for assigned equipment.

Requirements:


  • Solid background working in Equipment Maintenance supporting a 24/7 clean room manufacturing environment
  • Ability to generate equipment work instructions
  • Strong organizational, time management, and communication skills
  • Basic understanding of Environmental, health, and and Safety requirements as related to Equipment Maintenance
  • Basic understanding Electronics with the ability to read schematics
  • Basic understanding of Electromechanical, Vacuum, and Pneumatics
  • Can lead Equipment Maintenance in the troubleshooting to root cause of down equipment

Strong computer skills to include Word, Excel, and Project
