About Analog Devices

Analog Devices, Inc. (NASDAQ: ADI ) is a global semiconductor leader that bridges the physical and digital worlds to enable breakthroughs at the Intelligent Edge. ADI combines analog, digital, AI, and software technologies into solutions that combat climate change, reliably connect humans and the world, and help drive advancements in automation and robotics, mobility, healthcare, energy and data centers. With revenue of more than $11 billion in FY25, ADI ensures today's innovators stay Ahead of What's Possible. Learn more at www.analog.com and on LinkedIn and X .

At Analog Devices Automotive Business Unit, you will be able to work together with a global team, who has designed and introduced many successful products that won sockets from top car makers in Europe, Asia, and the USA. We, as GMSL SerDes group, are developing integrated circuits for automotive high-speed serial data communications for next generation safety and infotainment systems.

As a digital design engineer, you will be part of a global design team, and have opportunity to work with cross functional teams to design complex mixed-signal integrated circuits developed in GMSL SerDes group.

Main responsibilities:

  • Supporting feasibility and definition of product requirements of high-speed data communication products with the Product Definer responsible of the product.
  • RTL design of owned blocks using SystemVerilog
  • Creating/managing verification plans by understanding the top-level design and working with other design engineers.
  • Block and top-level RTL and gate level verification and regressions using SystemVerilog
  • Supporting Synthesis, STA, DFT and Place and Route supervision
  • Bench validation of owned functions
  • Working with test engineers in helping them finalizing their test programs towards product introduction
  • Answering customer & apps questions as needed

Preferred Qualifications & Experience:

  • MSEE with specialization in IC Design (VLSI).
  • At least 8+ years of experience in digital design
  • High speed and low power RTL Design and Verification using Hardware Description Languages (preferably System Verilog)
  • Hands-on experience in digital design & verification tools and methodologies
  • Experience in synthesis, DFT and supervision of timing driven Place and Route and UVM Verification background and experience in Video/Audio communication and processing is a plus
  • Good Communication / presentation skills in English and teamwork skills
