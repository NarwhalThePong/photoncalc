Job Description:

Senior Digital / Mixed-Signal IC Design or Verification Engineer

Location: Mendota Heights, MN or Broomfield, CO

Job Description Summary

Join Broadcom, a global technology leader and Fortune 500 powerhouse at the forefront of semiconductor innovation and AI infrastructure. Within our specialized Mixed-Signal ASIC division, we offer an exciting position in the explosive growth area of Data Center Enterprise Storage, where we build the critical IP responsible for providing low-power, high-performance analog and mixed-signal products. In this role, you will develop advanced semiconductor devices to support high-capacity storage needs across all top-tier service providers. You will work with highly talented teams to meet company business goals while growing your skills. 

We are looking for a Senior Digital IC Design or Mixed-Signal Verification Engineer to act as a methodology leader and key technical bridge for our worldwide engineering organization. This unique opportunity allows you to drive quality initiatives, pioneer modern automation (including AI-assisted workflows), and establish robust methodologies across our US and Asia design centers.

Key Responsibilities

  • Act as the primary technical liaison between local US design teams and global cross-functional teams, translating complex specifications into seamless execution.
  • Provide technical support through all phases of product development: testing, yield analysis, characterization, reliability analysis, qualification, and release to manufacturing. 
  • Adhere to strict schedule, die size, and power commitments. 
  • Conduct detailed design reviews with internal and external customers.

Digital IC Design: 

  • Define system requirements, digital control functions, and block partitions for next-generation preamplifier products. 
  • Perform digital logic design, Verilog coding, logic synthesis, formal verification, static timing analysis, and place and route. 
  • Support integrating the digital core with analog circuitry into a completed integrated circuit. 
  • Perform transistor-level high-speed digital IC design for custom cells and blocks. 

Digital Mixed Signal Verification:

  • Architect, develop, and maintain advanced UVM-based testbenches for the functional verification of complex mixed-signal ASICs. 
  • Own verification efforts at both the block and system levels for major tape-out projects, driving functional and code coverage to closure.
  • Champion verification quality by integrating new methodologies (e.g., SVA, UVM/OVM) to reduce mixed-signal validation bottlenecks.
  • Lead initiatives to explore and integrate modern automation and AI-assisted verification workflows to accelerate testbench generation and debug efficiency.

Qualifications & Skills

Core Requirements (All Candidates):

  • Education/Experience: Bachelors in Engineering and 8+ years of related experience; or Masters degree in Engineering and 6+ years of related experience
  • Communication: Excellent spoken and written communication skills, and the proven ability to coordinate technical problem-solving across global teams. 
  • Analytical: Proven ability to apply advanced math and physics to solve complex engineering problems. 
  • Bonus: An understanding of silicon process technologies (CMOS and Bipolar) and device physics is a strong plus. Expertise across both design and verification domains is highly desired.

Specialized Skills (Design Focus):

  • Strong hands-on experience with ASIC EDA tools used in synthesis, simulation, static timing analysis, formal verification, and place & route. 
  • Deep familiarity with Verilog/VHDL RTL coding and logic design.

Specialized Skills (Verification Focus):

  • Deep expertise in coverage-driven verification processes and frameworks (UVM/OVM). 
  • Strong understanding of event-driven simulator-based modeling techniques and how digital logic interacts with analog boundaries.
  • Hands-on experience with SystemVerilog Assertions (SVA) and behavioral modeling of analog circuits. 
  • Proficiency in scripting (Python, Perl, TCL, etc.) to automate verification workflows, manage regressions, and streamline simulation infrastructure.
