Job Summary:

The Senior Validation Engineer will be responsible for developing and implementing the methods and techniques to validate the performance of various electro-optical subsystems, drive measures in the Engineering environment, debug issues, and supervise larger quantity builds in manufacturing

Responsibilities:

  • Review electro-optical design from simple PCBA + Optical assemblies to more complex designs where multiple PCBA & optical parts work together. Including integration of optics, electronics, firmware, software, and thermal design.
  • Identify key parameters to measure and methods for the specific measurements, in collaboration with the Design team.
  • Write the full validation plan for the characterization of new designs, estimate time to complete measures on large quantities of devices.
  • Design and run experiments to validate design functionality and performance: from building laboratory setups comprised of light sources, detector, switches; function generators, oscilloscopes, etc., to developing software scrips and algorithms to collect relevant data, perform data analysis and extract key performance parameters.
  • Own the validation effort: supervise the measures, provide reports with expected yield indications for the operation team. Identify issues, provide detailed descriptions and help identify root cause, enable design and/or process engineer to identify fixes. Have a high awareness of milestones and impact of delay on overall program.
  • Work with operations team / manufacturing to review build data and yield indication.

Experience/Qualifications:

  • Bachelor's degree or above in Engineering or physical science with 8+ years of industry experience, relevant to validating and testing optical systems.
    Experience working with optical and/or electrical test equipment such as power meters, optical backscatter reflectometers (OBR), optical spectrum analyzer (OSA), Oscilloscopes, function generators (AWG), vector network analyzer (VNA).
    Experience with high-speed electro-optical devices and test equipment, such as electro-optical modulators, RF detectors, pulsed lasers, and optical sampling oscilloscopes.
    Experience developing test methods and optimization, tuning/calibration algorithms.
    Ability to understand and write scripts to communicate and control instruments.
  • Experience with Python is preferred.
    Experience with EDVT & Fast Fail methodology.
  • Familiarity with programming CPUs to interface with FPGA's is a plus.
