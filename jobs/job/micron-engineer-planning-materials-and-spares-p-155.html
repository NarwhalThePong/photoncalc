The Material Planning Engineer will be responsible for working within a team of individuals to manage spares and materials forecast for the Fab tools. The engineer will work with the various Fab Process areas to ensure spares and materials are accurately forecasted and liaise with Procurement to ensure sufficient stocking levels. The engineer will also work closely with the cost management and finance team on budgeting, compliance to budget and cost benchmarking. This responsibility may include identifying cost effective sustaining manufacturing improvements, minimizing regular expenditures, and helping to promote a cost-conscious culture.

  • Develop and maintain accurate Build of Material planning model of assigned commodities or Spares PM Maintenance Plan planning model of assigned Fab area.
  • Apply supply chain engineering concepts to manage forecast requirement and forecast accuracy performance to ensure no inventory shortage or material expiry issues that can cause production line stoppage.
  • Determine actions needed for excursion control and method improvement.
  • Design and coordinate projects with several different departments to continuously improve forecast accuracy with high efficiency.
  • Interface with research & development and process engineering to build BOM and forecast platform using Web Portal and Database.
  • Coordinate material or parts shortage and expiry issues with relevant stakeholders in order to zero out impact to company.
  • Develop reporting and automation capabilities to improve process efficiency and effectiveness.
  • Integrates AI-assisted tools and insights into daily work to improve efficiency, quality, or effectiveness, exercising sound judgment and complying with organizational standards and legal requirements. 
  • Contributes to a culture of continuous improvement by identifying, testing, and sharing AI-enabled enhancements within one’s scope of work.
