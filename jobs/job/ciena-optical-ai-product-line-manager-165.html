As a member of Ciena's Optical Systems Product Line Management team, you will own the strategy and commercialization of Optical networking solutions for AI connectivity across Data Center Interconnect (DCI), metro and long-haul domains. You will lead engagements with North American Communications Service Providers (CSPs) on how to engineer and monetize their optical networks for AI training and inference traffic. Working with the customer, Sales and Sales Engineering, you will build account-specific solution plans that support CSP business cases for AI connectivity demand, and you will translate what you learn into Ciena's product roadmap and solution portfolio.

This role sits at the intersection of network architecture and commercial strategy. You will be equally credible debating coherent optics and fabric design with a CSP's technology and product strategy teams and building the revenue and TCO case that justifies their investment.

Within twelve months, you will have established Ciena as the architectural reference point for AI connectivity with a defined set of North American CSP accounts.

You have personally taken a technical architecture position and converted it into a customer investment decision. You have driven technical engagements on networking for AI workloads with hyperscalers, neoclouds, CSPs or enterprises, and have worked closely with customer influencers across product strategy, engineering and technology introduction functions. Experience translating cloud networking practice into service provider environments is a strong advantage.

This role is based in North America.

Must-have

  • Deep working knowledge of optical and IP networking architectures across DCI, metro and long-haul
  • Working knowledge of AI cluster networking: scale-out and scale-across fabrics, distributed training traffic patterns, inference latency and distribution requirements
  • Ability to build and defend customer business cases, including service monetization models and TCO/capex/opex analysis
  • Ability to operate credibly across both architecture and commercial strategy, engaging a CSP technology office on design, and their strategy and finance stakeholders on the investment case
  • Ability to gather and translate customer and internal stakeholder requirements into product specifications, proposals, and RFI/RFP responses
  • Ability to work with cross-functional engineering, R&D and PLM teams to define and prioritize roadmap features
  • Strong analytical and quantitative skills; able to use data and metrics to support a strategic narrative
  • Clear communication and presentation skills across technical and non-technical audiences, including executive stakeholders
  • Ability to manage multiple initiatives with minimal direction in a globally matrixed, cross-functional environment
  • Willingness to travel approximately 25%, primarily within North America

Education

  • Bachelor's degree in Engineering (Electrical, Computer Science or related), or equivalent experience — required
  • Master's degree — preferred

Experience

Required

  • 10+ years in telecommunications or data communications, spanning networking hardware and software, architecture, product line management or solution development. Prior experience in product management, solutions architecture, technical marketing, or sales engineering
  • Demonstrated technical engagements on networking for AI workloads with hyperscalers, neoclouds, CSPs or enterprises
  • Track record of converting a technical architecture position into a customer investment decision

Preferred

  • Experience translating cloud networking practice into service provider environments
  • Experience with coherent optical technologies
  • Exposure to industry forums, standards bodies or global customer engagements

If you meet most of this and can demonstrate the combination of technical depth and commercial judgment we are describing, we would like to hear from you.
