Department Introduction

The Systems Integration Group (SIG) designs advanced semiconductor memory test equipment that enables next-generation memory technologies. We work across disciplines to build scalable, reliable, high-performance test solutions.

Join the team building SSD tester platforms for tomorrow’s AI infrastructure!

Summary:

We are looking for a hands-on hardware systems leader to architect SSD tester equipment platforms. This role focuses on tester hardware architecture and platform-level electrical design, integrating host compute, PCIe interfaces, high-speed interconnects, DUT interface hardware, power delivery, sideband control, firmware/software interfaces, and manufacturing needs into a complete test system.

Responsibilities

  • Define t ester system architecture, requirements, performance models, interfaces, and specifications.

  • Own platform architecture across host compute , PCIe topology, high-speed interconnects, DUT interface hardware, power delivery, and rack/module integration.

  • Partner with customers and internal teams to shape tester strategy, requirements, and roadmaps.

  • Drive hardware designs from concept through delivery supporting component selection, schematic entry/review, high-speed implementation, lab bring-up, system integration, and verification

  • Collaborate across electrical, mechanical, software, and manufacturing teams to deliver scalable, secure tester systems with quality, performance, reliability, and manufacturability.

  • Modernize existing systems to improve performance, efficiency, reliability, and maintainability.

  • Mentor engineers and represent the team in partner and stakeholder technical discussions.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering , Computer Engineering, or related field. with 7+ years of experience in electrical engineering or system-level hardware design.

  • Experience with computer architecture, system interfaces, digital/analog hardware, power delivery, and system-level integration.

  • Hands-on experience with PCB schematic design/review, BOM development, component selection, board-level tradeoffs, and high-speed hardware considerations including SI/PI, thermal constraints, cost, and manufacturability.

  • Familiarity with lab debug equipment such as oscilloscopes, power supplies, and protocol analyzers.

  • Strong multi-functional interpersonal skills .

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with PCIe, NVMe , Ethernet, or other high-speed interfaces.

  • Experience designing computer systems or hardware platforms such as motherboards, SSD validation test benches, or related system-level hardware.

  • Familiarity with SSD validation, qualification, or production/manufacturing test workflows.

  • Experience leading technical work or guiding engineering decisions.

  • Experience using Linux-based systems.

  • Understanding of industry standards such as JEDEC or PCI-SIG.
