Position Overview:

Artificial intelligence is transforming the world, and High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) is at the center of that transformation. As a Senior or Staff Engineer on the Dry Etch R&D team, you will play a key role in developing and scaling plasma‑based etch processes that enable next‑generation packaging technologies. This role offers deep technical ownership, broad cross‑functional collaboration, and the opportunity to influence long‑term technology roadmaps from early research through high‑volume manufacturing.

Key Responsibilities

Collateral Acquisition & Access Control

  • Coordinate secure download of foundry collateral (e.g., TSMC PDKs, IP bundles) from approved channels
  • Track delivery schedules and completeness of collateral versus plan
  • Enforce NDA and access-control policies for restricted foundry data

PDK Installation & Environment Setup

  • Install and configure PDKs, rule decks, models, and libraries in standardized environments
  • Align toolchain versions with foundry recommendations
  • Maintain directory structures and environment setup for multi-node support

Validation & Testing (Core Responsibility)

  • Complete PDK validation pipeline:
Unit testing (DRC/LVS/ERC/PERC, models, libraries)

1. Smoke testing of end-to-end CAD flows

Regression testing versus prior PDK releases
  • Confirm that all validation checkpoints are satisfied before transferring to establish teams
