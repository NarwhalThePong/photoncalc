Job Description

ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:

  • Responsible for managing Hard Disk Drive (HDD) inventory spread across numerous customer teams which includes loading and unloading drives in customer systems.
  • Responsible for tracking and recording inventory; providing general lab support, packaging, shipping, and receiving.
  • Executes customer testing (including performance benchmarks and electrical tests), updating firmware, and reporting problems as they occur to the engineering team.
  • Adheres to SIT lab test procedures and communicate written and verbal status.

Qualifications

REQUIRED:

  • The candidate must have a working knowledge of Linux system environments. This system knowledge must include the ability to setup complex computer systems and a basic understanding of system error logs.
  • Must be familiar or willing to learn how to work with test equipment, such as bus analyzers.
  • Adhere to all safety protocols and regulations.
  • Use appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE).
  • Report any safety hazards or incidents immediately.
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills.

PREFERRED:

  • Experience using pallet jacks or hand trucks.
  • Experience using hand tools or powered screwdrivers.
