Registered Apprenticeship Program - Process Technician

Micron · Boise, ID - Main Site · posted 2026-10-19

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

Launch your career with Micron's Registered Apprenticeship Program, where you'll earn a paycheck, gain hands-on experience, complete a college degree, and build the technical skills needed for a long-term career in semiconductor manufacturing.

Do you enjoy working with technology, solving complex problems, and learning how advanced manufacturing systems operate? Micron's Registered Apprenticeship Program offers a unique opportunity to combine classroom learning with real-world experience in a cutting-edge manufacturing environment.

As a Technician Apprentice at Micron Technology in Boise, Idaho, you will work in our world-class Fab while completing the Semiconductor Manufacturing Technician (SMT) Certificate and earning a STEM-focused Associate of Applied Science (AAS) degree through the College of Western Idaho (CWI).

Our apprenticeship follows an earn-and-learn model, combining paid on-the-job training, structured mentorship, technical education, and career development. Upon completion, apprentices earn a U.S. Department of Labor-recognized credential, an associate degree, and the experience needed to pursue long-term career opportunities within Micron.

As a Process Technician Apprentice, you will learn to monitor, control, troubleshoot, and improve semiconductor manufacturing processes used to produce leading-edge memory technology. Working alongside production teams, technicians, and engineers, you will support safe, efficient, and reliable fab operations while developing the technical skills needed for a long-term career in advanced manufacturing.

You will assist with monitoring process performance, identifying and resolving process-related issues, collecting and analyzing data, and supporting continuous improvement efforts. Using technical, analytical, and problem-solving skills, you'll gain hands-on experience with advanced semiconductor manufacturing processes and technologies while helping maintain product quality, yield, and operational excellence.

Key Responsibilities

  • Partner with production, engineering, and technician teams to support daily fab operations
  • Monitor process performance, identify issues, and support operational efficiency
  • Troubleshoot process-related issues and support resolution efforts
  • Collect, analyze, and document process data to support operational decision-making
  • Accurately document work performed and communicate updates clearly
  • Complete required safety training and follow all safety protocols
  • Support continuous improvement efforts across the fab
  • Adhere to all apprenticeship program guidelines and expectations
  • Leverage Artificial Intelligence (AI) and other digital tools to support learning, problem-solving, data analysis, and operational efficiency

Minimum Qualifications

  • High School Diploma or GED
  • Willingness and ability to enroll in and successfully complete the Semiconductor Manufacturing Technician (SMT) Certificate and a STEM-focused Associate of Applied Science (AAS) degree through the College of Western Idaho (CWI) as a required component of the apprenticeship program
  • Basic to intermediate PC skills (Microsoft Office Suite, Teams, Zoom)
  • Strong time management and organizational skills
  • Reliable written and verbal communication skills
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced, team-oriented environment
  • Ability to work rotating shifts, including nights, while balancing apprenticeship coursework. Class schedules are considered when shift assignments are made.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Strong interest in technology, manufacturing, and process improvement
  • Previous coursework or experience in STEM, manufacturing, industrial technology, robotics, electronics, chemistry, physics, or related fields
  • Experience collecting, analyzing, or working with data
  • Strong analytical, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills

Selection Process

Candidates advancing through the hiring process will be required to:

  • Complete a math assessment to evaluate foundational skills needed for success in the apprenticeship program
  • Participate in interviews and/or an onsite hiring event
  • Successfully complete all required pre-employment and apprenticeship screening requirements
