Job Description

ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:

Software, Automation & Equipment Engineering

  • Design, develop, test, integrate, deploy, and support PLC/HMI and equipment software for manufacturing equipment and automation projects.
  • Lead software projects from requirements definition through development, validation, production deployment, and technical handover, ensuring delivery within scope, schedule, quality, and performance requirements.
  • Develop reliable, maintainable, scalable, and standardized software solutions using software engineering best practices, reusable components, technical documentation, and revision control.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams and equipment suppliers to define requirements, integrate systems, assess technical risks, and implement effective solutions.
  • Review software designs and control logic, provide technical guidance and knowledge transfer, and support complex software and control-system issues when required.

Smart Manufacturing, Innovation & Continuous Improvement

  • Develop and implement innovative solutions using automation, data analytics, IoT, AI/ML, Computer Vision and digital technologies, including proof-of-concept solutions, equipment data collection, monitoring, dashboards, predictive quality, and smart maintenance applications.
  • Identify opportunities to improve quality, yield, productivity, cycle time, OEE, equipment availability, downtime, labor efficiency, and operational efficiency.
  • Validate results, apply structured problem-solving methods, standardize successful solutions for replication, and scale them across applicable equipment and production lines.

Qualifications

REQUIRED:

  • Bachelor's or Master's Degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics Engineering, Robotics/Automation Engineering, or a related technical field.
  • Experience at least 3 years in Manufacturing process
  • Basic understanding of manufacturing processes and equipment
  • Strong hands-on experience with PLC/HMI programming and manufacturing automation systems.
  • Familiarity with automation concepts and control systems
  • Knowledge of Six Sigma, Lean Manufacturing, or Industry 4.0 concepts 
  • Knowledge of HDD Process & Technology, HDD Quality Management, ESD & Contamination

SKILLS:

  • Proficiency in PLC/HMI  programming.
  • Knowledge of Programming C#, C++, Python, AI, Computer Vision, Image Processing, Machine Learning technologies and web applications.
  • Data analysis skills (Excel, basic statistics)
  • Demonstrate a good level of English proficiency (Speaking/Written)
