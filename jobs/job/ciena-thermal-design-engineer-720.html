How You Will Contribute

We are seeking a motivated and talented Thermal Design Engineer to join our team. In this role, you will help design and develop next-generation optical switching and transport products, guiding thermal solutions from concept through production. Working closely with cross-functional teams, you will be responsible for ensuring that complex electro-optical systems meet thermal performance, reliability, and operational requirements across a wide range of environmental conditions. Your work will combine thermal analysis, CFD simulation, and experimental validation to drive innovative and robust product designs.

As a key member of the design organization, you will:

  • Define and develop thermal architectures for new products and technologies
  • Perform thermal analysis and CFD simulations to evaluate and optimize product designs
  • Plan and execute laboratory testing to validate thermal performance and correlate results with simulation models
  • Identify thermal risks and develop mitigation strategies throughout the product development cycle
  • Collaborate with mechanical, electrical, optical, software, and manufacturing teams to deliver high-quality products
  • Support the introduction of new products into manufacturing and contribute to sustaining engineering activities for existing platforms

Required Qualifications

  • Master's or PhD degree in Mechanical Engineering with specialization in Heat Transfer and Fluid Mechanics
  • 5+ years of experience in thermal design and verification of electronic systems
  • Hands-on experience with Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) software
  • Strong background in engineering laboratory testing, data analysis, and experimental methods
  • Excellent communication, collaboration, and interpersonal skills

Assets

  • Experience using FloTHERM for thermal modeling and simulation
  • Experience with Creo CAD software
  • Knowledge of mechanical design, manufacturing processes, and product fabrication techniques
