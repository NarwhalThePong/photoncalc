Job Description

ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:

The Technical PhD Intern will support machining and lapping process development for next-generation wafer technologies.

  • The role offers hands-on experience applying modeling, simulation, data analytics, AI/ML, software, and hardware development to improve process capability, quality, and manufacturing performance.
  • Support machining and lapping process development projects.
  • Develop and apply modeling and simulation methods to understand process behavior.
  •  Analyze process and equipment data using statistical and AI/ML techniques.
  • Assist in developing software tools, algorithms, and engineering analytics solutions.
  • Support experimental design, process characterization, and root cause investigations.
  • Collaborate with process, equipment, software, and data engineering teams.
  • Document and present technical findings and recommendations.

Qualifications

REQUIRED:

  • Currently pursuing a PhD degree in Mechanical Engineering, Materials Science, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Physics, Data Science, or equivalent experience
  • Internship period must be at least 4 months; can be on-site at Bangpa-in, Ayutthaya

PREFERRED:

  • Research experience in machining, lapping, precision engineering, manufacturing processes, or advanced materials. 
  • Knowledge of Design of Experiments (DOE), statistical analysis, and process optimization.
  • Familiarity with sensor systems, automation, or hardware development.

SKILLS:

  • Experience in modeling, simulation, data analysis, or computational research. 
  • Experience with AI/ML, data analytics, or predictive modeling
  • Programming experience in Python, MATLAB, C/C++, or similar.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, teamwork, and communication skills.
  • Strong with Root Cause Analysis and Technical Communication
  • Strong written and verbal English communication
