Responsibilities:

  • Strategize and lead productivity, yield, quality, cost improvement engagement with global sites and network team via process optimization, new/FOAK chemistry & technology qualification, CIP development collaboration with suppliers.
  • Collaborate with cross functional team for breakthrough in defects reduction and particle removal efficiency enhancements.
  • Develop roadmap for process and hardware CIP implementation
  • Drive process & hardware improvement development with an emphasis on safety, defect reduction, cost efficiency, scale, quality, and sustainability in Wet Process (SWC, Bench, Scrub, Backside, Bevel, etc.).
  • Collaborate with suppliers to establish equipment start-up acceptance criteria and tool service requirements, and work with global facilities to address new tool requirements.
  • Improve tool efficiency and utilization across all Micron sites through benchmarking and innovative pathfinding solutions.
  • Set up IoT sensors for defense line strengthening in Wet tools and develop new solutions for excursion prevention.
  • Establish and improve process conditions and technology, upgrade process capability, and reduce production costs.
  • Enable new solutions for energy savings, water conservation and waste reductions.

Qualifications & Skills:

  • Proven experience in process & hardware improvement and development within the semiconductor industry.
  • Remarkable skill in developing and implementing hardware CIP roadmaps and start-up acceptance criteria.
  • Strong analytical skills for evaluating, promoting, and planning new equipment and materials.
  • Tangible results in boosting tool performance, establishing process parameters, and streamlining process management projects.
  • Ability to strictly adhere to safety, quality, and sustainability standards while driving innovative solutions.

Education and Experience:

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Engineering, Materials Science, or a related field, or equivalent experience.
  • Extensive experience in semiconductor equipment and process management in Wet Process
  • Proven track record in maturing tool performance and establishing process parameters for semiconductor equipment.
  • Experience in abnormal analysis and improvement, and a solid history of successful project implementation.
