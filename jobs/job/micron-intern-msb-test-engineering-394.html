Department
Test Engineering, Electrical Failure Analysis and Physical Failure Analysis

Project Title
AI-Driven Semiconductor Failure Analysis and Laboratory Digitalisation

Project Description
This internship provides an opportunity to gain hands-on exposure to semiconductor failure analysis methodologies, laboratory automation, and AI-Enabled engineering workflows. The project focuses on leveraging data analytics, digital tools, and Artificial Intelligence to enhance failure analysis efficiency, improve knowledge management, and accelerate engineering decision-making.
The intern will learn how Electrical Failure Analysis and Physical Failure Analysis techniques are used to identify the root causes of product and process issues across memory technologies in a high-volume manufacturing environment.

Objective of the Project

  • Develop a digital or AI-Enabled solution that improves failure analysis workflow efficiency.

  • Apply data analytics and visualization tools to enhance laboratory process visibility.

  • Explore automation opportunities that improve engineering productivity and knowledge accessibility.

  • Develop an understanding of how failure analysis contributes to product quality and manufacturing excellence.
     

Opportunities for Full Time Employment
High-performing interns who demonstrate strong technical capability, learning agility, and successful project outcomes may be considered for future internship or full-time employment opportunities within Micron’s engineering organization, subject to business needs and hiring requirements.

Project Scope

  • Analyze laboratory operational metrics, turnaround-time trends, and relevant engineering datasets.

  • Develop dashboards, automation tools, or knowledge-management solutions for selected failure analysis workflows.

  • Explore Micron-qualified Artificial Intelligence, Generative AI, and AI Assistant tools for knowledge retrieval, reporting, and workflow efficiency.

  • Collaborate with Test Engineering stakeholders to understand failure analysis requirements and validate proposed solutions.
     

Learning Opportunities

  • Learn about semiconductor device structures, packaging technologies, and memory manufacturing processes.

  • Gain exposure to Electrical Failure Analysis and Physical Failure Analysis techniques used in root-cause investigations.

  • Apply data analytics and visualization tools to laboratory operational metrics and engineering datasets.

  • Explore automation technologies using Python, Power Automate, Microsoft Copilot, or other Micron-qualified digital tools.

  • Strengthen technical communication by presenting project findings and recommendations to engineering stakeholders.


Deliverables

  • A digital or AI-Enabled solution that improves a selected failure analysis workflow.

  • A dashboard, automation tool, or knowledge-management solution that enables faster data-driven decision-making.

  • An assessment of improvements in productivity, process visibility, or engineering effectiveness.

  • Final project documentation and presentation summarizing the methodology, findings, outcomes, and recommendations.
     

Impact of the Project

  • Improve visibility and efficiency within selected failure analysis workflows.

  • Enable faster access to relevant engineering data and technical knowledge.

  • Enhance engineering decision-making through data analytics and AI-Enabled insights.

  • Identify opportunities for continued laboratory digitalisation and automation.
     

Skillsets Required

  • Basic programming knowledge in Python, Structured Query Language, or a similar language.

  • Familiarity with data analytics, data visualization, automation tools, or workflow development.

  • Interest in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Generative AI, and AI-Enabled engineering applications.

  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills.

  • Familiarity with Microsoft Copilot and workflow platforms is advantageous.
     

Course of Interest
The ideal candidate should be pursuing a Bachelor’s Degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Mechanical Engineering, Materials Science, or a related field of study.

Duration of Period
The ideal candidate should be able to commit to a full time internship period of 5 months from Jan to Jun 2027.
