At Micron, we're not just offering a job; we're presenting a chance to be part of a team that develops the future of memory and storage solutions. Job Responsibilities

  • Proficient at Synthesis, Static Timing Analysis (STA), and Logic Equivalence Checking (LEC)
  • Ability to understand and integrate new mixed signal complex IPs
  • Expertise in developing constraints and timing models
  • Manage synthesis to timing closure, ensuring all checks for silicon success
  • Experience with hierarchical designs involving high-frequency clocks
  • Proficient with scripting, automation, and EDA tools
  • Proficiency with AI and AI-enabled workflows
  • Solid understanding of low power builds involving multiple power domains
  • Identify and meet requirements for critical paths of multi-GHz builds
  • Collaborate effectively in multi-functional and globally dispersed teams

Requirements

  • M.S./M.Tech, BS/BE in Electronics or related field
  • Minimum of 6+ years of relevant experience
  • Proven ability to successfully implement complex builds
  • Ambitious and determined to compete at a global level
