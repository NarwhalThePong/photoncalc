Job / Role Summary

The MTS is a senior technical contributor who leads critical dry etch modules and advances next-generation NAND scaling by owning key processes, driving equipment roadmaps with hardware teams, and leading multi-functional problem solving for yield, defectivity, and manufacturability. The role requires deep dry etch fundamentals, strong predictive/mitigation thinking, and mentorship contributions aligned to technical leadership expectations.

Main Responsibilities

  • Lead development of dry etch technology and essential modules; serve as a dry etch area expert.

  • Develop/innovate dry etch processes to meet structural and electrical specifications; demonstrate mechanistic understanding of parameter-to-outcome relationships.

  • Work closely with hardware teams to develop the equipment roadmap for current and upcoming NAND products .

  • Lead multi-functional teams to solve complex structural problems; present technology development plans for current/upcoming nodes and build mitigation plans for future issues.

  • Lead supplier head-to-heads; drive RCA, SPC, and corrective actions to close gaps.

Other Responsibilities

  • Mentor less-experienced engineers and contribute toward technical leadership development expectations.

  • Strengthen cross-area collaboration by identifying opportunities and driving toward collaborative solutions.

  • Integrates AI-assisted tools and insights into daily work to improve efficiency, quality, or effectiveness, exercising sound judgment and complying with organizational standards and legal requirements.

  • Contributes to a culture of continuous improvement by identifying , testing, and sharing AI-enabled enhancements within one’s scope of work.

Candidate Profile

Minimum Qualifications / Experience

  • PhD ≥ 8 yrs , or Master’s ≥ 11 yrs, or Bachelor’s ≥ 1 3 yrs of relevant experience in
    Electrical Engineering, Materials Science, Chemical Engineering, Physics, or related field,

  • Advanced dry etch PD experience

Must-have technical skills

  • Deep plasma etch fundamentals; reactor knowledge; high aspect ratio etch scalability experience.

  • DOE/SPC/statistics and model-based problem solving.

Must-have soft skills

  • Cross-functional leadership, mentorship mindset, and strong stakeholder communication.

Highly desirable / preferred

  • Data science basics / Python coding / AI-assisted analysis.
