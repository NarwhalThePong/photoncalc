Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

1. Key Responsibilities:

  • Process Optimization: Establish, monitor, and optimize daily assembly process parameters to ensure stable production lines.
  • Troubleshooting & Failure Analysis: Lead the containment and root-cause analysis of manufacturing defects and process abnormalities.
  • Yield & Quality Improvement: Implement Statistical Process Control (SPC) to reduce process variation, drive yield enhancement, and prevent quality excursions.
  • Documentation: Author and update standard operating procedures (SOPs), failure mode and effects analysis (FMEA), and technical reports.
  • Cross-functional Support: Collaborate with equipment, product, and quality engineering teams to resolve technical bottlenecks.

2. Role Requirement

  • Work Experience: 0 to 2 years of experience in semiconductor manufacturing, packaging, or assembly processes (Fresh graduates with a strong research background in related fields are highly welcome).
  • Work Environment: Ability to work in a cleanroom environment and flexibility to support on-call rotations or shifts when critical production issues arise.
  • Attributes: Strong analytical thinking, proactive problem-solving mindset, and a high degree of self-motivation.

3. Education

  • Degree: Master’s degree (M.S.) is required.
  • Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Physics, or a related STEM discipline.

4. Skills and Experience

  • Core Technical Knowledge: Fundamental understanding of semiconductor packaging processes (e.g., Wafer Mount, Wafer Singulation, Laser Groove,).
  • Data Analysis Tools: Proficiency in statistical analysis software (e.g., JMP, Minitab) and familiarity with SPC, DOE (Design of Experiments), and FMEA methodologies.
  • Failure Analysis: Basic knowledge of failure analysis tools and techniques (e.g., OM, SEM, EDX, SAT).
  • Language & Communication: Solid written and verbal communication skills in English for technical documentation and collaboration.
