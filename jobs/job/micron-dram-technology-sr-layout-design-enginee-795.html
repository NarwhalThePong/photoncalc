As a DRAM Engineering Group (DEG) Layout Designer at Micron Technology, Inc. you will be responsible for translating schematics into layout used for the creation of fabrication reticules. You will be required to meet all engineering and process related criteria needed for an assigned Dram product. You will organize and prioritize logistics and resource allocations to meet scheduled deadlines and proactively develop methodologies for issue resolution. In this role you will work with Design and other engineering groups to apply multiple layout techniques for the design and verification of digital and analog circuits. You will be expected to understand various circuit design protocols, different fab processes, mask generation techniques, and tape out processes and procedures.

 

Our team vision is a continuing desire to develop your skills working in a Team across worldwide geographies! Enabling the creative career path you deserve with a collaborative environment and groundbreaking technology and growing upon your imagination and creativity.


Responsibilities:


  • Develop and optimize IP layouts for DRAM products while ensuring compliance with design, process, and reliability requirements.
  • Perform physical verification activities including DRC, LVS, EM, quality reviews, and documentation to support successful tape-out execution.
  • Collaborate with design, CAD, and global engineering teams to solve layout challenges and drive project success.
  • Guide and support team members through layout reviews, knowledge sharing, and technical problem-solving.
  • Plan, communicate, and present layout strategies, progress, and results to stakeholders across worldwide teams.

Minimum Qualifications:


  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electronics Engineering, VLSI Engineering, Mechatronics, or a related field.
  • Experience developing custom layout designs in advanced CMOS technologies with proficiency in physical verification methodologies.
  • Strong understanding of layout fundamentals including electromigration, IR drop, latch-up, parasitic analysis, matching, shielding, coupling, and crosstalk effects.
  • Proficiency with Cadence Virtuoso design environments and verification tools such as Mentor Calibre.
  • Demonstrated problem-solving skills related to area optimization, power, performance, and layout quality.
  • Ability to collaborate effectively with circuit designers, CAD teams, and cross-functional engineering partners.

Preferred Qualifications:


  • Knowledge of layout automation, scripting, or SKILL programming to improve design efficiency.
  • Understanding of the impact of physical layout on circuit speed, power consumption, capacitance, and area.
  • Experience supporting global engineering teams and working in a highly collaborative environment.
  • Ability to develop innovative approaches that improve productivity and turnaround time in layout design execution.
  • Experience leveraging AI-powered tools to enhance workflow efficiency and engineering effectiveness.
