Our Test Engineers are part of a team who ensure we meet the required quality standard across the full range of our SPTS single-wafer products and help the company meet our customers’ requirements and expectations!


Purpose of the role

As a Test Engineer you’ll be part of a team that handle, integrate and test modules to set test procedures, ensuring that our high standards are adhered to and any defects or quality issues are identified and rectified in real time.

 

Your day to day responsibilities will include:

  • Installation of modular semi-conductor equipment or fully integrated systems.
  • Testing of live equipment to set specifications (3 phase, single phase, pneumatic and fluid power).
  • Electrical and mechanical fault finding down to component level.
  • Cosmetic/functional quality control.
  • Recommending improvements to procedures and methods where deemed fit.
  • Actively working as part of a team to overcome complex issues.
  • Driving throughput of product to achieve deadlines and meet our customer requirements.
  • Recording test data on Shop Floor Online software suite.
  • Occasionally supporting the Product Groups in the development of new products
