Job Description:

  • Develop and execute contamination control strategies across fab , equipment, and FOUP/FOSB environments to safeguard product quality, yield, and manufacturing stability.

  • Lead contamination monitoring, environmental surveillance, and real-time control systems for AMC, particles, and advanced FOUP environments.

  • Investigate contamination-related issues, perform risk assessments, and drive corrective and preventive actions using data-driven methodologies.

  • Establish contamination control standards, procedures, and best practices while ensuring compliance with cleanroom and operational requirements.

  • Drive automation, analytics, AI-enabled solutions, and process improvements to enhance contamination detection, operational efficiency, and manufacturing performance.

  • Support equipment, material, and technology qualifications, as well as advanced node manufacturing readiness and process capability improvements.

  • Collaborate across engineering, operations, facilities, AMHS, and global organizations as contamination control subject matter expert .

Job Requirement:

  • Bachelor’s / master’s degree in all sciences ( e.g. chemistry) and chemical / environmental / material science engineering .

  • Must be able to work in cleanroom environment

  • Good interpersonal skills with the ability to collaborate effectively with a diverse variety of people.

  • Basic proficiency in software skills, such as MS Office Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.

  • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.

  • Basic knowledge of analytical techniques , such as Ion chromatography, gas chromatography and particle analyzers, etc. is a plus

  • To be open to work swing shift (Sun- Thur or Tue-Sat) based on business needs.

  • Interest in and knowledge of the semiconductor industry and Micron is preferred

  • Ability to apply baseline digital fluency and role ‑ appropriate AI literacy to use AI ‑ enabled tools responsibly and effectively for research, analysis, content creation, problem ‑ solving , operational tasks, and achieving business outcomes.
