Responsibilities will include, but are not limited to:

Contribute to the Development of New Memory Products by owning the overall logic design of the on-chip memory controller, including all optimizations and pathfinding.

  • Responsible for meeting the performance/area/power targets
  • Ability to interpret device and system specifications to produce required functionality
  • Participate in new circuit conception and design as needed
  • Define and build design architecture spec documents for the new features
  • Architect, design, and integrate complex RTL blocks for sophisticated memory products, driving functionality from specification through verification and design closure.
  • Develop and optimize synthesis and timing constraints, perform STA, and resolve timing challenges to meet performance, power, and area objectives.
  • Back-end implementation activities including APR, timing closure, physical signoff readiness, and functional/timing ECO execution.

Maintain Technical Expertise and provide training

  • Define best known design and verification practices and communicate to the entire department
  • Prepare detailed documentation as required
  • Participate in recruiting, and interviewing new candidates
  • Build new hire ramp up plans
  • Engage in continuing education and competitor analysis

Manage Verification Processes

  • Perform RTL and synthesized netlist (gate level) verification at block, subsystem, and fullchip level
  • Facilitate netlist bring up to achieve basic functionality
  • Responsible for delivering design simulation including post layout
  • Create verification plan for new and existing features and check expected behavior in Verilog
  • Collaborate with Product Engineering wrt validation plan and review Silicon result
  • Assist in silicon design validation, reticle experiments, qual and tape-out revisions as needed

Contribute to Cross Group Communication to Work Towards Standardization and Group Success

  • Work with Device, System, Probe, Test, CAD, Field Application Engineers, and Product Engineering groups to ensure accurate manufacturability of product
  • Proactively solicit mentorship from various groups (i.e. Standard cell development, simulation, voltage)

Methodology & Automation/AI

  • Improve the processes efficiency and quality leveraging AI flows
  • Leverage Artificial Intelligence for code generation and verification flows.
