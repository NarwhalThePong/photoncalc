System Architect (Protocol Bridges)

Candidate Responsibilities:

  • Identify new markets and emerging application trends to define innovative protocol bridges, interface aggregator and connectivity hub solution roadmap

  • Translate customer requirements to product specifications with compelling value proposition and competitive advantages. Actively engage with customers to gain deep understanding of their system and product requirements

  • Conduct competitive technical analysis and benchmarking

  • Collaborate with Cross functional team - Design & Test Engineers, Applications Engineers, Marketing to develop and launch new products on time

  • Prepare technical documentation – product specifications, datasheets, white papers, presentations, application notes, and develop product demos that highlight the value proposition and advantages of products

  • Oversee the development of application reference circuits and demonstration boards

  • Provide technical guidance and support for complex customer issues to secure design-ins

  • Provide application support and technical training for key customers and field application engineers

  • Collaborate with Product Marketing and Customer account teams - provide technical expertise, assist with customer engagements, and support marketing campaigns

Candidate Profile:

  • 15+ years of work experience in new product definition and architecture specification

  • Solid knowledge of multiple interface specifications - USB2/eUSB2, I3C, I2C, UART, SPMI, SDIO, I2S, Real time Ethernet 100M/1G

  • Prior specification and development experience with protocol bridging, aggregator and interface expander solutions (MCU based or Hardware FSM based)

  • Working knowledge of Timestamping, Security/Encryption is beneficial

  • Experience with IEEE1722 and IEEE1588 based implementations in the past

  • Good understanding of mobile, data centers, robotics and embedded/IOT system architectures

  • Strategic thinking to collaborate with partners and OEMs to arrive at innovative proposals and new architectures

  • Active participation and contribution to standardization efforts

  • Prior experience of developing System-C function models is desired. Timing accurate modeling experience is a plus

  • Excellent verbal and written communication and teamwork skills, ability to work with cross-functional teams and can-do attitude

Education Qualification

  • • BSEE in Electronics/ Electrical Engineering, MSEE is desirable
