Key Responsibilities

  • Lead full-chip floor planning, integration, physical implementation, signoff, and tape-out execution.
  • Drive layout implementation of complex analog, mixed-signal, memory, and high-speed interface designs.
  • Own DRC, LVS, ERC, EMIR, reliability, and manufacturability closure.
  • Define and scale layout methodologies, best practices, and execution frameworks.
  • Leverage Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Agentic AI to improve layout productivity, design quality, automation, and faster decision-making.
  • Drive automation initiatives using AI-assisted design, verification, debugging, and knowledge management solutions.
  • Lead cross-functional and global teams, mentor engineers, and drive technical excellence.

Qualifications

  • B. Tech /M. Tech in Electrical Engineering, Electronics, VLSI, or related field.
  • 15+ years of hands-on custom layout experience.
  • Proven success leading full-chip programs from concept through silicon tape-out.
  • Deep expertise in:
    • Analog & Mixed-Signal Layout
    • Memory & High-Speed Interface Layout
    • Full-Chip Floor planning & Integration
    • Signal Integrity, Power Integrity, EM/IR
    • Physical Verification & Signoff
    • Advanced Node Technologies (FinFET/GAA)
  • Strong proficiency with Cadence Virtuoso, Calibre, StarRC, Totem, and related EDA tools.
  • Proven experience leveraging AI/Generative AI/Agentic AI to enhance engineering productivity and design automation.
  • Excellent technical leadership, communication, and problem-solving skills.
  • Experience with LPDDR, HBM, NAND, SERDES, PCIe, UCIe, or advanced SoC products.
  • Strong Python, SKILL, Tcl, and automation expertise.
  • Experience building and deploying AI-powered engineering workflows and intelligent design agents.
