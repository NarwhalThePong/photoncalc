About GlobalFoundries

GlobalFoundries (GF) is a leading full-service semiconductor foundry providing a unique combination of design, development, and fabrication services to some of the world’s most inspired technology companies. With a global manufacturing footprint spanning three continents, GF makes possible the technologies and systems that transform industries and give customers the power to shape their markets. For more information, visit www.gf.com .

Introduction

Lead or co-lead the development of high-performance compute processor IP products for the GF’s IP Engineering team in Hsinchu. Provide technical leadership across product development, customer engagements, and strategic initiatives to ensure high-quality delivery and effective collaboration across global, cross-functional teams.

Your Role

  • Drive execution of large-scale IP development projects with end-to-end ownership or co-ownership.

  • Define technical strategies, review architectures and specifications, and ensure delivery excellence.

  • Lead and mentor engineering teams, fostering talent development, succession planning, and organizational effectiveness.

  • Strengthen engineering capabilities through process improvements, methodologies, and technical leadership.

  • Provide technical guidance in areas such as architecture, microarchitecture, RTL design, verification, engineering flows, signoff, and product delivery, as needed.

  • Collaborate with global teams on technology roadmaps, project planning, customer engagements, methodology development, quality improvements, and product execution.

  • Support organizational and business objectives through cross-site coordination, operational excellence, and continuous improvement initiatives.

Job Requirements

  • Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related discipline.

  • 15+ years of experience in digital front-end design, RTL implementation, verification, validation, or computational IP development, including significant technical leadership responsibilities.

  • Proven track record leading large-scale IP/SoC projects with accountability for quality, delivery, and customer satisfaction.

  • Experience working across global and multi-site engineering teams; leadership of distributed teams is highly desirable.

  • Strong expertise in microprocessor, memory, and system architecture.

  • Hands-on experience in RTL design, micro-architecture, verification, formal methods, functional coverage, and technical specification development.

  • Proficiency in SystemVerilog, Verilog, UVM, C/C++, Python, Perl, and scripting languages.

  • Familiarity with industry-standard EDA tools, including simulators, synthesis, lint, formal verification, coverage, and automation tools.

  • Strong program execution, resource planning, risk management, and delivery management skills.

  • Exceptional analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making capabilities.

  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills with the ability to influence stakeholders and drive consensus across organizations.

  • Proven ability to mentor, coach, and develop high-performing engineering teams in fast-paced environments.

  • Experience driving engineering methodologies, quality improvements, and organizational effectiveness is preferred.
