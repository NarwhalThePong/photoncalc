Staff Engineer - ASIC DV

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

Be part of ASIC Design Verification team responsible for verification of IP/Block or Subsystem.

Roles & Responsibilities:

• To be part of a highly skilled ASIC Team working on latest technologies

• Responsible for overall IP/Block and Subsystem verification from Test plan creation, System Verilog/UVM testbench development to Sign-off

• Ability to lead and mentor a team of verification engineers

• Ensure first pass product through verification coverage and sign-off criteria

• Pair with similar domain specialists across other geographical locations on core technical initiatives

Minimum Qualifications:

• M.S./M.Tech, BS/BE (Electronics) with 10+ Years of experience

• Should have expertise in IP/Block/Subsystem level verification

• Experience with AMBA protocols like AXI/AHB

• PCIe/NVMe/DDR protocol knowledge and work experience on DDR/PCIe/NVMe controllers or similar IPs 

• Proven track record of building Verification Plan, UVM Environment and Test Benches

• Experience with RTL debugging, Scoreboard, Assertions, Functional coverage coding and Code Coverage analysis

• Sound knowledge of Verilog and System Verilog languages

• This position requires good written and verbal communication skills as well a strong commitment and ability to work in cross functional and global teams

Preferred Qualifications:

• NVMe protocol knowledge and experience with working on NVMe controllers

• Good exposure and familiarity with security related aspects (boot, ROM, FW protection, keys generation, etc.) 

• Experience with using AI tools (Claude etc)

• Prior experience with managing a team of at least 5 DV engineers

• Experience with Technically leading an IP/Block to completion

Domain:

• IP/Block/Subsystem Verification (RTL Front end verification)
