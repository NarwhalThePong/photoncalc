NCG Digital Design Engineer

Micron · Jalisco, Mexico · posted 2026-10-26

Apply on Micron's site →

Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

As an NCG Digital Design Engineer, you will help craft and develop advanced digital and mixed-signal circuits that enable next-generation memory products. You will contribute to innovative silicon-to-systems solutions, using AI-assisted tools to improve engineering efficiency, accelerate learning, and enhance product quality!

You will collaborate with global design, verification, product development, and cross-functional teams throughout the product lifecycle. This role offers an opportunity to build expertise in memory technologies and digital IC design while making an impact on product performance, reliability, manufacturing efficiency, and time-to-market.


Responsibilities:


  • Develop and verify advanced Memory and Logic IP for next-generation memory products using simulation, modeling, verification methodologies, and industry-standard EDA tools.
  • Build scripts, utilities, and automated workflows using AI-powered tools and programming techniques to improve engineering efficiency, data analysis, and design quality.
  • Collaborate with Design Methodology, CAD, Modeling, Verification, Product Engineering, Test, Probe, Process Integration, Assembly, and other global teams to solve technical challenges and drive successful project execution.
  • Participate in technical reviews, design bring-up, debugging, and problem-solving activities while applying continuous learning to improve design quality.
  • Innovate and contribute to standardized design and verification approaches that support product performance, reliability, cost competitiveness, and manufacturing efficiency.

Minimum Qualifications:


  • BS or MS in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field.
  • Proficiency in digital logic fundamentals, computer architecture, and semiconductor principles developed through coursework, academic projects, internships, or hands-on experience.
  • Experience with a hardware description language such as SystemVerilog, Verilog, or VHDL and exposure to digital IC design workflows such as RTL development, simulation, verification, synthesis, or timing analysis.
  • Experience with scripting or programming using Python, Perl, Tcl, or a similar language, with strong analytical, debugging, and problem-solving skills.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively, learn new technologies, and contribute within a global engineering environment.

Preferred Qualifications:


  • Knowledge of memory architectures, memory systems, or semiconductor device fundamentals.
  • Familiarity with Synopsys, Cadence, Siemens EDA, or similar semiconductor design tools gained through coursework, internships, academic projects, or personal projects.
  • Understanding of verification concepts such as constrained-random testing, assertions, coverage, or Universal Verification Methodology (UVM).
  • Experience developing automation scripts, data-analysis tools, or other engineering productivity solutions, including AI-assisted approaches.
  • Demonstrated initiative or leadership through academic projects, internships, student organizations, technical competitions, or other collaborative experiences.
