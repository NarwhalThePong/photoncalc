Job Description

Role Overview

We are seeking an experienced and strategic Metrology Equipment Engineering Manager to lead a team of  Metrology Equipment Engineers supporting wafer manufacturing operations in San Jose and Fremont. In this role, you will be responsible for the technical direction, operational performance, and continuous improvement of the wafer metrology equipment fleet. You will serve as the primary escalation point for equipment issues across the metrology engineering team, drive prioritization decisions that balance line availability, quality risk, and resource capacity, and develop your team's technical and professional capabilities. You will partner closely with manufacturing, process integration, quality, and equipment supplier teams to ensure measurement systems meet the precision and throughput demands of leading-edge HDD wafer production.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities

Team Leadership & People Management

  • Lead, mentor, and develop a team of Metrology Equipment Engineers, fostering a culture of technical excellence, accountability, and continuous learning
  • Set clear performance expectations, conduct regular 1:1s, and deliver timely, constructive feedback through formal performance reviews and ongoing coaching
  • Support career development planning for team members across technical and leadership growth paths
  • Drive team engagement, retention, and skill development through targeted training, stretch assignments, and cross-functional exposure
  • Onboard new team members and ensure effective knowledge transfer across the team
  • Model adoption of AI-assisted analysis tools and automation workflows to elevate team efficiency and analytical capability

Escalation Management

  • Serve as the primary technical escalation point for all wafer metrology equipment issues, including critical measurement excursions, tool downtime, and data quality anomalies
  • Apply structured problem-solving methodologies (8D, RCCA, DMAIC) to drive rapid and permanent resolution of high-severity equipment issues
  • Engage with equipment suppliers, field service engineers, and internal stakeholders to resolve complex technical issues that exceed individual engineer scope
  • Maintain clear escalation pathways and communication protocols to ensure manufacturing leadership is informed of risk exposure and recovery timelines
  • Own post-incident reviews to capture learnings and prevent recurrence across the fleet

Prioritization & Resource Management

  • Manage and prioritize workload across the team to align metrology engineering resources with production urgency, quality risk, and strategic program milestones
  • Balance competing demands across preventive maintenance schedules, qualification activities, excursion investigations, and improvement projects
  • Develop and maintain a team capacity model to support headcount planning, overtime decisions, and contractor or vendor resource allocation
  • Partner with manufacturing and process engineering leaders to align metrology equipment roadmap and readiness with production ramps and new technology introductions

Metrology Equipment Operations & Technical Oversight

  • Oversee the operation, calibration, qualification, and maintenance of the full wafer metrology equipment portfolio, including optical, SEM, and physical measurement systems
  • Ensure all measurement systems meet accuracy, repeatability, and reproducibility requirements through rigorous MSA, GRR, and capability assessments
  • Review and approve measurement procedures, protocols, equipment qualifications, and technical documentation developed by the team
  • Establish and maintain equipment health monitoring systems, KPI dashboards, and fleet performance metrics (uptime, MTTR, calibration compliance, measurement uncertainty)
  • Ensure compliance with industry standards, internal quality systems, and audit requirements for equipment traceability and measurement integrity

Continuous Improvement & Strategic Initiatives

  • Identify and lead equipment and process improvement initiatives that enhance measurement accuracy, throughput, cost-effectiveness, and data quality
  • Drive evaluation and qualification of next-generation metrology technologies to support roadmap requirements and replace aging equipment
  • Champion the integration of SPC, advanced data analytics, and AI/ML tools into metrology workflows to enable predictive maintenance and faster excursion detection
  • Contribute to pFMEA processes to proactively identify and mitigate measurement system risks
  • Represent the metrology engineering team in cross-functional forums, technology reviews, and supplier business reviews

Communication & Stakeholder Engagement

  • Communicate metrology equipment status, risks, and improvement initiatives clearly to manufacturing, quality, process integration, and senior leadership stakeholders
  • Prepare and present regular team performance updates, project status reports, and escalation summaries to the Director of Wafer Engineering
  • Build and maintain strong working relationships with equipment suppliers, calibration vendors, and external metrology partners
  • Collaborate with peer engineering managers to align priorities, share best practices, and resolve cross-functional dependencies

Qualifications

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Physics, Materials Science, or a related technical discipline; Master's degree preferred
  • 7+ years of experience in metrology, semiconductor equipment engineering, or a related manufacturing engineering discipline
  • 2+ years of demonstrated people management or formal technical leadership experience
  • Hands-on expertise with wafer metrology equipment such as optical profilometers, ellipsometers, SEM/CD-SEM, overlay measurement tools, or comparable precision measurement systems
  • Strong foundation in measurement systems analysis (MSA), GRR, SPC, and statistical data analysis
  • Demonstrated ability to manage escalations, drive root cause analysis, and communicate effectively under time pressure
  • Experience with equipment qualification methodologies, calibration standards, and metrology documentation practices
  • Proficiency with data analysis tools such as Python, JMP, or equivalent

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in HDD, MEMS, or advanced semiconductor wafer manufacturing environments
  • Familiarity with AI/ML-assisted data analysis, predictive maintenance platforms, or advanced process control systems
  • Working knowledge of pFMEA, DMAIC, or equivalent structured quality improvement frameworks
  • Experience managing supplier relationships and leading supplier performance reviews
  • Demonstrated track record of building and developing high-performing engineering teams
