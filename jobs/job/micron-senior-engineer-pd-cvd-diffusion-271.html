Job Description

Micron's Singapore Process Development (PD) group in Technology Development (TD) is seeking a Senior Engineer in Plasma-CVD/Diffusion to support the development of advanced technology nodes. This outstanding opportunity allows you to build and optimize Plasma-CVD or Diffusion processes for Micron's innovative NAND Flash products.

As a PD Engineer at Micron, you will transform ideas from conception through flawless process development and successful implementation in our manufacturing facilities. This role is pivotal to Micron's products and may require business-related travel. You will excel in areas such as Plasma-CVD or Thermal Diffusion process and film characterization, optimization, building, troubleshooting, process transfer, and fundamental research.

You will work closely with Process Integration, Metrology, Surface characterization labs, Electrical Failure Analysis, Yield Improvement, Manufacturing, and Equipment vendors/suppliers to ensure our thin film processes meet the strict physical and electrical requirements for Micron Products.

This position requires demonstrated skills in a research and development (R&D) environment, outstanding organizational abilities, and the capacity to work both independently and in collaboration with others. You may frequently provide weekend support to the R&D Pilot Line to manage pilot/manufacturing line issues and coordinate program-critical lots through various modules.

Your responsibilities will include, but are not limited to:

  • Process Development

    • Design and develop and deliver process package to meet product specification requirements as per program targets and timelines

    • Characterize the processes using appropriate techniques (SEM, TEM, XPS, SIMS, AFM, etc.)

    • Design and implement experiments, analyze data and present it in the appropriate forums.

  • R&D Pilot Line in the Fab

    • Improve process maturity, capability and sustain processes in the fab under your ownership

    • Updating the Fab engineers on customer issues and technology needs.

    • Supporting root cause defect and unique fail mode analysis.

  • Process Integration

    • Delivering advanced technology to meet future product nodes.

    • Developing recipes to meet structural & electrical requirements.

    • Managing wafers though Thin Film levels in support of special requirements.

  • Other Process Areas

    • Working in multi-functional teams to jointly develop Array and CMOS structures.

    • Supporting multi-functional failure analysis and yield improvement.

  • Advanced Thin Film peers

    • Maintaining or developing expertise in multiple process, hardware, or technology areas.

    • Serving as a resource for peers in recipe & process development.

Successful candidates must have:

  • PhD in Physics, Materials Science, Chemical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Chemistry, or a closely related field of study or,
  • Bachelor/Masters in Physics, Material Science, Chemical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Chemistry, or related field of study, accompanied by more than 5 years of relevant experience in CVD/Diffusion.
  • Understanding in one or more of the following topics: Transport phenomena, Electromagnetics, Atomic/Molecular physics, and Surface phenomena.
  • Solid troubleshooting, experimental build, and data analysis skills.
  • Strong computer skills, including MS Office.
  • Plasma physics background and understanding of plasma sources used in plasma CVD or equivalent experience.
  • Understanding of CVD and Diffusion deposition processing principles.
  • Rudimentary understanding of process control and Statistical Process Control (SPC).
  • Understanding of solid state devices.
  • Knowledge of advanced data analysis techniques and scripting.
  • Willingness to work off shift and weekends if necessary.
