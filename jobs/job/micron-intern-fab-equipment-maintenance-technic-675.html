Job Description:

 

You must be enrolled in an AAS/Certificate Program in Mechatronics, Robotics, Engineering Technology, or related field of study program! Micron Technology Virginia is looking for enthusiastic interns to join our state-of-the-art research and development fabrication facility. As one of the most advanced fabs globally, we specialize in various process areas including Wet Etch, Dry Etch, Diffusion, Photolithography, Metrology, RDA, PCVD, CMP, Implantation, and Probe.


As a Fab Equipment Maintenance Technician Intern, you will gain a basic knowledge of the semiconductor fabrication process. Interns will be exposed to new technology and the equipment we use in the clean room environment to manufacture the world’s leading memory solutions! Interns receive on-the-job training that includes safety and cleanroom protocol, preventative and corrective maintenance, basic troubleshooting, and our ongoing improvement programs. With knowledge gained, you will have the opportunity to collaborate with cross-functional sub-teams to evaluate methods to optimize the efficiency of equipment maintenance and repairs.

 

Responsibilities and Duties

  • Complete required safety training and conduct all work in accordance with safety policies.
  • On the job training for preventative and corrective maintenance on semiconductor equipment.
  • Receive training and participate in area continuous improvement programs.
  • Partner with operations technicians and shift engineers/technicians to complete daily priorities that improve learning cycles.
  • Provide troubleshooting support for equipment-related problems and address root causes.
  • Improve equipment availability by investigating chronic tool problems and finding solutions.
  • Maintain documentation on tool status, including observations and work performed.
  • Perform and facilitate audits and submit good catches on safety and quality issues.

 

Education & Qualifications

  • Must be enrolled in an AAS/Certificate in Mechatronics, Robotics, Engineering Technology, or related field of study.
  • Encouraged completion of degree between December 2027 and June 2028
  • Relocating to Manassas, VA to complete a 3-month internship
  • Must be willing to relocate to Manassas, VA if offered a full-time role after internship.

 

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Students enrolled in Mechatronics, Engineering Technology, or Instrumentation, Automation programs.
  • Mechanical proficiency with the ability to understand and troubleshoot mechanical systems.
  • Knowledge of AC/DC circuits and basic fundamentals of electricity.
  • Intermediate PC skills
  • Using Artificial Intelligence
