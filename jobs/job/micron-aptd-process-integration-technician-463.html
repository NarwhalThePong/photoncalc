Your responsibilities will include, but are not limited to, the following:

  • Primarily focus on wafer matching including lot pairing and execution.
  • Configure the bonding UI and continuously track development lots, including interpreting wafer matching outputs, executing wafer shuffling within the bonding UI, and monitoring subsequent wafer build and pairing.
  • Apply AI-enabled ways of working to improve efficiency in wafer matching operations, lot tracking, data summarization, exception monitoring, and routine reporting where applicable.
  • Assist Process Integration (PI) Engineers to coordinate and trouble shoot daily line issues such as disposition of line deviations and engineering job submissions for analysis.
  • Assist in inline inspection and coordinate non-routine metro/RDA inspection on R&D experiments.
  • Support process flow setup activities for production and the short loop flow.
  • Extract, analyze, and summarize data from SWR and Conversion experiments in a report format and make recommendations to engineering based on the data.
  • Manage wafer shipment logistics between fabs (swap lots and fab-to-fab offloads) for technology transfer and yield learning.
  • Monitor key inline/probe/final parametric indices for any deviation and to trigger first level of investigations promptly.
  • Competent in the knowledge and expert usage of Micron’s systems to perform routine operational tasks effectively (Micron Central/DataNav/SPACE etc.).
  • Own or contribute towards activities and projects that improve STC PI operational procedures, systems/tools, process capability, yield, and quality.
  • Assist PI team supervisor(s) to manage Team Level Key Performance Indexes (KPI) such as Global Conversion Process (GCP), SWR, WIP status.

Successful candidates for this position will have:

  • Diploma or equivalent background
  • Excellent multi-tasking and problem-solving skills.
  • Solid understanding of SPC, SWR, QDR, Micron Central and Klarity.
  • Experience with FE MES and LMS is preferred, as the role involves handling SWM outputs and operating within manufacturing execution systems.
  • Strong teamwork skills and the ability to build strong peer relationships.
  • Proven ability to troubleshoot problems and address root causes.
  • Proven ability to communicate effectively both written and verbal.
  • Strong customer service skills and is dedicated to meeting the needs of customers.
  • Basic knowledge of fabrication process areas (i.e. Photo, Dry Etch, Diffusion, CVD, Wet Etch, Plating, Implant, PVD, and CMP).
  • Proven ability to work effectively and independent with minimal direct supervision.
  • Proven ability to make sound judgments, including problem escalation to PI engineering when appropriate.
  • Basic computer language scripting/AI skills are advantaged.
