Join Micron as a Test Development Engineer and be a part of our mission to transform how the world uses information! This role offers an outstanding opportunity to work on innovative technology and collaborate with world-class teams.


Responsibilities


  • Develop reliability stress and testing approaches for NAND products.
  • Build and maintain NAND product reliability and characterization test programs.
  • Collaborate with product reliability engineers to define reliability test flows.
  • Support the operations team to ensure flawless execution of test flows on live products.
  • Provide test program support for failure validation and characterization.
  • Contribute to burn-in board build and software development.

Requirements


  • Master's or Bachelor's degree in Engineering, preferably in Microelectronics, Electrical, or Computer Engineering.
  • Proven understanding of Automated Test Equipment for reliability testing.
  • Solid understanding of NAND flash memory reliability failure mechanisms and testing methodologies.
  • Hands-on experience with C++/Python and Object-Oriented Programming.
  • Positive attitude and self-motivation for learning and development.
  • Excellent analytical approach and structured personality.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Experience with using AI tools to improve test program development is advantageous.
