Job Summary:

The Senior Fiber Assembly Process Engineer will be responsible for the development of advanced assembly instrumentation for high-performance and high throughput fiber splicing, and study and address fiber and splice reliability at the scale of datacenters. This role combines fiber engineering, opto-mechanical automation, optical assembly manufacturing and semiconductor process development.

Responsibilities:

  • Study and root-cause sources of fiber splice loss and loss variability.
  • Develop and deploy advanced fiber processing equipment working with industrial partners.
  • Develop advanced measurement methodologies for validating fiber and fiber splice losses at datacenter scale.
  • Use optical simulation tools to model behavior & performance fiber-fiber interfaces for a wide range of advanced fiber designs.
  • Assess modal properties, loss mechanisms, dispersion, coupling, and other key fiber characteristics relevant to demanding optical applications, through optical modeling and experimental validation, model closure.
  • Work with the optical packaging and assembly validation teams to develop and deliver test methods for the performance and reliability of fiber interfaces.

Experience/Qualifications:

  • MSc or PhD in photonics, optics, physics, engineering, or a related field, or equivalent industry experience.
  • 5+ years of experience in fiber optics, optical system development and assembly.
  • Strong understanding of optical fibers and fiber splicing & measurement equipment.
  • Familiar with key opto-mechanical packaging and assembly equipment.
  • Proficiency in Python and JMP.
  • Experience with mechanical and/or optical instrumentation automation and control.
  • Familiarity with back-end semiconductor packaging is a plus.
