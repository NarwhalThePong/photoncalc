Overview

As an Astera Labs Synthesis & STA Engineer – High-Speed SerDes you will play a crucial role in driving the planning, coordination, and execution supporting the design of Astera Labs' portfolio of high-speed analog/mixed-signal SerDes and connectivity ASICs used in the world's leading cloud service providers, server, and network OEMs. This role requires end-to-end ownership of logic synthesis and static timing analysis across the digital portions of mixed-signal SerDes PHY designs, deep technical expertise in mixed-signal integration, and close collaboration with analog, RTL, physical design, and verification teams to ensure robust timing closure at advanced nodes. 

Key Responsibilities

  • Perform RTL synthesis, driving area, power, and timing optimization for digital controller and DSP blocks within high-speed SerDes PHY designs.
  • Author, develop, and validate SDC constraints from scratch, including clock definitions, generated clocks, multi-frequency/multi-mode constraints, I/O delays, false paths, multicycle paths, and clock-domain crossing exceptions unique to SerDes architectures.
  • Set up and manage MMMC (Multi-Mode Multi-Corner) environments to enable accurate and efficient STA across process, voltage, and temperature (PVT) corners.
  • Drive timing closure from synthesis through sign-off, ensuring robust timing across digital blocks embedded within analog/mixed-signal SerDes IP and full-chip SoC integration.
  • Define and manage I/O timing budgets and interface constraints between analog and digital boundaries in hierarchical mixed-signal designs.
  • Apply advanced sign-off methodologies at TSMC 5nm/3nm and below, including OCV/AOCV/POCV and PVT effects relevant to high-speed serial link performance.
  • Leverage ETM/ILM libraries for hierarchical timing analysis and correlation of SerDes hard-macro blocks, balancing runtime and accuracy.
  • Provide actionable timing feedback at both block and full-chip levels, including root cause analysis, timing debug, and ECO guidance for SerDes digital logic.
  • Manage large-scale multi-corner/multi-mode STA runs with automation, partitioning, and efficient resource usage, developing scripts and flows to improve turnaround time.
  • Generate and validate timing ECOs, partnering with physical design, analog, and RTL teams for quick closure on high-speed data-path and clock-path issues.
  • Partner closely with analog/mixed-signal designers, SerDes architecture, implementation, and verification teams to drive timing convergence and provide sign-off level expertise for PHY-level and chip-level integration.
  • Collaborate on clock tree and power domain strategies for SerDes digital blocks, ensuring compatibility with PLL/CDR-generated clocks and multi-supply domains.

Basic Qualifications

  • Bachelor's in Electrical Engineering or Computer Science required; Master's preferred.
  • ≥8 years of experience in synthesis, timing constraints development, STA, and sign-off for high-speed mixed-signal IP in Server, Storage, Networking, or SerDes applications.
  • Strong proficiency in writing and debugging SDC constraints, with deep understanding of clock modeling, generated clocks, clock-domain crossings, and exception handling.
  • Expertise in STA methodology and timing closure at both block and full-chip level using Synopsys PrimeTime and/or Cadence Tempus.
  • Strong knowledge of synthesis, place-and-route, extraction, and equivalence checking flows (Formality/Conformal) in advanced nodes (5nm/3nm and below).
  • Proficiency with Cadence and/or Synopsys physical design/STA toolchains.
  • Strong scripting ability (Tcl, Python, Perl).
  • Ability to work independently with strong prioritization and a professional, customer-focused mindset.

Preferred Experience

  • Direct experience with high-speed SerDes, Ethernet PHY (112G/224G), or PCIe/CXL PHY digital controller and DSP timing challenges.
  • Understanding of analog/mixed-signal integration concerns, including analog-digital interface timing, PLL/DLL/CDR clock structures, and level-shifter/isolation cell constraints.
  • Knowledge of ECO methodologies, DFT tools, and test coverage analysis for mixed-signal designs.
  • Experience working with IP vendors for both RTL and hard-macro integration, including Liberty (.lib), LEF, and behavioral model characterization.
  • SystemVerilog/Verilog proficiency.
  • Familiarity with power-aware synthesis and STA (UPF/CPF flows).
  • Experience with high-speed clocking architectures and multi-rate clock domain management typical in SerDes designs.
