Micron is seeking an engineer for Design Enablement and Layout in Advanced Packaging Technology Development. This role involves coordinating cross-functional engineering teams across geographies to develop and optimize design rules, requirements, design, and layout from early development through manufacturing!


Job Description

Key Responsibilities:

  • Develop and refine Design Rules, Requirements, and Test Structures for advanced packaging.
  • Create layout and DBR test vehicle and product designs to support technology development.
  • Ensure robust Design Rule Checks (DRC) and appropriate responses to deviations.
  • Analyze large datasets to extract meaningful insights and drive data-driven decision-making.
  • Leverage of artificial intelligence to reduce development time.
  • Manage structure evaluation to support development and quantify process margins.
  • Strategically partner with teams to resolve process issues related to layout and prioritize solutions.
  • Document and communicate complex problem resolutions and drive cross-node design rule alignment.
  • Maintain effective communication across Process Integration, Product Engineering, Design, and Advanced Mask teams.

Minimum Qualifications:

  • BS/MS/PhD in Electrical Engineering, Microelectronics, Physics, or related field.
  • 2+ years of experience in semiconductor industry in areas such as Process Integration, Yield Enhancement, Product Engineering, Design, Test Structure Development, or Unit Process Development.
  • Very good understanding of semiconductor memory process flows and TSV based packaging.
  • Hands on experience and proficiency in EDA tools (Cadence Virtuoso Layout and Schematic Editor, Calibre, Hspice etc.)
  • Familiarity with Design Rule Characterization and validation on Silicon.
  • Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, R, or MATLAB
  • Capable of resolving complex technical issues and communicating clearly, with Exposure to CAD group interactions, data post-processing, and database/reticle data transfer.

Nice to have:

  • Experience coding in SKILL or SVRF
  • AI Agentic Workflow
  • AI tool usage in layout design
