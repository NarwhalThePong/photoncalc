What You'll Do

In this role, you will play a key part in advancing business priorities by delivering high-impact work across your area of expertise.


Our Assembly Technicians are part of a team who ensure we meet the required quality standard across the full range of our SPTS single-wafer products and helping the company meet our customers’ requirements and expectations!


You’ll be working in our high performing manufacturing team to assemble our sophisticated processing equipment.


This position offers an excellent opportunity for someone who is looking to establish a career in a fast-growing, award-winning company with a clearly defined career development path!


This role requires shift work; Monday to Friday 6am-2pm (week 1) and Monday to Friday 2pm-10pm (week 2). There may also be the requirement to work on a night shift basis. This is a permanent role and we have a number of these positions available currently.


Your day to day responsibilities will include:

  • Production builds of electrical and electromechanical systems.
  • Providing high quality assembly expertise and knowledge of manufacturing methods.
  • Assembling a wide variety of electro-mechanical sub-assemblies.
  • Carrying out Vacuum leak checking of finished product to the highest standard.
  • Following standard operation procedures (SOP’s) to ensure quality assurance and carry out the associated checks with meticulous record keeping
  • Working with quality and production engineering teams to ensure continuous improvement of workplace.
  • Maintaining safety and standard operating procedures, ensuring safe working practices always, and adhering to any department processes, rules, and regulations

KLA is proud to be an equal opportunity employer. For this role we are looking for someone who:

  • Has proven mechanical / manufacturing experience
  • Has a flexible work ethic
  • Can commit to timescales and targets with excellent time management
  • Has the ability to use their own initiative
  • Demonstrates strong communication – You’ll enjoy establishing and maintaining good working relationships with colleagues in your own and wider team
  • Can demonstrate good attention to detail to allow you to maintain our high level of production standards and efficiency
  • Has good analytical skills with a logical approach to problem solving
  • Is confident reading technical mechanical and electrical diagrams

What we will offer you?

Annual leave starting at 25 days (plus bank holidays), share scheme, contributory pension scheme, cash health plan, cycle to work scheme, global bonus plan, rewards scheme, life assurance and generous shift allowance.


Occasional domestic and international travel, including to the United States, may be required as part of this role.
