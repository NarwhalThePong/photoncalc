What Makes This Opportunity Unique:

Micron Technology values a collaborative and inclusive work environment where each team member can thrive. As a Engineer, you will be crucial in our mission to deliver flawless, top-tier solutions. This is an outstanding chance to work with innovative technology and be part of a team committed to achieving ambitious goals and implementing groundbreaking projects!

Responsibilities and Tasks:

  • Support the smooth operations of RAM lots and tools, including optimizing RAM tool performance and completing cost-saving projects.
  • Manage lot disposition and tool operations to ensure accurate completion of lot measurements without quality issues or errors.
  • Collaborate closely with teams like HVM RAM to align with metrology and RDA processes and work with process owners to ensure actions align with specifications and procedures.
  • Coordinate and conduct training or briefings for the operations team on procedures and best-known methods (BKM) or any changes communicated.
  • Track operational performance metrics and implement continuous improvement efforts to improve metrology and RDA processes.
  • Ensure end-to-end project execution, including efficiency and RPT reduction projects, to meet operational requirements.
  • Perform basic analysis of measurement data, including conducting GR&R and fixing RAM tools and performance to support investigations for process deviations.
  • Adhere to safety procedures and area work rules.
  • Support any additional assignments as advised by the supervisor.
  • Willing to work in shifts (day or night, including weekend)
  • Artificial Intelligence
    Using, applying and leveraging AI, identify opportunities to streamline workflows, optimize processes, and support innovation by incorporating AI-driven solutions and enabling data-driven decision making across projects and teams.

Requirements:

  • Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Nano-optoelectronics, Material Science.
  • Proficiency with metrology and RDA tools such as CDSEM, KLA, AFM, E-beam
  • 2 - 3 years of experience in managing metrology process.
  • Experienced in tools optimization.
