Job Description

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovative memory and storage solutions. We are transforming how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

This opportunity is offered through the U.S. Department of War SkillsBridge Program and is open to active-duty service members only. The SkillsBridge Intern – Semiconductor Equipment Technician will gain hands‑on experience supporting Micron’s advanced semiconductor manufacturing equipment and operations. This internship is designed to help transitioning service members build industry‑relevant technical experience and prepare for potential full‑time opportunities at Micron following military separation. Participants remain on active duty and continue to receive military pay and benefits during the SkillsBridge period.

Responsibilities

Assist with preventive and corrective maintenance of advanced semiconductor manufacturing equipment

Troubleshoot mechanical, electrical, pneumatic, and vacuum subsystems

Use technical documentation, schematics, and standard operating procedures

Support equipment installations, upgrades, and qualification activities

Follow Micron safety, quality, and cleanroom protocols

Partner with equipment technicians, engineers, and operations teams to support manufacturing goals

Using AI applications when relevant

Required Qualifications

Active‑duty U.S. service member approved for participation in the DoD SkillsBridge Program

Background in mechanical, electrical, electronics, or maintenance disciplines (civilian or military)

Ability to work in a manufacturing and cleanroom environment

Strong troubleshooting skills and attention to detail

Preferred Qualifications

Familiarity with preventive maintenance concepts, technical manuals, or fault isolation

Experience working with industrial or high‑tech equipment

Program Details

Program Type: Department of Defense SkillsBridge Internship

Duration : 90–180 days, aligned with the participant’s approved SkillsBridge window

Schedule: Full‑time, on‑site

Location: Boise, ID and Manassas, VA

Compensation & Employment Status

This internship is unpaid under the DoW SkillsBridge program

SkillsBridge interns are not employees of Micron during participation

Military pay, benefits, and entitlements remain the responsibility of the U.S. Department of War

Housing Support

To support successful participation in the SkillsBridge program:

  • An apartment or extended-stay hotel will be provided for the duration of the SkillsBridge program.
  • Participants are responsible for a daily contribution of $11 for Manassas, VA, or $8.00 for Boise, ID.
  • Housing support is limited to the approved SkillsBridge participation period.

Additional housing terms and conditions will be provided prior to the program start date.

Learning & Development

During the SkillsBridge internship, participants will gain:

  • Exposure to semiconductor manufacturing operations
  • Hands‑on equipment maintenance and troubleshooting experience
  • Understanding of industrial safety, quality, and EHS standards
  • Career readiness for technician roles in a high‑tech manufacturing environment

Additional Information

Successful completion of the SkillsBridge internship may result in consideration for full‑time employment with Micron; however, conversion is not guaranteed.
