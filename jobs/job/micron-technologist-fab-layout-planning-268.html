As a Layout Technologist, you will drive the tactical planning and execution of tool move-in through collaboration with operations, logistics and relevant vendors. You will ensure that layout drawing is matched with factory site location and seamless workflow and communication in getting tools in to the designated location within the schedules for supporting factory ramp

Main Responsibilities :

  • Conduct site assessments in collaboration with key stakeholders (e.g. Tool Owners, Facilities teams) prior to equipment move-in to ensure plans are feasible and executable on-site.

  • Perform move path assessments, including First-Of-A-Kind (FOAK) equipment, to ensure safe, feasible, and executable movement within the fab .

  • Execute datum marking and verification activities to ensure alignment with layout and installation requirements.

  • Manage furniture, safety devices, and temporary staging within the fab.

  • Coordinate with contractors on equipment move-in and move-out activities within the fab.

  • Communicate and escalate tool layout constraints or on-site assessment issues to Layout Engineers in a timely manner.

  • Integrates AI-assisted tools and insights into daily work to improve efficiency, quality, or effectiveness, exercising sound judgment and complying with organizational standards and legal requirements.

  • Contributes to a culture of continuous improvement by identifying, testing, and sharing AI-enabled enhancements within one’s scope of work.

Other Responsibilities :

  • Deploy appropriate AI-driven solutions in achieving effective and efficient outcomes for self and teams
