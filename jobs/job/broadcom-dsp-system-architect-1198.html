Job Description:

We are looking for full-time DSP System Architects at our Irvine location. Please contact me if you are interested,

Responsibilities include:
• Develop specification, architecture, and micro-architecture of digital signal processing and communications algorithms for Robotics, UAV and Automotive applications
• Bit-exact C/C++ and MATLAB/Simulink system modeling and simulation
• Develop and run system level simulation suites of the physical layer transceivers and perform vector matching verification with RTL simulations

• Define and document chip requirements, architecture, verification and lab test plan
• Lab testing and debug of ASICs


Requirements:
• Bachelors in Engineering and 8+ years of related experience or Masters degree and 6+ years of related experience or PhD and 3+ years of related experience
• Expertise in Digital Communication Theory & Digital Signal Processing algorithms
• Experience in C/C++, MATLAB/Simulink
• Experience in equalizers & timing recovery is highly desirable

• Experience architecting communications systems for high performance ASIC based products is desirable
• Good hands-on skills in the lab
• Good oral and written communication skills
