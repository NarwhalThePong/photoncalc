dBm ↔ mW ↔ W converter

Convert optical or RF power between dBm, dBW, mW, µW and W, and turn any dB gain or loss into a linear ratio. Free, instant, no ads in the math.

Results

Formula

P[dBm] = 10 · log₁₀( P / 1 mW )    P[mW] = 10^(P[dBm] / 10)
P[dBW] = P[dBm] − 30    power ratio = 10^(dB/10), field ratio = 10^(dB/20)

Worked example

A 13 dBm laser diode: P = 10^(13/10) = 19.95 mW. Add a 3 dB loss and you have 10 dBm = 10 mW, because −3 dB is a factor of 10^(−0.3) ≈ 0.501.

How it works

dBm is power on a logarithmic scale referenced to 1 milliwatt, so 0 dBm = 1 mW, 10 dBm = 10 mW and 30 dBm = 1 W. Because it is logarithmic, a fiber link budget becomes simple addition: transmitter power in dBm minus losses in dB gives the received power in dBm.

The same conversion applies to optical power meters and RF spectrum analyzers. dBm needs no impedance for power; impedance matters only if you convert to volts.

FAQ

How many mW is 0 dBm?

Exactly 1 mW.

How many watts is 30 dBm?

1 W (1000 mW).

What is the difference between dB and dBm?

dB is a ratio (gain or loss); dBm is an absolute power level referenced to 1 mW.
