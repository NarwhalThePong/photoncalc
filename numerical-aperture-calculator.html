Numerical aperture & acceptance angle calculator

Compute the numerical aperture of a step-index fiber or waveguide from its core and cladding indices, plus the acceptance half-angle, full cone and index contrast.

Results

Formula

NA = √(n_core² − n_clad²) = n₀ · sin θ_max
Δ = (n_core − n_clad) / n_core    critical angle θ_c = arcsin(n_clad / n_core)

Worked example

Core 1.48, cladding 1.46 in air: NA = √(1.48² − 1.46²) = √0.0588 = 0.2425, so the acceptance half-angle is arcsin(0.2425) = 14.03° (a 28.07° full cone).

How it works

Light entering a step-index fiber is guided only if it hits the core–cladding boundary beyond the critical angle. Tracing that back to the input face gives the maximum launch angle θ_max; n₀ sin θ_max is the numerical aperture. A larger NA collects light more easily but supports more modes.

For coupling a laser into a fiber, the focused beam's divergence half-angle should be below the acceptance angle (and, for single-mode fiber, its spot should match the mode-field diameter).

FAQ

What is the NA of standard single-mode fiber?

About 0.14 for SMF-28-type fiber (manufacturer's nominal value).

Does NA depend on wavelength?

Only weakly, through the dispersion of the two indices.

Why does n₀ matter?

Immersion in water or oil changes the acceptance angle, not the NA.
